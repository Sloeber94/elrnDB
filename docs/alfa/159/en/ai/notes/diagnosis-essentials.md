# Diagnosis essentials (Alfa 159)

The junction units and the Smart-Key note are the Alfa 159's; the rest applies to every model.

Check these before starting any fault diagnosis. Where the manual covers an item, the note in brackets
says where to look.

- Battery and battery disconnect (Smart-Key: leave the TEG inserted; see Maintenance › Introduction)
- Fuses and relays: amperage, maxi/mini types ([fuses.md](fuses.md))
- Junction units and fuse boxes: CBA, CFO, CVM, CPL, CVB ([fuses.md](fuses.md))
- Earths and earth points (component family C in Connectors)
- Connectors: pin numbering, mating halves, locking tabs (Electrical equipment › 3 Connectors)
- Wire colour codes and stripes ([data-model.md](data-model.md))
- Wire gauge (mm²) (the Sec. column of the pinouts)
- Splices and junctions ("via splice" in the pinouts)
- Ignition states: OFF, ACC, INT (ignition-switched), CPL (permanent)
- CAN and A-BUS data lines
- Control units and their locations (component location drawings)
- Diagnostic socket and fault codes (Fault diagnosis, Test)
- Multimeter: voltage, resistance, continuity
- Test lamp and fused jumper leads
- Short to earth, open circuit, short to battery
- Isolating the battery before work
- Torque specifications and fastener types (Technical Data › Tightening torques)
- Operation numbers (R.R., R+R) and the removal/refitting order ([rendering-rules.md](rendering-rules.md))
- Component location drawings
- Wiring diagram symbols and harness references
- Engine, gearbox and equipment variants
