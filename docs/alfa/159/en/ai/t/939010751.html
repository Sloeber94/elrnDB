1. Retract the protective boot on the driveshaft.

If the boot cannot be moved back, cut it using an appropriate tool and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Clean the driveshaft constant velocity joint until the safety ring is accessible.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Extend the safety ring using suitable pliers.

2. Extract the wheel side constant velocity joint from the driveshaft.

NOTE: On joints without a safety ring, remove the constant velocity joint from the driveshaft by force.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Remove the wheel side constant velocity joint safety ring.

On joints without a safety ring, the ring is positioned on the driveshaft and must therefore be removed from this position.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Remove the protective boot.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Wash the constant velocity joint components thoroughly using diesel oil or petrol and visually inspect that there are no signs of seizing or grooves on the balls or seats.

- Position the new protective boot on the driveshaft.

1. Place a new safety ring in the housing on the constant velocity joint.

Check that the safety ring is correctly fitted.

For joints without a safety ring, fit a new safety ring on the driveshaft.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

- Place the wheel side constant velocity join in its housing on the driveshaft.

Push the constant velocity joint until the safety ring is correctly positioned.

- Fill the joint and the boot with the specified grease.
