The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0190 (Fuel high pressure sensor)

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected if the voltage entering the junction unit (Pin 43) is more than 4.75 V (detail "short circuit to Vbatt") or if it is less than 0.25 V (detail "short circuit to earth or open circuit").

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
