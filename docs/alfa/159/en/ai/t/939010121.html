S033 STEERING WHEEL AND BRAKE PEDAL VIBRATIONS

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the brake pedal is depressed slightly (even with the brakes cold) at average speed, the steering wheel and the brake pedal vibrate.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK THE WHEEL STUD TIGHTENING

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the wheel studs are correctly fastened

Move on to Step 2

Incorrect tightness

Tighten the wheel studs correctly.

2

CHECK FRONT DISCS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • condition;
  • changes in thickness;
  • irregular disc wear.

Move on to Step 3

Front discs worn irregularly or damaged.

Regrind the discs or, if necessary, replace them

Op. 3310A54

Op. 3310A52

3

CHECK ON PAD SURFACES CONDITION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check:

  • pad brake surface intactness;
  • that the pads are not worn.

End of diagnosis

1. Brake surfaces vitrified or with traces of grease, oil or water

1. Clean again, check and remove the causes of the fault

2. Front pads worn (reduced pad compressibility)

2. Replace the front pads
