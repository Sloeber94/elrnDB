PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Lift the wper arms off the glass, turn on the panel and select one of the functions (e.g. intermittence) to see whether the noise persists.

Move on to Step 1

The noise is no longer present.

Replace the wiper blades and/or wiper arms.

Op. 5050B28 WINDSCREEN WIPER BLADE/S - R+R

Op. 5050B32 WINDSCREEN WIPER ARM/S - R+R

1

CHECK THE WIPER IS CORRECTLY FASTENED

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Turn off the function selected previously using the stalk command and turn off the panel. Then remove the wiper arms and panel to ensure the windscreen wiper is correctly fastened to the body.

Op. 5050B32

Move on to Step 2

Incorrect fastening of the windscreen wiper.

Restore the correct fastening of the windscreen wiper.

2

CHECK THE WINDSCREEN WIPER LINKAGE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Turn off the instrument panel and check the linkage movement is correct, in particular:

  • ensure the linkage does not interfere with the body or is hindered by foreign bodies present in the wiper housing.
  • check that the windscreen wiper unit has been correctly assembled.

End of diagnosis

1. The linkages interfere with the body.

1. Restore the linkages to correct working order.

2. Foreign bodies present in the windscreen wiper housing.

2. Remove the foreign bodies from the windscreen wiper housing.

3. Incorrect assembly of the windscreen wiper.

3. Replace the windscreen wiper unit
