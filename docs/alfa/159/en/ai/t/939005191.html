The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P2008 (flow valve), with detail "short circuit to +Vbatt"

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected if there is a short circuit to positive in the circuit connected to Pin 45 of the junction unit for at least 0.24 secs.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
