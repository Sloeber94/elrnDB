No voice message is given on the NAV system

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK AUDIO VOLUME

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the speaker is not crossed out on the icon in the NAV screen

Move on to Step 2

Voice command audio volume (set to OFF), with the speaker icon crossed out

Press the NAV key once (less than 2 seconds) and enable the audio

2

CHECK AUDIO MESSAGES

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Press the NAV button on the navigation system front panel to listen to the voice commands.

Check for audio signals

Move on to Step 3

The radio lowers the volume and the voice command cannot be heard

In the NAV screen, adjust the voice command volume

3

CONNECTIONS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the correct connection between the NAV module and speaker for playing the audio signal. See the wiring diagram

See E3510 RADIO

Move on to Step 4

1. Connections not correct

1. Restore correct power supply, see wiring diagram

See E4090 INSTRUMENT PANEL AND INDICATORS

2. Front speaker defective

2. Replace the speaker

Op. 5570T90 FRONT SPEAKER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

3. Front speaker defective

3. Replace the speaker

Op. 5570T98

4

CHECK NAVIGATION SYSTEM OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the navigation device is working properly

Fault persists

Navigation device defective

Replace Connect

Op. 5580P12 CONNECT PANEL REPLACE
