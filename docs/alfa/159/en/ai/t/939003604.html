H007 EXCESSIVE ROLLING/PITCHING - AT FRONT

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The customer complains of an excessive rolling and/or pitching sensation of the vehicle, especially at the front.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

ROAD TEST TO EVALUATE THE ROLLING - PITCHING

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Carry out a road test to evalute the folling/pitching of the vehicle

See Test 44B ROAD TEST FOR ROLLING/PITCHING EVALUATION

End of diagnosis

Subjective judgement compared with the reference vehicle 6

Acceptable behaviour of the vehicle

Subjective judgement compared with the reference vehicle 5.5

Move on to Step 2

Subjective judgement compared with the reference vehicle 3-4

Move on to Step 3

2

END OF TRAVEL BUFFER CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Visually inspect that the end of travel buffer is not faulty and is correctly inserted in its housing

Move on to Step 3

End of travel buffer deteriorated

Replace the end of travel buffer

Op. 4410A12 FRONT SUSPENSION DAMPER (ONE), LH OR RH - FOR MCPHERSON STRUT SYSTEM - INCLUDES DISMANTLING/REASSEMBLY OF ASSEMBLY AT BENCH - R.R

End of travel buffer not correctly inserted in its housing

Restore the correct fitting of the end of travel buffer

3

UPPER TRACK CONTROL ARMS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the bushes securing the upper track control arm to the bodyshell

Also check the condition of the track control arm pin boot

Move on to Step 4

Bushes secured to the bodyshell not intact

Replace the upper track control arm

Op. 4410B68 LOWER SWINGING ARM(ONE), LH OR RH OF FRONT SUSPENSION - R R -CHECK WHEEL ALIGNMENT SEPARATELY

Arm pin boot not intact

Replace the upper track control arm

Op. 4410B68 LOWER SWINGING ARM(ONE), LH OR RH OF FRONT SUSPENSION - R R -CHECK WHEEL ALIGNMENT SEPARATELY

4

LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARMS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check condition:

- the front bush securing the lower track control arm to the engine frame;

- the rear hydraulic bush securing the lower track control arm to the engine frame;

- the pin securing the lower track control arm to the steering knuckle.

Move on to Step 5

Front bush not intact

Replace the lower track control arm

Op. 4410B52 FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM REAR BUSH LEFT OR RIGHT - REPLACE AT BENCH

Rear hydraulic bush not intact

Replace the rear hydraulic bush complete with support

Op. 4410B52 FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM REAR BUSH LEFT OR RIGHT - REPLACE AT BENCH

Lower track control arm attachment pin not intact

Replace the lower track control arm

Op. 4410B52 FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM REAR BUSH LEFT OR RIGHT - REPLACE AT BENCH

5

SHOCK ABSORBER EFFICIENCY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the efficiency of the shock absorbers by carrying out manual compression manoeuvres (resting on the bonnet by the centre of the wheel), which should be carried out on both sides of the vehicle

Move on to Step 6

Sudden return without damping

Replace the shock absorber that is not working properly

Op. 4410A12 FRONT SUSPENSION DAMPER (ONE), LH OR RH - FOR MCPHERSON STRUT SYSTEM - INCLUDES DISMANTLING/REASSEMBLY OF ASSEMBLY AT BENCH - R.R

6

VEHICLE GEOMETRY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

After replacing the shock absorbers, check the geometry

End of diagnosis

Geometry values do not conform

Restore the geometry to the correct values

Op. 4450A10 FRONT WHEEL GEOMETRY - CHECK USING OPTICAL EQUIPMENT - INCLUDES ADJUSTMENT IF NECESSARY
