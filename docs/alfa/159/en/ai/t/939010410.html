Op. 4410C10 FRONT SUSPENSION ANTI-ROLL BAR - R+R - CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY

Mark the position of the flexible mounting for the power unit rear support in relation to the front suspension frame.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the flexible mounting for the power unit rear support (1b) from the front suspension frame.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the power unit rear support flexible mounting (1a) in its housing and secure it by tightening the new bolts (1b) to the recommended torque.
