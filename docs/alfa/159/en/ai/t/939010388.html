Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

In order to carry out the timing check, the special timing templates must be fitted on the camshafts and crankshaft as shown below.For this motorization, the preliminary operations cannot be performed on the vehicle, therefore refer to engine overhaul.

Camshaft timing

1. Remove plug (1a) and refit exhaust camshaft template (1b).
