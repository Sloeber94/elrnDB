N060 CONNECT AUDIO NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Audio not present from all sources (radio, CD, phone, navigation system)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect to the Connect using the diagnostic equipment and check for the presence of any errors, also check that the radio controls on the steering wheel are working properly.

Move on to Step 2

1.Speaker wiring broken/short circuited

1.Restore the correct wiring

2.Speakers defective

2.Replace front speakers

Op. 5570T90 FRONT SPEAKER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

or rear speakers

Op. 5570T98

3.Radio controls defective

3. Restore the correct operation of the controls.

2

ELECTRICAL CONNECTION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Release the Connect from the dashboard and check that the connection with the speakers is properly fitted

See E3510 RADIO

Check the audio controls on the steering wheel if present.

Move on to Step 3

1.Speaker output connection at the Connect not properly connected.

1.Connect the connection correctly.

2.Audio controls on steering wheel defective.

2.Restore the correct operation.

3

CHECK AUDIO SIGNALS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the presence of the audio signals see wiring diagram

See E3510 RADIO

Move on to Step 5 for versions without Bose system

Audio signals at the Connect output interrupted because the final audio stage is interrupted.

Replace Connect

Op. 5580P12 CONNECT PANEL REPLACE

3

CHECK AUDIO SIGNALS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the presence of the audio signals see wiring diagram

See E3510 RADIO

Move on to Step 4

Audio signals at the Connect output interrupted because the final audio stage is interrupted.

Replace Connect

Op. 5580P12 CONNECT PANEL REPLACE

4

AUDIO AMPLIFIER CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the audio amplifier is correctly supplied by following

See Test 5580TA CHECK AUDIO AMPLIFIER OPERATION

Move on to Step 5

1. Amplifier not correctly supplied

1. Restore correct power supply.

2. Amplifier defective

2. Replace audio amplifier

Op. 5580T42 BOSE SOUND SYSTEM AMPLIFIER - R.R.

3. Wiring defective between Connect and audio amplifier.

3. Restore correct wiring.

5

CONNECT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that Connect is working properly

End of diagnosis
