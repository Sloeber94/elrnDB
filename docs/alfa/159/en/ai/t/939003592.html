F002 REAR WHEELS LOCK WHILST BRAKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the brakes are operated the distribution between the front and rear wheels is not correct: in extreme conditions the rear wheels lock and the vehicle may skid

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

First of all check whether the ABS warning light and the handbrake warning light come on simultaneously during starting and then go out

Move on to Step 1

The two warning lights remain on after starting

Proceed as instructed in the

See table K062 ABS AND HANDBRAKE WARNING LIGHTS BOTH ON

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic test equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the tester input.

Check that there are no faults in the ABS control unit.

Move on to Step 2

1. Error detected in the control unit.

1. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2. No communication with the control unit.

2. Restore:

  • Connection between the ABS control unit and the diagnostic connector;
  • ABS control unit supply

See E7020 ABS

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) and carry out the active diagnosis for:

  • Pump motor;
  • Solenoid valves.

Move on to Step 3

Fault detected in one of the solenoid valves.

Replace the ABS electronic control unit electrohydraulic unit

Op. 3340A12 ABS HYDRAULIC AND ELECTRONIC CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

3

REAR BRAKE CALIPER CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the rear brake caliper pistons.

Move on to Step 4

Pistons worn or seized.

Replace the rear brake calipers

Op. 3310B34

4

CHECK ON FLEXIBLE BRAKE PIPES AND BLEEDING

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Visually inspect that there are no bends or constrictions in the brake pipes, bleed the system and check that the fluid comes out evenly from the bleed screws.

Op. 0010T65 HYDRAULIC BRAKE SYSTEM - BLEED AIR

Move on to Step 5

Bends or constrictions present, the fluid does not come out evenly.

Replace the section of pipe affected by the problem

Op. 3340B ABS SYSTEM PIPES

5

FRONT BRAKE STATE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Visually inspect that there is no grease, oil or dirt on the braking surfaces of the front pads.

End of diagnosis

Braking surfaces dirty.

Clean, check and eliminate the causes of the problem: if necessary, replace the front pads

Op. 3310A60 FRONT BRAKE PADS - REPLACE
