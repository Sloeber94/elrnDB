Removing

Refitting

1. Fully undo the bolts (1a) loosened previously and remove the mobile timing belt tensioner (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Reposition the moving timing system tensioner in its housing and secure it without tightening the nut.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

- On this car, it is not possible to remove/refit the mobile timing belt tensioner on the car.

To perform this operation, remove/refit the power unit

Op. 1004B10 POWER UNIT WITH MANUAL GEARBOX - R.R

and then work at the bench, customising the engine service procedures for this operation.
