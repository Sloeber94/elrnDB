Refitting

1. Undo clips (1a) and remove the gear lever external gaiter (1b) from the panel.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Replace the gear lever exterior gaiter in its seat on the panel and secure it using the clips.

- Place the gear lever panel in its housing and secure it using the clips.
