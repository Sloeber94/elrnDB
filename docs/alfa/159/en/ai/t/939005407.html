N070 CONTROLS ON THE STEERING WHEEL NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the buttons on the front panel of the radio are pressed the corresponding functions are selected. Whilst when the controls on the steering wheel are operated the corresponding function is not selected.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Test results

1

OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Using the controls on the steering wheel, check the correct operation, even in steering conditions

Move on to Step 2

1. One of the controls on the steering wheel faulty

2. All the controls on the steering wheel faulty

1. Move on to Step 2

2. Move on to Step 3

2

STEERING WHEEL MODULE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the following, in order:

- Correct power supply for the module on the steering wheel

- Continuity of the wiring

- Operation of controls on the steering wheel

See E3510 RADIO

Move on to Step 3

1. Wiring faulty

2. Controls on the steering wheel faulty

1. Restore the wiring to correct operation

2. Repair / replace the controls on the steering wheel

3

RADIO MODULE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the reception of the signals from the module on the steering wheel is correct at the radio connector

See E3510 RADIO

End of diagnosis

Controls from radio steering wheel conversion module faulty

Replace the radio
