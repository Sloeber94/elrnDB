The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P2264 (water in diesel filter).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected if there is water detected in the fuel or if there is a short circuit to earth in the circuit connected to Pin 74 of the junction unit.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
