Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The warning light remains on even when the driver's seat belt is fastened.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The Air Bag Node sends the message to the centre front courtesy light where the seat belt not fastened warning light is housed

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the Examiner in the Instrument Panel Node ambient check that the seat belts not fastened warning light is correctly activated in Active Diagnosis.

Move on to Step 2

1.The seat belt not fastened warning light is not flashing

1.Replace the centre front courtesy light

Op. 7040E40 MODULAR FRONT COURTESY LIGHT - R R

2.Seat belt fastened switch short circuited to earth

2.Replace seat belt mounting and switch

Op. 7065A15 ANCHORAGE ROD OF A FRONT SEAT BELT ON A SEAT WITH HEIGHT ADJUSTMENT, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R .

3.Wiring between air bag control unit and courtesy light short circuited to earth

3.Renew wiring insulation

2

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the diagnostic equipment connected, check at the Air Bag Node that the fault is not longer present

End of diagnosis
