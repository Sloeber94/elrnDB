PRELIMINARY CHECKS

Operation

Check whether the problem is accompanied by noise from the gearbox with the vehicle stationary and in neutral. Check the transmission fluid level

1

CHECK FOR LEAKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Wash the gearbox thoroughly, spread with talc and carry out a road test.

Check that there are no leaks.

In particular, check the condition of the gaskets:

- gear input shaft seal;

- rear cover gasket;

- differential oil seal.

Move on to Step 2

.

Leaks from gaskets.

Replace the gaskets

Leaks from left differential flange

Replace the flange

Op. 2110D52 FLANGE ON DIFFERENTIAL - R.R WITH DRIVESHAFT REMOVED

Leaks from right differential oil seal.

Replace the oil seal

Op. 2110D62 RIGHT DIFFERENTIAL SHAFT OIL SEAL - R.R WITH INTERMEDIATE DRIVESHAFT REMOVED

GEAR CASING CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Use talc to check visually that the gear casing is intact.

End of diagnosis

Casing damaged, with presence of cracks or deformities.

Replace the gear casing, rear cover or entire gearbox
