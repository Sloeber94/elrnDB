S062 FRONT DOORS CREAKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle moving, particularly over rough ground or during acceleration or braking, noise can be heard from the one of the front doors.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK DASHBOARD/DOOR PANEL COUPLING

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the door panel and dashboard are correctly coupled

Move on to Step 2

Faulty dashboard fastening.

Restore the correct fastening of the dashboard. Restore the felt pads if necessary

2

CHECK DOOR FASTENINGS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the fastening:

  • of the door opening limiter;
  • of the hinges (upper and lower);
  • of the lock.

Move on to Step 3

1. Faults in limiter fastening.

1. If necessary, lubricate the limiter and restore the correct fastening

Op. 7005A28 FRONT SIDE DOOR LIMITER - R.R.

2. Faults in hinge fastening.

2. If necessary, lubricate the hinge pin and restore the correct fastening

Op. 7005A24 FRONT SIDE DOOR HINGES (BOLTED) - R.R. WITH DOOR DETACHED

3. Faults in lock fastening.

3. Restore the correct fastening of the lock

Op. 7005N21 FRONT SIDE DOOR LOCK MOTOR/LOCK ASSEMBLEY FOR ONE DOOR, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

4. Faults in striker.

4. Check that the striker fastening is correct

Op. 7005B14 FRONT SIDE DOOR LOCK STRIKER - R.R

3

CHECK DOOR PANEL

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Remove the door panel and check for foreign bodies inside the door.

Op. 7005E10 FRONT SIDE DOOR INTERIOR PANEL - R.R.

Move on to Step 4

Foreign bodies present.

Remove the foreign bodies.

4

CHECK DOOR INNER FASTENINGS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the fastening:

  • of the window mechanism;
  • of the moving window guides;
  • of the moving window fastening element;
  • of the door opening/closure linkage

End of diagnosis

1. Faults in window fastening.

1. Restore the correct fastening of the window

Op. 7005M24 FRONT DOOR,ONE (LEFT OR RIGHT) ELECTRIC WINDOW MECHANISM - CHECK POSITIONING AND FASTENING WITH DOOR PANEL REMOVED

2. Faults in guide fastening

2. Restore the correct fastening of the guides

Op. 7005D22 FRONT SIDE DOOR DESCENDING WINDOW GLASS GUIDE (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. WITH DOOR PANEL (AND SUPPORT FRAME) REMOVED

3. Faults in fastening element

3. Replace the fastening element

Op. 7005D16 FRONT SIDE DOOR DESCENDING WINDOW GLASS FASTENING ELEMENT (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

4. Faults in linkage fastening.

4. Restore the linkage fastening clips, see Assembly

Op. 7005C FRONT DOOR LOCK CONTROLS
