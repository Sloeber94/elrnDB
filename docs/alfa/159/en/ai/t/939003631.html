The "Instrument Panel Node" receives information on the outside temperature from the "Driver's Door Node" which in turn receives the figure from the sensor on the moving part of the driver's rear view mirror.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

ACTION

Check that the temperature value shown on the instrument panel display is plausible.

Also check that when the NQS is switched off and switched back on again the temperature value is plausible.
