Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the side lights on, when the lever on the right of the steering wheel is operated the headlamp washer function does not work properly.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the windscreen wipers are working properly

Move on to Step 1

The windscreen wipers are not working either

Proceed as instructed in the diagnosis

See table J001 WINDSCREEN/REARSCREEN WASHERS NOT WORKING

1

PIPE AND JET CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that there are no obstructions or incorrect connections for the windscreen washer pipes

Check the condition of the jet.

Op. 5050D13 HEADLAMP WASHER SYSTEM TYPES AND FITTINGS - R R

Move on to Step 2

1. Pipes blocked or damaged

1. Repair or replace the pipes

2. Jet obstructed or damaged

2. Repair or replace the jet

Op. 5050D08 HEADLAMP WASHER JET/S - R R

2

HEADLAMP WASHER TIMER CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the operation of the headlamp washer timer

See E3022 HEADLAMP WASHER

Move on to Step 3

Headlamp washer timer not working properly.

Replace the headlamp washer timer

3

HEADLAMP WASHER ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • Headlamp washer fuse;
  • Headlamp washer timer power supply;
  • Connection between headlamp washer timer and steering column switch unit;
  • Connection between headlamp washer timer and headlamp washer pump

See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE

Move on to Step 4

1. Component defective.

1. Replace defective component (in the case of a fuse, find out what caused it to blow)

2. Connections not correct.

2. Restore the correct connections.

4

HEADLAMP WASHER PUMP CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the headlamp washer pump is working properly.

HEADLAMP WASHER PUMP CHECK

Headlamp washer pump not working properly.

Replace the headlamp washer pump

Op. 5050D20 HEADLAMP WASHER PUMP - R+R
