Removing

Removing

Refitting

1. Loosen the band and remove the resonator from the connector on the air hose.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the resonator back in its housing and fasten it to the connector on the air housing using the band.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Loosen the band (1a) securing the hose (1b) to the air hose between the flow meter and the throttle body.

2. Undo the bolts and remove the resonator assembly.

- If necessary, loosen the band and separate the resonator from the hose.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Connect the hose to the resonator and secure it using the band.

- Fit the resonator assembly in place and secure it using the bolts.

- Tighten the band securing the hose to the air hose between the flow meter and the throttle body.
