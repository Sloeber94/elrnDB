0010T21 ENGINE COOLANT - CHECK DENSITY AND CHANGE, IF NECESSARY

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

- When the engine is cold, check that the level of coolant in the reservoir reaches the MAX notch on the reservoir.

- If necessary, top up with the specified fluid to the MAX notch.

Do not remove the tank cap when the engine is warm as there is a risk of scalding.
