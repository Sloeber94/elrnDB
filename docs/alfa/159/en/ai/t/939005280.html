Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) fixing the exhaust pipe (1b) to the catalytic pre-converter for the left head.

2. Undo the nuts securing the exhaust pipe (1b) to the catalytic converter.

- Remove the exhaust pipe and gaskets.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the exhaust pipe in position with the gaskets.

- Tighten the nuts and bolts securing the exhaust pipe to the pre-catalyzer and to the catalytic converter.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
