Refitting

Removing

- Clean any residues of the old gasket from the lower cylinder head plane.

- Check the planarity of the cylinder head lower plane.

- If the maximum difference in planarity is less than 0.05 mm, then the cylinder head lower plane does not require refacing.

- If the maximum difference in planarity is 0.05 - 0.20 mm, then the cylinder head lower plane should be refaced.

If grinding is necessary, it should be carried out on both cylinder heads.

The maximum permitted regrinding depth is 0.25 mm; if the cylinder head lower plane requires more regrinding , the cylinder head must be replaced.

- Remove valve deposits, checking the rod for scoring or signs of binding.

- Check that the diameter of the valve stem corresponds to the recommended figures.

- Refit the camshaft caps on the cylinder head according to the position marked during the dismantling.

The camshaft caps are marked with a number starting from the timing side going toward the flywheel side (even numbers for the left cylinder head and uneven numbers for the right cylinder head) and a letter, "I" for the intake side camshaft and "E" for the exhaust side camshaft.The projections on the camshaft caps should be facing the centre of the cylinder head.
