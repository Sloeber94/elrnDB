Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Hardening/creaking of backrest when folding over

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

HINGE MOVEMENT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the hinges move freely

Move on to Step 2

Interference with foreign objects or cover/upholstery.

Remove the causes of the interference

2

CHECK ON MATCHING BETWEEN BACKREST AND VEHICLE INTERIOR TRIM

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the matching of the backrest with the vehicle interior trim (wheel arch covers, side trims, door panels, etc.) folding the backrest over.

Move on to Step 3

Interference with trims.

Reposition the trims and/or carry out the correct backrest/side trim tightening/fitting cycle.

3

CHECK ON CONDITION OF BACKREST SIDE HINGES

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the backrest side hinges (and the centre ones for split seats) and make sure they conform.

Move on to Step 4

No plastic bush on the rotation pin.

Remove the backrest and fit the plastic noise prevention bush; lubricate.

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present.

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace the left backrest

Op. 7045A13 FRONT SEAT WITH SIDE BAG (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Replace the right backrest

Op. 7045A15 R R HANDLE FOR FRONT SEAT FORE AND AFT ADJUSTMENT
