With the vehicle moving, there is noise coming from the bottom area of the passenger compartment

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the wind noise is actually coming from the underbody.

Move on to Step 1

See table T001 WIND NOISE FROM THE FRONT DOORS

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T020 WIND NOISE FROM THE SUN ROOF

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T025 WIND NOISE FROM THE REAR AREA

1

CHECK ON GUARD UNDER THE ENGINE

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the guard under the engine and make sure it is correctly fitted

Move on to Step 2

under engine guard

Replace the guard under the engine

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

Guard under the engine not fastened correctly

Renew the fastening of the guard

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

2

CHECK UNDER THE FLOOR PANEL

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the trim under the floor panel. In particular, make sure:

- The plugs are present;

- The plugs are correctly fitted;

- The seals are present and in good condition.

End of diagnosis

Problems with the trim under the floor panel.

Renew the plugs and seals for the trim under the floor panel
