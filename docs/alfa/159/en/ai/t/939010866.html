Refitting

The following procedure describes the removing-refitting of the right rear wheel stub axle, carry out the same operations for the left rear wheel stub axle.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the rear brake disc guard (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Unscrew bolt (1a) and remove the rear wheel rpm sensor (1b) from the steering knuckle.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

- Position a hydraulic jack underneath the rear wheel stub axle.

1. Undo the bolt fixing the rear suspension front wishbone, stub axle side.

2. Unscrew bolt (2a) and bolt (2b) securing the rear wheel hub stub axle, rear suspension longitudinal arm side.

3. Remove the rear wheel hub stub axle.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
