S01 ENGINE KNOCK

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

There is slight knocking that usually gets worse during acceleration. The engine produces a dry noise typical of metallic knocking which changes when the throttle opens.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

There is slight knocking that usually gets worse during acceleration. The engine produces a dry noise typical of metallic knocking which changes when the throttle opens.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Make sure that the knocking is not noticeable when the road surface changes. The latter can cause a change in the engine torque transmitted to the rear wheels which could also lead to engine detonation.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods
