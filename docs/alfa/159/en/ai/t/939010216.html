Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Press the START/STOP button and turn off the engine.

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from the turbine variable geometry solenoid.

See L036 TURBINE VARIABLE GEOMETRY CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE

Press the START/STOP button.

Measure the voltage between Pin 1 of the solenoid wiring side connector and Earth. Ensure it is greater than 11V.

See L036 TURBINE VARIABLE GEOMETRY CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE

Press the START/STOP button.

Move on to Step 4

Move on to Step 2

2

In the engine compartment junction unit, remove fuse F11 (third from the right in the lower row)

See B001 JUNCTION UNIT

Press the START/STOP button.

Check the voltage between the fuse inlet contact and earth. Ensure it is greater than 11 V.

Replace the junction unit

Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.

3

If fualts are present, repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine compartment junction unit and the solenoid.

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

The test can be considered over

Replace the junction unit

Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.

4

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from engine control unit connector B

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

Press the START/STOP button.

Measure the voltage between Pin 2 of the solenoid wiring side connector and Earth. Ensure it is less than 0.3 V.

See L036 TURBINE VARIABLE GEOMETRY CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine control unit and the solenoid

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

5

Press the START/STOP button. Measure the voltage between Pin 2 of the solenoid wiring side connector and Earth. Ensure it is greater than 500 kOhm.

See L036 TURBINE VARIABLE GEOMETRY CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine control unit and the solenoid

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

6

Measure the resistance between Pin 1 of the solenoid wiring side connector and Pin 7 of engine control unit wiring side connector. Ensure it is less than 5 Ohms.

See L036 TURBINE VARIABLE GEOMETRY CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine control unit and the solenoid

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

7

Reconnect the wiring harness connector to the engine control unit

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

Connect a test bulb between pins 1 and 2 of the solenoid

See L036 TURBINE VARIABLE GEOMETRY CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE

Press the START/STOP button.

Carry out the following active diagnosis: Turbo regulator, check that the bulb comes on.

Move on to Step 9

Move on to Step 8

8

Reconnect the wiring harness connector to the solenoid

See L036 TURBINE VARIABLE GEOMETRY CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE

Replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R

The test can be considered over

9

Replace the solenoid valve

Op. 1064B42 SOLENOID CONTROLLING TURBOCHARGER VANE POSITION ACTUATOR - R.R

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Visually inspect that during start-up, the turbocharger actuator connecting rod (turbine stator vanes adjustment device) moves by around 10 mm in the direction of the actuator.

With the engine switched off, connect connector EX09 and transducer EX06 in the section of pipe between the actuator and the solenoid valve.

Select intake pressure measuring gauge in instrument mode.

Start up the engine and let it idle, then wait until pressure pressure stabilizes.

The reading should not exceed 450-550 mmHg.

The graphic acquisition of the pressure trend can be carried out by selecting either the 2000 or 4000 mmHg scale.
