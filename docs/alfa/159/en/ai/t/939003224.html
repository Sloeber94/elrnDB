Longitudinal electrical adjustment not working/not working properly

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK ON SWITCH STATUS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Select the following control unit at the diagnostic equipment:

Driver's/passenger seat control unit.

Check the value of the following parameter:

Sliding button and check that it indicates released when the switch is released and that it indicates pressed when the switch is pressed.

Move on to Step 5

The status of the switch does not change.

Move on to Step 2

2

CHECK ON WIRING CONNECTIONS AT SLIDING SWITCH

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the connection of the wiring at the switch for the electric seat control.

Also check for any oxidation/impurities at the connector/switch.

Move on to Step 3

1.Switch wiring connector detached or badly positioned on seat mounting.

1.Attach connector correctly to switch.

2. Presence of oxidation/impurities at connector/control unit.

2.Eliminate oxidation/impurities at connector/switch.

3

CHECK ON WIRING CONNECTIONS AT CONTROL UNIT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the connection of the wiring at the control unit for the electric seat control.

Also check for any oxidation/impurities at the connector/control unit.

Move on to Step 4

1.Seat wiring connector detached or badly positioned on seat control unit.

1.Attach connector correctly to control unit.

2. Presence of oxidation/impurities at connector/control unit

2.Eliminate oxidation/impurities at connector/control unit.

4

SLIDING SWITCH OPERATION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the sliding switch is working properly

Move on to Step 9

Incorrect operation of switch.

Replace the switch

Op. 7045J50 DRIVER'S SIDE FRONT SEAT POSITION MEMORISATION SYSTEM ECU - R+R FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL WIRING CHECK

5

CHECKS USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the diagnostic equipment connected, check:

through active diagnosis that the sliding actuator is working properly.

Move on to Step 9

Incorrect operation of sliding actuator.

Move on to Step 6

6

CHECK ON CONNECTION AT MOTOR

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the connection is correctly attached at the motor

Also check for any oxidation/impurities at the connector/motor.

Move on to Step 7

1.Connectors at motor detached or badly positioned.

1.Attach connector correctly to motor

2.Presence of oxidation/impurities at connector/motor.

2.Eliminate oxidation/impurities at connector/motor.

7

CHECK ON ELECTRIC SEAT WIRING

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the electric seat wiring.

Move on to Step 8

Presence of cuts/tears in the seat wiring.

Replacement of the torn/broken wiring see wiring diagram

See E3076 ELECTRICALLY-ADJUSTABLE SEATS

8

CHECK ON CORRECT ENGAGEMENT OF PROPELLER SHAFTS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the propeller shafts between the motor and the steering rack engage correctly

Move on to Step 9

Shafts detached/broken

Correct engagement/replacement of transmission cable(s) or replacement of motor assembly

Op. 7045J26 FRONT SEAT SLIDE MOTOR, L/H OR R/H - R+R FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL WIRING CHECK

9

DRIVER'S/PASSENGER SEAT CONTROL UNIT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Replace the driver's/passenger seat control unit

Op. 7045J36 SEAT MOVEMENT ECU R R FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Contact technical service for further information on the problem

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The seat does not move forwards, there are difficulties attaching the sliding guides

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK ON CORRECT ATTACHMENT OF BOWDEN CABLE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the bowden cable is correctly attached or whether it is broken at the attachment device

Move on to Step 2

Bowden cable disconnected from the housing (lever, device) or broken

Reposition bowden cable or replace it.

2

CHECK ON CONDITION OF GUIDE RELEASE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the guide release ratchet mechanism at the connecting rod.

Move on to Step 3

Plastic ratchet mechanism broken/distorted.

Replace guide release ratchet mechanism

3

CHECK ON GUIDE DEVICE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the guide device.

Move on to Step 4

Device on sliding guide broken/distorted.

Replace the sliding guides

Op. 7045A66 SLIDING GUIDE (ONE),LEFT OR RIGHT FOR FRONT SEAT - R.R.

4

CHECK FOR PRESENCE OF FOREIGN BODIES BETWEEN GUIDE PROFILES

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that there are no foreign bodies between the guide profiles and/or in the guide control mechanism.

Move on to Step 5

Presence of foreign bodies that adversely affect the operation of the system.

Remove the foreign bodies from the system.

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace the sliding guides

Op. 7045A66 SLIDING GUIDE (ONE),LEFT OR RIGHT FOR FRONT SEAT - R.R.
