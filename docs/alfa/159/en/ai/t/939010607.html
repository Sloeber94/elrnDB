Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

1. Undo bolts (1a) and nuts (1b) and remove the particulate filter bracket (1c).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Loosen the bands and disconnect the hoses from the rigid pipes for the particulate filter differential pressure sensor.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Unscrew and remove the exhaust gas temperature sensor on the particulate filter.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Undo the nuts fixing the intermediate exhaust pipe to the particulate filter.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Position a hydraulic jack (1a) equipped with a tool (1b) for supporting the particulate filter.

2. Release the exhaust pipe assembly from its flexible blocks and remove.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the rear exhaust silencer in its housing and secure to the flexible supports.

- Remove the hydraulic jack with the suitable tool for supporting the fuel tank.

1. Tighten the nuts fixing the intermediate exhaust pipe to the particulate filter to the specified torque.
