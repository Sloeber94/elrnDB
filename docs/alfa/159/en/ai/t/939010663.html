1. Undo the bolt (1a) and disconnect the engine oil supply pipe to the cooling radiator (1b), rear pipe side, then remove.

Refitting

Removing

- Refit the front pipe carrying engine oil to the cooling radiator, connect to the rear pipe and tighten the bolt.

- Connect the front engine oil supply pipe to the cooling radiator, engine oil cooling radiator side, and tighten the bolt.

- Fit the radiator air duct in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

- Place the cold air intake for the air filter back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.
