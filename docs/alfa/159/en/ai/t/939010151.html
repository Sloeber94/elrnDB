After rain or washing, water infiltration through the sunroof is noticed.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

SUN ROOF DRAINAGE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the sunroof drainage pipes.

In particular, check:

  • pipe condition;
  • for dirt, leaves etc.

Move on to Step 2

Dirt in the drains.

Clean the guides with a vacuum cleaner and reapply grease if necessary using Molikote PG75 in the mechanism slide area.

If necessary, restore the correct fitting.

Op. 7030B38 SLIDING ROOF DRAINAGE PIPES (WATER DRAIN) - CHECK

2

SUN ROOF SEAL CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the sun roof seal..

In particular, check:

  • for kinks in the seal;
  • the condition of the seal.

Move on to Step 3

Seal fitting faulty

Refit the seal correctly

Op. 7030B22 GLASS SEAL - R+R WITH GLASS REMOVED

3

SUN ROOF ALIGNMENT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the sun roof is fitted correctly.

In particular, check:

  • correct alignment of the roof - compartment;
  • seal adheres correct to the roof.

Move on to Step 4

Faults with the sun roof installation.

Check that the sun roof is fitted correctly.

Op. 7030B17 SUN-ROOF GLASS - ALIGNMENT

4

CHECK THE SEAL OF THE SUNROOF SLIDE BOLTS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the seal of the bolts securing the slides to the sunroof structure.

End of diagnosis

Incorrect bolt fastening.

Restore the correct fastenings of the slides to the sunroof structure and check for the seal
