Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the rocker controlling the relevant valve.

2. Remove the associated hydraulic tappet.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Lubricate the hydraulic tappet with engine oil and replace in its seat.

- Lubricate the valve control rocker with engine oil and replace in its seat.

If the valve control rockers are replaced, the camshafts must also be replaced.
