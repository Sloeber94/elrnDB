Removing

Refitting

- Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the battery terminal (-).

Description

Connector

-

Battery

See A001 BATTERY

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the air flow meter.

Description

Connector

1

Air flow meter

See K041 AIR FLOW METER

2. Loosen the band and disconnect the corrugated hose from the air flow meter.

3. Loosen the band (3a) and remove the air flow meter (3b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the air flow meter back in its housing and secure it using the band.

- Connect the corrugated hose to the air flow meter and secure it using the band.

- Connect the electrical connection to the air flow meter.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods
