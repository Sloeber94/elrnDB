1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the lock (2a) from the motor (2b).

3. If necessary, undo the bolts (3a), then disconnect the electrical connection (3b) and separate the bridge (3c) from the motor (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

- Check that the motor and the bridge are not damaged.

- If removed, position the bridge, then connect the electrical connection and tighten the bolts.

- Position the motor on the lock.

- Tighten the bolts.
