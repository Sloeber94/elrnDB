The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0110 (Air flow meter temperature signal)

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected if the junction unit inlet voltage (Pin 53) is greater than 4.95 V (positive short circuit) (detail "Beyond the upper limit" or less than 0.05 V (short circuit to earth or break in the circuit) (detail "Below the lower limit"). In this case the junction unit replacement value is 40 °C.
