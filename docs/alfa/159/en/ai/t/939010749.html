Refitting

Removing

- Refit a new safety ring in its seat on the left driveshaft.

- Lubricate the mating surfaces on the left drive shaft with oil.

- Connect the left driveshaft to the differential.

Fit the drive shaft until the safety ring locks. Take care not to spoil the teeth.

- Refit the left drive shaft in its seat on the wheel hub and secure by screwing in a new nut without tightening.

1. Tighten the bolt securing the lower fork connecting the front suspension damper to the lower wishbone to the specified torque.
