The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0235 (Supercharging pressure sensor), with detail "short circuit to +Vbatt or open circuit"

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected if there is a positive short circuit or break in the circuit connected to Pin 7 of the junction unit. As a result the maximum power is reduced and the exhaust gas recirculation valve is switched off. In addition, if there is a short circuit to the positive, the supercharging pressure can no longer be controlled.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
