Applies to: all engines; all production periods

FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

Current socket/ cigar lighter P50 and the one at the back on the tunnel P51 receive a power supply from the ignition-controlled line (INT/A) of relay T12 and are protected by fuses F44 and F52 respectively, both housed in the junction unit under the dashboard B2.

In the STATION WAGON version the rear current socket P52 power supply arrives via the relay T21 located in the fuse box in the boot B45; this is energized by the ignition-controlled line (INT/A) which arrives directly from the steering lock node M89; fuse F55, also located in B45, protects the P52 power supply circuit.

Unlike the other two, the rear current socket does not have a ring nut that lights up when the side lights are switched on.
