Removing

Refitting

Op. 7065C60 INTERIOR REAR VIEW MIRROR FOR VERSIONS WITH RAIN/DUSK SENSOR - R.R.

1. Release the bulb holder (1a) releasing the retainers (1b) and (1c).

2. Move the dusk sensor slightly.

3. Disconnect the electrical connection.

4. Recover the dusk sensor.

5. If necessary, remove dusk sensor mount (5a) after undoing retainers (5b).

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the dusk sensor is undamaged and that the sensitive part is not dirty.

- If removed, refit the dusk sensor and do up the retainers.

- Connect the electrical connection.

- Position the dusk sensor.

- Refit the cover and attach the retainers.

- Check that the windscreen is clean.
