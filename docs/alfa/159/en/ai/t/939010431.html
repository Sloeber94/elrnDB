Removing

Refitting

1. Undo and remove the sump condensed engine oil return pipe connector.

2. Unscrew and remove the engine oil level sensor.

3. Undo bolts (3a) and remove buffeting bulkhead (3b).

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the bulkhead and tighten the bolts fixing it to the oil sump.

Screw in the engine oil level sensor.

Fit the sump condensed engine oil return pipe connector.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the crankcase oil sump partition (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Removing
