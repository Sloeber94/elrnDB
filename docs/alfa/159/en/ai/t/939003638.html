With the ignition ON, the engine oil temperature warning light in the additional instrument panel is not working;

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

the engine oil temperature faulty signal is made available to the instrument panel and, via a serial line, to the gauge.
