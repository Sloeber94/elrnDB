PRELIMINARY CHECKS

Problems encountered

Operation

First of all check whether the problem is accompanied by poor braking action.

Visually check the level of the brake fluid in the reservoir

Op. 0010T64 BRAKE AND/OR CLUTCH FLUID CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP AS NECESSARY

Move on to Step 1

Poor braking action detected.

Proceed as instructed in the

See table F001 POOR BRAKING ACTION

Insufficient brake fluid level.

Restore the correct level

Op. 0010T64 BRAKE AND/OR CLUTCH FLUID CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP AS NECESSARY

and carry out a road test. If the fault persists, move on to Step 1.

Problems encountered

Operation
