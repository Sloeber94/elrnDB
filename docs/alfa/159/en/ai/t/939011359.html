Refitting

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

Op. 7210E10 R.R. FRONT BUMPER

1. (For the left grille only) Remove the duct (1a) releasing the retainers (1b).

2. Release the retainers for the electrical wiring.

3. Remove the grille (3a) releasing the retainers (3b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the grille is not damaged.

- Position the grille and attach the retainers.

- Attach the retainers for the electrical wiring.

- (For the left grille only) Position the duct and attach the retainers.
