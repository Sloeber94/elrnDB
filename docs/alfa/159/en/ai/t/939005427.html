Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK TYRE WEAR

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the tyre wear status.

More specifically:

- wear on outer edge;

- wear on inner edge;

- wear on tyre shoulder;

- tread wear

Move on to Step 4

Uniform wear on both sides: inflation pressure low

Make sure tyres are inflated to the correct pressure. If necessary, replace the tyres.

Step wear on one side (outer or inner)

Move on to Step 2

Wear on outer edge: exterior smooth

Move on to Step 2

Tyre shoulder wear

The problem is due to the customer's over-sporty driving style: If necessary, replace the tyres.

Central tread wear: inflation pressure excessive

Make sure tyres are inflated to the correct pressure. If necessary, replace the tyres.

Uniform wear on tread surface: compound quality

Replace the tyres.

Tread wear.

Replace the tyres.

SHOCK ABSORBER EFFICIENCY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the efficiency of the shock absorbers.

End of diagnosis

Front shock absorbers inefficient.

Replace the shock absorbers

Op. 4410A12 FRONT SUSPENSION DAMPER (ONE), LH OR RH - FOR MCPHERSON STRUT SYSTEM - INCLUDES DISMANTLING/REASSEMBLY OF ASSEMBLY AT BENCH - R.R

If necessary, replace the front tyres.

Rear shock absorbers inefficient.

Replace the shock absorbers

Op. 4420A12 REAR SUSPENSION DAMPER (ONE), LH OR RH - FOR MCPHERSON STRUT SYSTEM - INCLUDES DISMENTLING/REBUILDING OF ASSEMBLY AT BENCH R R

If necessary, replace the rear tyres.
