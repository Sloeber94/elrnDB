5540B53 BULB (ONE) FOR RIGHT LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

Kind

Applies to

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Open the bonnet.

- Proceed as follows to replace the high beam bulb.

1. Remove the cover (bayonet mount).

2. Disconnect the electrical connection for the bulb.

3. Release the bulb retaining clips by pushing in the arrowed direction.

4. Extract the bulb from the bulb holder.

If the bulb is working properly, avoid touching it with your hands. If this does happen, clean the bulb with alcohol.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

- Proceed as follows to replace the turn signal bulb.

1. Remove the cover (bayonet mount).

2. Extract the bulb holder and bulb (bayonet fitting).

3. Extract the bulb from the bulb holder (bayonet fitting).

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check the condition of the bulb and make sure that it is clean.

- Proceed as follows to replace the high beam bulb.

Avoid touching the bulb with your hands. If this does happen, clean the bulb with alcohol.

1. Fit the bulb in the bulb holder. The correct position for the bulb is illustrated.

- Do up the bulb retaining clips.

- Connect the electrical connection for the bulb.

- Refit the cover (bayonet mount).

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

- Proceed as follows to replace the turn signal bulb.

- Fit the bulb in the bulb holder (bayonet mount).

- If necessary, fit the bulb holder and bulb in the housing (bayonet mount).

- Refit the cover (bayonet mount).

- Close the bonnet lid when the operation is complete.

- Check that the bulb is working.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods
