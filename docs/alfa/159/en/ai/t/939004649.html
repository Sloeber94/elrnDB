Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive

FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

The (earth) activation signal for the rear fog lamps reaches pin 35 of connector G of M1 from the control button in the panel to the left of the steering wheel H90.

The side lights and dipped hedlamps activation signal is sent to pin 28 of connector G and 2 of connector I of the Body Computer M1 from the steering column switch unit H5.

See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

the fog lights activation signal reaches pin 1 of connector I of the Body Computer M1

See E2030 FOG LIGHTS

On the basis of these signals, Body Computer M1 controls the power supply of the rear fog lamp in tailight F36 from pin 31 of connector E of M1.
