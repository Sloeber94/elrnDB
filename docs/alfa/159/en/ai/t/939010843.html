Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Mark the position of the bush in relation to the lower track control arm.

1. Working on the press and using suitable tools, remove the rear bush assembly (1a) from the lower track control arm (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Working on the press and using suitable tools, place a new rear bush assembly in its housing on the lower track control arm.

Take care over the fitting position: the bush has two splines (1a) in the internal opening which should match with the projections on the track control arm.
