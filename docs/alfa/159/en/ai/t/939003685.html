Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the Examiner in the Instrument Panel Node ambient check that the seata belt not fastened warning light is correctly activated in Active Diagnosis

Move on to Step 2

1.The seat belt not fastened warning light does not flash

1.Replace the centre front courtesy light

Op. 7040E40 MODULAR FRONT COURTESY LIGHT - R R

2.Seat belt not fastened switch defective

2. Replace seat belt mounting with switch

Op. 7065A15

3.Seat belt switch not correctly connected to earth

3.Restore earth connection

2

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the diagnostic equipment connected, check at the Air Bag Node that the fault is no longer present.
