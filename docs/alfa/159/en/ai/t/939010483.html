- Prise up and remove the exhaust side camshaft front oil seal.

Take care not to damage the oil seal seal during its removal.The oil seal removal operation is destructive so the seal must always be replaced.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
