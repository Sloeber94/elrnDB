Removing

Refitting

Op. 5580C18 AIR BAG MODULE - R+R

- Follow the warnings for the removal of the Air Bag module closely.

Op. 4110A18 TRIM ON STEERING WHEEL - R.R.

1. Release the retainer (1a) then move the module (1c) releasing the retainers (1b).

2. Release the wiring (2a) from the retainers (2b).

3. Keep the modules and wiring.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the modules and wiring are not damaged.

- Position the modules and attach the wiring to the retainers.

- Attach the retainers for the modules.

Op. 4110A18 TRIM ON STEERING WHEEL - R.R.

Op. 5580C18 AIR BAG MODULE - R+R

- Check that the radio controls are working.
