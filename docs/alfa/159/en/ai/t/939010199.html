1060GS DEBIMETER OPERATION CHECK

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Press the START/STOP button and turn off the engine.

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from the air flow meter.

Press the START/STOP button.

Check that the voltage across pin 1 of the meter

See K041 AIR FLOW METER

and the earth is greater than 11 V.

Move on to Step 3

Move on to Step 2

2

If faults are found, restore the wiring between the engine compartment junction unit and the debimeter

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Replace it if necessary.

The test can be considered over

Replace the junction unit

Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.

3

Check that the voltage across pin 1 and 2 of the meter

See K041 AIR FLOW METER

Replace the wiring if necessary

is greater than 11 V.

Move on to Step 5

Move on to Step 4

4

If faults are found, restore the wiring between the engine control unit and the debimeter

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Replace it if necessary.

The test can be considered over

Replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R

5

Check that the voltage across pin 3 of the meter

See K041 AIR FLOW METER

and earth is between 4.8 and 5.2 V

Move on to Step 4

6

Replace the debimeter

Op. 1060G10 AIR FLOW METER - R.R.

Repair the original debimeter and replace the engine management control unit.

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R
