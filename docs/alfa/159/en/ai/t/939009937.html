Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Noise (creaking) is heard when the clutch pedal is operated

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the engine idling and the gearbox in neutral, press the clutch pedal fully down and see whether the noise disappears. Then, with the engine running and the handbrake on to lock the wheels, engage first gear and release the clutch to see whether the noise disappears.

Move on to Step 1

1. The noise disappears with the clutch pressed

1. Proceed as instructed in the

See table S01 ENGINE KNOCK

2. The noise disappears with the gear engaged.

2. Proceed as instructed in the

See table S015 NOISE WHILST DRIVING

1

CHECK THRUST BEARING

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the thrust bearing: it should not be worn

Move on to Step 2

Bearing fault.

Replace the thrust bearing.

Op. 1820B38 HYDRAULIC CLUTCH ACTUATOR WITH COAXIAL CONTROL - R.R WITH GEARBOX REMOVED

2

CLUTCH CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the cluch plate and the flexible coupling.

End of diagnosis

Fault in clutch plate or flexible coupling

Replace the clutch plate

Op. 1810A20 CLUTCH - R + R

2

CLUTCH CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the cluch plate and the flexible coupling.

End of diagnosis

Fault in clutch plate or flexible coupling

Replace the clutch assembly

Op. 1810A20 CLUTCH - R + R
