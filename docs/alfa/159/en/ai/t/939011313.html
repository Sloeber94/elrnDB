Removing

Refitting

1. Release seat sliding control cable (1a) from retainer (1b).

2. Release retainers (2a) of Air Bag wiring (2b).

3. Release retainers (3a) and (3b) and remove spring device (3c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the spring device is not damaged.

- Position the spring device and attach the retainers.

- Engage the Air Bag wiring retainers.

- Engage the seat sliding comand cable into the retainer.
