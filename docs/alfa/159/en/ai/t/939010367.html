0010T11 ENGINE OIL - CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP, IF NECESSARY

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

The engine oil level check should be carried out with the vehicle on a flat surface with the engine off a few minutes (5) after the engine has been switched off.

- If the oil level is close to or actually below the MIN mark, introduce the recommended oil through the filler until it reaches the MAX mark.

If, after checking, the engine oil level is above the MAX mark, it must be corrected.

After topping up the engine oil, before checking the level, let the engine run for a few seconds and wait for a few minutes after it has been switched off.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

The engine oil level check should be carried out with the vehicle on a flat surface with the engine off a few minutes (5) after the engine has been switched off.

- Extract the engine oil dipstick, clean it and then fully reinset it, extract it and check that the level is between the MIN and MAX marks on the actual dipstick.

- If the oil level is close to or actually below the MIN mark, introduce the recommended oil through the filler until it reaches the MAX mark.

If, after checking, the engine oil level is above the MAX mark, it must be corrected.

After topping up the engine oil, before checking the level, let the engine run for a few seconds and wait for a few minutes after it has been switched off.
