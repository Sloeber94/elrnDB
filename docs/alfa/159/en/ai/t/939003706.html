PRELIMINARY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the engine coolant level is within the preset levels and that the refrigerant fluid circulates correctly in the system.

Check that there are no constrictions in the water pipes to the heater.

Move on to Step 1

1. Engine coolant level too low.

1.Restore the correct level checking that there are no leaks from the system.

2. Coolant does not circulate correctly (thermostatic valve not working properly).

2.Refer to the

See table K025 ENGINE COOLANT TEMPERATURE GAUGE NOT WORKING

3. Water pipes constricted.

3.Repair the water pipes.

1

CHECK ON CORRECT CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the engine on and the compressor off, place the control unit to the AUTO function with the temperature knob at the maximum hot HI position.

Also select the recirculation button to the outside air intake position and set the fan speed to 3-4 bars (display) and the distribution to front ventilation.

Using the Examiner, check that the engine temperature has reached at least 70°C.

There is a noticeable variation in temperature (getting hotter).

End of diagnosis

The temperature does not appear to get hotter.

Move on to Step 2

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment and check that there are no errors:

  • at the climate control node;
  • at the engine temperature sensor;
  • at the passenger compartment temperature sensor;
  • at the outside temperature sensor;
  • at the air mixture actuators.

Move on to Step 3

1. Error at climate control node.

1. Move on to Step 4

2. Error at the engine temperature sensor.

2. Refer to the

See table K025 ENGINE COOLANT TEMPERATURE GAUGE NOT WORKING

3. Error at the passenger compartment temperature sensor.

3. Move on to Step 4

4. Error at the outside temperature sensor.

4. Move on to Step 4

5. Short circuit to earth error.

5. Check that the Lin Bus cables are not caught between the metal parts (pay particular attention to the gearbox bolts) and move on to Step 4

6. Short circuit to +12V error.

6. Check that the Lin Bus cables are not caught between the metal parts (pay particular attention to the gearbox bolts) and move on to Step 4

7. Mechanical interference / stuck error.

7. Move on to Step 6

Air mixture actuator not working

Move on to Step 4.

4

CHECK ON CLIMATE CONTROL NODE ELECTRICAL CONNECTIONS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the engine switched off, remove the control unit from the dashaboard support and disconnect the wiring connector from the control unit and check visually for any damage to the connector and reconnect it correctly to check for false contacts.

Check the electrical connections at the outside temperature sensor, the passenger compartment temperature sensor and the air mixture actuators.

Move on to Step 5

1.Electrical connections loose or damaged.

1.Refit the electrical connections correctly.

2.Outside temperature sensor not working.

2.Replace the outside temperature sensor

Op. 7005R30 ELECTRICALLY CONTROLLED DOOR MIRROR (ONE) - R+R

3.Passenger compartment temperature sensor not working.

3.Replace the climate control system control unit

Op. 5040D15

4. Air mixture actuator not working

4.Replace the air mixture actuator

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF MIXTURE ACTUATOR

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check whether the electrical wiring connections are loose.

Disconnect and visually inspect for damage at the connector contacts and reconnect the connection to the motor mounting.

If it persists, before replacing the actuator, check its operation by manually activating the function that the actuator should carry out.

With the engine running, switch on the fan at maximum speed.

Place the flaps in the various distribution positions via the control/control unit (manually).

Check the air flow in the passenger compartment with your hand at the vents.

End of diagnosis

1. Electrical connections loose or damaged.

1. Renew the electrical connections

See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

2. There is no air flow intake into the passenger compartment through the vents.

2. Move on to Step 6.

6

CHECK ON IMPEDIMENTS TO THE ROTATION OF THE MIXTURE ACTUATOR

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Remove the actuator and check through the control unit that the actuator turns checking for the presence of mechanical impediments to its rotation.

End of diagnosis

1.The actuator does not turn.

1.Replace the air mixture actuator

2.Kinematic components of flaps broken/stuck.

2.Replace the components that is faulty.
