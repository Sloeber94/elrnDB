Q006 SUN ROOF DOES NOT STOP IN END OF TRAVEL POSITION

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the relevant control is operated, the sunroof opens/closes normally but does not stop correctly in the open, swivel opening or closed positions.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK SUNROOF CONTROL DEVICE ALIGNMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Remove the glass and check that the slides and guides are correctly aligned.

Op. 7030B18 SLIDING ROOF GLASS PANEL - R+R

Move on to Step 2

Faults with the sun roof installation.

Check that the sun roof is fitted correctly.

Op. 7030B54 SUN ROOF (ASSEMBLY) - ALIGNMENT AND POSITIONING

2

CHECK THE SUNROOF END TRAVEL DEVICE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the end travel switch and the control cam located on the motor.

End of diagnosis

Faults in the end travel device

Restore the connections or replace the motor

Op. 7030B70 SUN ROOF MOVEMENT MOTOR - R+R
