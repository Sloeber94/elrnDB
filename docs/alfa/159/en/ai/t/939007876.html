This bearing is used by the ABS active sensors to calculate the wheel speed. It should NEVER come into contact with metal shavings because they could alter the gap and prevent a correct reading from the sensor.

Op. 4450B12 FRONT WHEELS (TWO) - R+R

Check that the noise is not due to irregular friction between the shock absorber stem and the gasket, checking the condition of the stem chrome surface.

End of diagnosis

There is irregular friction

Lubricate the stem raising the dust cover with a layer of silicon grease.
