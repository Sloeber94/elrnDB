Refitting

Lift up the wiper arm.

1. Release the retainers (1a) for the blade from the wiper arm housings (1b).

2. Keep the blade.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the blade is not damaged and that the rubber is intact.

Place the blade in position attaching the retainers to the wiper arm.

Lower the wiper arm.

Check that the rear windscreen wiper is working properly.
