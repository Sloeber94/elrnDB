0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

First of all check that none of the following conditions exists:

. 5 seconds have not elapsed since the key was extracted

. The same key or another object was inserted in the key recognition device within 5 seconds of the key being extracted

. A key recovery extraction was carried out

Move on to Step 1

5 seconds have not elapsed since the key was extracted

Wait for 5 seconds

The same key or another object was inserted in the key recognition device within 5 seconds of the key being extracted

Do not reinsert the key or another object in the recognition device

A key recovery extraction was carried out

Proceed as instructed

See table B006 KEY CANNOT BE EXTRACTED FROM BLOCK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic socket and check at the brake node that the data for the wheel speed is valid

Move on to Step 2

Error detected for the wheel speed

Continue as described by the diagnostic equipment

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic socket and check the following at the steering lock node:

- That there are no errors

- Continuity of the wiring

See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

- B-Can continuity

- Correct presence of signals at the steering lock

See Test 5580ED ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK CHECK

Move on to Step 3

Presence of errors

Continue as described by the diagnostic equipment

Wiring broken

Restore wiring

See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

B-Can interrupted

Restore B-Can continuity

See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES

Signals not correct

Restore signals

See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

Problems encountered

End of diagnosis
