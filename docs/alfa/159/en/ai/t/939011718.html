Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

DESCRIPTION

The instruments currently on board the vehicle in question are composed of the main control panel located inside the steering wheel and a central instrument console in the middle of the dashboard. The main panel contains a large reconfigurable matrix LCD (HIGH version) or a negative contrast alphanumerical LCD (LOW version only).

There is also a buzzer and a brightness sensor inside the instrument panel for the automatic adjustment (daytime/nightime) of the brightness of the instrument panel lights.

See E2535 CONTROL LIGHTS

The centre instrument console consists of:

- fuel level gauge with reserve warning light

- engine coolant temperature gauge with overheating warning light

- turbo pressure gauge

The instrument panel is a CAN node through which information is exchanged with the Body Computer and with the other electronic units.

The centre module containing the three sets of instruments is not a B-CAN node, but is connected by means of a special serial line to the instrument panel node which sends the module all the signals necessary for the display of the required information.

- Speedometer: the function of the speedometer includes displaying the vehicle speed in the instrument panel: this information comes from the ABS control unit, via the Body Computer.

- Engine rpm: this function involves the display on the instrument panel of the engine rotation speed by means of the message sent via the CAN from the engine management control unit.

- Milometer: the milometer function includes the display and memorizing of the total and trip mileage: the journey information is processed by the Body Computer by means of a counter; the special button in the panel to the side of the steering wheel zeroes the trip meter.

- Fuel level: the Body Computer receives the signal from the sensor integrated in the pump assembly inside the fuel tank and processes it to guarantee suitable filtering/damping for the panel gauge, also managing the reserve warning light.

- Engine oil level: the function signals the status of the engine oil level at starting (for about 3 seconds) in the display. The actual instrument panel operates a hot wire oil level sensor. If the oil level is below the minimum value, the message "minimum oil level" is also sent.

- Brake pad wear state: this function involves the specific warning light coming on: the Body Computer detects the brake pad wear status when braking (signal from the brake pedal) in a prolonged manner (occasional signals are ignored).

- Handbrake applied: the warning light for the handbrake status is controlled by a specific switch; in addition, if a given speed level is exceeded in conjunction with this signal, a warning signal is activated: the signal is sent, via the CAN, from the Body Computer.

- Insufficient brake fluid: this function involves the "insufficient brake fluid level" warning light in the instrument panel coming on with the signal sent by the Body Computer via the CAN.
