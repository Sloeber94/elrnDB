Removing

Refitting

1. Prise up and remove the right differential shaft oil seal.

Take care not to damage the oil seal seat during its removal.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Lubricate a new right differential shaft oil seal using transmission fluid.

- Place the new differential right shaft oil seal in its housing.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
