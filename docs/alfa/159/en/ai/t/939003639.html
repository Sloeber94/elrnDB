Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

With the ignition ON, the engine oil temperature gaute in the additional instrument panel is not working

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

the engine oil temperature faulty signal is made available to the instrument panel and, via a serial line, to the gauge.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected to the diagnostic socket, check the operation of the pointer through active diagnosis

Move on to Step 2

1.The pointer does not work for the serial line with the NQS interrupted

1.Renew serial line

2.Instrument power supply interrupted

2.Restore power supply

3.Instrument defective

3.Replace gauge

Op. 5560C51 COMBINED ANALOGUE CONTROL PANEL - R+R OR REPLACE

2

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the continuity/insulation of the connection between the engine oil temperature sensor and the instrument panel

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Move on to Step 3

Cable interrupted/not insulated

Restore continuity/insulation of the cable.

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
