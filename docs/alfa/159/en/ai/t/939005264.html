1048A63 HOSE CONNECTING RESONATOR, FLOW METER AND THROTTLE BODY - R.R.

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the nut (1a) fixing the bracket (1b) integrated with the air hose (1c).

2. Loosen the band securing the air hose (1c) to the throttle body.

3. Loosen the band (3a) and disconnect the oil vapour recovery pipe (3b) from the air hose (1c).

4. Loosen the band (4a) and disconnect the hose (4b) from the resonator.

5. Loosen the band and remove the air hose (1c) from the flow meter.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Connect the air hose to the flow meter and secure it using the band.

- Connect the hose to the resonator and secure it using the band.

- Connect the oil vapour pipe to the air hose and secure with the band.

- Tighten the band securing the air hose to the throttle body.

- Tighten the nut securing the bracket integrated with the air hose.
