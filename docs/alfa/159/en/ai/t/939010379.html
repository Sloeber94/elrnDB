0010T82 DIESEL SYSTEM - BLEED WATER FROM DIESEL FILTER RESEVOIR

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

If the presence of water in the fuel filer is revealed on the vehicle display, bleed as soon as possible.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Turn key on without starting the engine.

This operation pressurises the fuel filter.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

- Disconnect the electrical connection from the water presence sensor on the fuel filter.

Fit a pipe of appropriate length to the pipe section on the wate presence sensor on the fuel filter to allow fluid to be drained into a suitable container after bleeding.

- Loosen the water presence sensor on the fuel filter.

Carry out this action gradually, taking care to check that the amount of fuel expelled is between 75 and 80 cc

- Tighten the water sensor on the fuel filter.

- Examine the expelled fluid: if it contains water without evident traces of diesel, repeat the bleeding operation.

- If traces of diesel appear, repeating the bleeding operation ensures that the fuel filter does not contain any more water.

- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position.

- Connect the electrical connection to the water presence sensor on the fuel filter.

- Check that no more leaks are present in the lower part of the fuel filter after running the engine for one minute.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
