Refitting

Removing

- Check that the coaxial cable is not damaged.

- Place the coaxial cable in position and connect the retainers.

- Close the rear wiring protection and attach the retainers.

- Connect the rear connection for the aerial lead.

- Close the front wiring protection and attach the retainer.

- Connect the front connection for the aerial lead.

- Attach the roof lining to the rear retainers and support it.

- Tighten the nut fixing the roof lining in the rear courtesy light housing.

- Attach the rear etaining buttons.

- Working in the front courtesy light opening, tighten the bolts fixing the roof lining.
