STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check whether any of the errors are present using the diagnostic equipment:

  • P2149 – Unit 2 injector control (injectors 2-3)
  • P2151 – Unit 2 injector control (injectors 2-3)

Move on to Step 21

Move on to Step 2

2

Press the START/STOP button and turn on the panel.

Check that the voltage across Pin 2 of engine control unit connector B

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

and earth is greater than 0.3 V.

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine control unit and injector 2

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

3

Press the START/STOP button.

Check that the resistance across pin 2 of control unit connector B

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

and earth is greater than 500 kOhm. Continue to Step 7

Move on to Step 4

4

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from injector 2.

Check that the resistance across pin 2 of control unit connector B

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

and Earth is more than 500 kOhm.

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine control unit and the injector

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

6
