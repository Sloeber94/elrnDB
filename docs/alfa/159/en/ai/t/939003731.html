The seat belt does not rewind correctly and/or fully after the buckle is undone.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check visually for folds, fraying and/or damage to the belt that could prevent it from rewinding.

Move on to Step 1

Folds are present on the front belt

Remove the folds and replace the belt if necessary

Op. 7065A10 FRONT SEAT BELT (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Folds are present on the rear belt

Remove the folds and replace the belt if necessary

Op. 7065B10 REAR SEAT BELT (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Front belt strap damaged

Replace the front belt

Op. 7065A10 FRONT SEAT BELT (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Rear belt strap damaged

Replace the rear seat belt

Op. 7065B10 REAR SEAT BELT (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

1

CHECK CORRECT INSTALLATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the system installation is correct.

End of diagnosis
