The customer complains of: The engine does not start, the starter motor is working normally

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the engine oil level is correct

Op. 0010T11 ENGINE OIL - CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP, IF NECESSARY

Check that the air intake/vacuum circuit is working properly

See Test 1048OA AIR INTAKE/VACUUM CIRCUIT CHECK

Check the condition of the fuel supply circuit and make sure that it is working properly

See Test 1040OA FUEL SUPPLY CIRCUIT CHECK

Move on to Step 1

Oil level incorrect

Restore the correct oil level

Op. 0010T11 ENGINE OIL - CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP, IF NECESSARY

1

AMBIENT PARAMETERS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Select the following control unit at the diagnostic equipment: EDC16C39 Diesel electronic injection control unit.

Check the value of the following parameters:

. Battery voltage

See Test 5530BB BATTERY VOLTAGE CHECK

. Objective Diesel pressure

. Measured Diesel pressure

See Test 1060GH DIESEL FUEL PRESSURE CHECK

. Engine rpm

See Test 1060GI ENGINE SPEED CHECK

Move on to Step 2

2

PARTICLE FILTRATION SYSTEM CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the DPF system is working properly

See Test 1080BE PARTICULATE FILTER SATURATION CHECK

Move on to Step 3

3

PREHEATING RELAY ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Carry out the active diagnosis for the preheating relay, checking that the result is positive.

The check on the following system is only necessary if the outside temperature is below 0 °C.

Move on to Step 5

The result of the active diagnosis is negative

Move on to Step 4

4

HEATER PLUGS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the heater plugs are working properly, by carrying out the

Move on to Step 5

5

ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the F1 fuse has not blown (it is the last one at the bottom of the vertical row)

Move on to Step 7

Fuse F1 has blown

Restore the connections or, if necessary, replace the junction unit in the engine compartment

Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.

HIGH PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment to the diganostic socket. Check as described in the

See Test 1060OD INSPECTION OF THE HIGH PRESSURE FUEL CIRCUIT

that the diesel pressure parameter is correct

Move on to Step 14

Pressure not correct

Move on to Step 9

7

LOW RETURN PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check as described in the

See Test 1060OA CHECK ON RETURN LOW PRESSURE FUEL CIRCUIT

that the pressure in the return pipe to the tank is less than 1 bar

Move on to Step 8

Pressure above the maximum limit: return pipe to the tank obstructed

Repair the pipe, if necessary, replace it

8

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check as described in the

See Test 1060OB CHECK ON SUPPLY LOW PRESSURE FUEL CIRCUIT

that the diesel pressure between the filter and the pressure pump is between 2 and 3 bar

Move on to Step 14

Pressure below the minimum limit or above the maximum limit

Move on to Step 9

9

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Constrict the return pipe from the pressure relief valve and carry out the test again

See Test 1060OB CHECK ON SUPPLY LOW PRESSURE FUEL CIRCUIT

with the pipe constricted, checking that the filter outlet pressure remains below 2 bar

Move on to Step 10

Pressure rises to 5 bar: pressure regulator valve on filter mounting incorrectly adjusted

Replace the valve

See Test 1044B16

10

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the difference in pressure upstream and downstream of the filter is less than 1 bar

Move on to Step 11

Difference in pressure more than 1 bar

Replace the diesel filter cartridge

See Test 1044B20

11

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the supply pump pressure at the filter inlet is more than 2.2 bar

Move on to Step 14

Pressure below 2 bar

Move on to Step 12

12

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Measure the difference in pressure between the electrical pump outlet and the filter inlet checking that it is less than 0.3 bar

Move on to Step 15

Difference in pressure more than 0.3 bar

Replace the pipe

13

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check as described in the

See Test 1040AA SUPPLY VOLTAGE AND ABSORPTION CURRENT CHECKS

that the pump supply voltage is equal to 12V and that the current uptake is between 2 and 10 A

Move on to Step 14

Voltage lower than 12 V

Check electrical wiring

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Current greater than 10 A: pump defective

Replace the fuel pump

Current less than 2 A: pump obstructed

Replace the fuel pump

and clean the fuel tank
