Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

When the alarm is activated, the control LED does not switch off

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Test results

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner Plus or Smart) and check that there are no faults in the system

End of diagnosis

There are faults present

Move on to Step 2

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment carryout the active diagnosis of the LED activation

End of diagnosis

The LED is not activated

Move on to Step 3

3

CHECK THE WIRING

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the continuity and insulation of the wiring between the Body Computer and the door panel

Check that the supply for the LED leaves the Body Computer and that the LED comes on

See E7015 ALARM

End of diagnosis

The supply for the LED does not leave the Body Computer

Replace Body Computer

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R.R

The supply is sent from the Body Computer but the LED does not light up

Replace the LED.
