The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0100 (Flow meter), with detail "Below the lower limit"

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected if the voltage at the junction unit inlet (Pin 42) is too low (short circuit to earth) for at least 0.3 secs.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
