Removing

Refitting

In the following procedure the removing-refitting of the left rear suspension anti-roll bar joint is described, carry out the same operations for the right rear suspension anti-roll bar joint.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 4450B06 LEFT REAR WHEEL - R.R

1. Undo the nuts (1a) and remove the rear suspension anti-roll bar joint (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the left rear suspension anti-roll bar joint (1a) in its housing and secure it by tightening the new nuts (1b) to the recommended torque.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Op. 4450B06 LEFT REAR WHEEL - R.R

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
