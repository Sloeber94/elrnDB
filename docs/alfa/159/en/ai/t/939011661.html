DESCRIPTION

The vehicle is fitted with two GAS DISCHARGE headlamps.

The electronics in each of the two headlamps switches the gas discharge bulbs on and off at the times defined by the software and in accordance with international legislation.

The system for each lamp comprises an igniter located at the base of the bulb and a ballast located in the lower part of the lamp.

The left lamp is the master control unit whilst the right one carries out a slave role. The master acquires the command for the dipped headlamps, the vehicle speed and from the potentiometers located on the wheel axles.

Depending on the inputs, the control unit controls the relevant adjustment motor, located in the headlamps, directly and tilts it as appropriate.

For more details

See descriptions 5540 EXTERIOR LIGHTING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

On versions with gas discharge headlamps, the light beam is adjusted automatically and there is no activation of the selector in the panel on the left side.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Xenon Gas headlamps

The device is enabled only with the dipped beams on.

The electronics for the two headlamps receive a supply from a fuse which protects other ignition-controlled power supplies.
