Applies to: all engines; all production periods

To raise the vehicle using lift arms or a workshop lift, arrange the ends of the arms or the lift in the areas shown in the diagram only.

The vehicle should only be raised at the side. Never raise the vehicle at the front. After having lifted the vehicle, support it using safety stands.
