Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Undo nuts (1a) and remove clips (1b) securing the lower fuel tank protection.

2. Remove the lower fuel tank protection.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Replace the lower fuel tank protection and secure it using the bands and the nuts.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
