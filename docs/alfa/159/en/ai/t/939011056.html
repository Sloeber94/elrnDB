Removing

Refitting

1. Extract the side repeater (1a) releasing the retainers (1b) and (1c).

2. Disconnect the electrical connection.

Description

Component

LEFT SIDE DIRECTION INDICATOR

See F020 LEFT SIDE REPEATER

RIGHT SIDE DIRECTION INDICATOR

3. Keep the side repeater.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the side repeater is not damaged.

- Connect the electrical connection.

- Fit the side repeater in place attaching the retainers.
