With the vehicle moving, there is noise coming from the rear area of the passenger compartment

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

First of all check that the boot closes correctly and that the outside aerial is correctly fastened.

Check that the wind noise is actually coming from the rear area.

Move on to Step 1

The wind noise is coming from the front doors.

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T001 WIND NOISE FROM THE FRONT DOORS

The wind noise is coming from the rear doors.

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T002 WIND NOISE FROM THE REAR DOORS

The wind noise is coming from the area of the bonnet.

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T005 BONNET WIND NOISE

The wind noise is coming from the area of the windscreen.

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T006 WINDSCREEN WIND NOISE

The wind noise is coming from the sun roof.

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T020 WIND NOISE FROM THE SUN ROOF

The wind noise is coming from the lower part of the passenger compartment.

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T010 PASSENGER COMPARTMENT WIND NOISE, LOWER AREA (UNDERBODY)

1

REAR LIGHTS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the rear lights.

In particular, make sure:

- The alignment of the lights with the wing or the tailgate is correct;

- The lights are correctly fitted

Move on to Step 2

Fitting of exterior rear lights faulty

Restore the correct fitting of the exterior rear lights

Op. 5540A56 EXTERIOR REAR LIGHT (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Internal rear light fitting faulty.

Restore the correct fitting of the internal rear light

Op. 5540A68 INTERIOR REAR LIGHT (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

2

REARSCREEN CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the rearscreen perimeter seal (no creases or wrinkles)

End of diagnosis

Perimeter seal not correctly fitted.

Restore and, if necessary, replace the perimeter seal, removing the window

Op. 7035B14 REAR WINDOW GLASS - R.R INCLUDES CLEANING HOUSING
