1080BE PARTICULATE FILTER SATURATION CHECK

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Turn the ignition ON with the engine off.

Turn off all the electrical users.

Display the dust saturation percentage parameter and ensure it is greater than 110%.

2

In the active diagnosis section, run diesel particulate filter Regeneration and ensure it is successful

Move on to Step 4

3

Check the fault disappears.

The test can be considered over

Move on to Step 4

4

Check there are no leaks or obstructions in the exhaust circuit, including the hoses

Repair or if necessary replace the faulty components

Op. 1080B EXHAUST EMISSION CONTROL SYSTEM

5

Check the precatalytic converter is not faulty

Replace the precatalytic converter

Op. 1080B40 CATALYTIC PRE-CONVERTER - R.R

6

Check the main catalytic converter is not faulty

Replace the catalytic converter with particulate trap

7

Check that the exhaust gas temperature sensor on the precatalytic converter is correctly connected

See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL

Restore the correct connections

8

Check that the exhaust gas temperature sensor on the precatalytic converter does not contain visually detectable faults.

Replace the temperature sensor on the precatalytic converter

Op. 1080B64 CATALYTIC CONVERTER EXCESS TEMPERATURE THERMOCOUPLE - R + R

9

Check that the exhaust gas temperature sensor on the main catalytic converter is correctly connected

See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL

Restore the correct connections

10

Check that the exhaust gas temperature sensor on the main catalytic converter does not contain visually detectable faults.

Replace the temperature sensor on the precatalytic converter

11

Check that the differential pressure sensor on the main catalytic converter is correctly connected

See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL

Restore the correct connections

12

Check that the differential pressure sensor on the main catalytic converter does not contain visually detectable faults.

Replace pressure sensor

13

Replace the catalytic converter with particulate trap
