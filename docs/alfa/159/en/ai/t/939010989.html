Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the rpm sensor.

Description

Connector

1

Rpm sensor

See K046 RPM SENSOR

2. Undo the bolt (2a) and remove the rpm sensor (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the rpm sensor in its housing and secure it using the bolt.

- Connect the electrical connection for the rpm sensor.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

This operation can only be carried out with the power unit removed, therefore refer to:Op. 1004B10 POWER UNIT WITH MANUAL GEARBOX - R.ROp. 1004D10 POWER UNIT (MANUAL GEARBOX), REMOVED, DISMANTLE AND REASSEMBLEOp. 1004D40 ENGINE - POSITION ON STAND AND REMOVEOp. 1004E10 ENGINE, REMOVED - REMOVE CYLINDER HEAD AND OIL SUMP FOR INSPECTION - INCLUDES POSITIONING ON STAND AND REMOVALOp. 1004E20 ENGINE - DISMANTLE AND REASSEMBLE FOLLOWING OPERATION 1004E10 - WASH AND CHECK DISMANTLED PARTS - REFIT CYLINDER HEAD AND OIL SUMP - DOES NOT INCLUDE REPAIRS TO CYLINDER HEAD AND AUXILIARY UNIT
