Op. 4410A12 FRONT SUSPENSION DAMPER (ONE), LH OR RH - FOR MCPHERSON STRUT SYSTEM - INCLUDES DISMANTLING/REASSEMBLY OF ASSEMBLY AT BENCH - R.R

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the front suspension track control arm (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Refit the front suspension track control arm (1a) and secure by tightening the new bolts (1b) to the specified torque.

The upper track control arms have DX or SX stamped on them which identifies the front suspension fitting side. The flexible bush with the metal edge should be facing the front of the vehicle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
