Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Raise the vehicle.

1. Undo rod connection nuts (1a) and (1b).

2. Remove the connecting rod.

3. If necessary, unscrew nuts (3a) and remove rod (3b) from the antiroll bar.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the rod is not damaged.

- If removed, position the rod mount on the anti-roll bar.

- Fit the rod in the housing.

- Tighten the rod connection nuts.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.

Reset the position of the headlamps using an Examiner or another diagnostic instrument.
