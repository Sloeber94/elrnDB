Removing

- Clean the residues of sealantfrom the mating surfaces between the engine block and the crankcase sump.

1. Ensure the locating bushes are present on the crankcase.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

- Apply a strip of grey silicone sealant to the contact surfaces between the crankcase sump and crankcase as shown in the diagram in the figure.

The sealant strip must be unbroken.Apply the sealant strip with the greatest accuracy.The tubular nozzle opening diameter must be 2.0 mm.Apply a sealant strip 2.0 - 2.5 mm thick to the engine oil intake fitting.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Place the crankcase sump in its housing.

2. Tighten bolts (2a) and (2b) securing the crankcase to the specified torque in the order shown in the figure.

When fitting the crankcase sump, take care not to damage the sealant strip.Finish the installation operation within 10 minutes of applying the sealant.
