Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

The gearbox fluid level only needs checking in the event of a lost external seal and repairs to the gearbox. Top up if necessary.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

1. Undo the oil filler plug.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Unscrew the gearbox oil drain plug and allow the oil to drain out into a suitable container.

The threaded plug (a) should not be used for draining and refilling the gearbox oil. It should not therefore be removed from its seat.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

- Clean the drain plug and the plug seat removing any residues of sealant.

- Apply sealant (LOCTITE 510 or the equivalent) to the drain plug thread and tighten in its seat to the recommended torque.
