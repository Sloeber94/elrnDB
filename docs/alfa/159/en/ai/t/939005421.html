0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Bleed the air and check the fault does not recur.

End of diagnosis

The fault recurs

Move on to Step 1

1

CHECK ON THE EFFICIENCY OF THE EXPANSION TANK CAP

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the efficiency of the expansion tank cap.

Move on to Step 2

Cap inefficient.

Replace the cap.

2

COOLING SYSTEM LEAKAGE TEST

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the seal of the cooling system

Op. 0510C30 ENGINE COOLING SYSTEM - LEAKAGE TEST

Move on to Step 3

There are engine cooling system leaks.

Proceed as instructed in the

See table X10 ENGINE COOLANT LEAKS

3

CHECK FOR GURGLING

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that no gurgling or oil is present in the coolant reservoir.

Move on to Step 4

Presence of fluid or air bubbles: head gasket damaged.

Replace the damaged gasket.

4

CHECK CYILNDER HEAD HYDRAULIC SEAL

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the hydraulic seal of the cylinder head.

Move on to Step 5

Seal not efficient.

(only for 3.2 V6 version)

Remove the cylinder head

Op. 1016C10 SINGLE CYLINDER HEAD - R.R. AND REPLACE GASKET

(only for 3.2 V6 version) Replace the head

Op. 1016D10 CYLINDER HEAD – R.R. AND REPLACE GASKETS

5

CHECK ENGINE MONOBLOC HYDRAULIC SEAL

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the hydraulic seal of the monobloc

Move on to Step 6

Seal not efficient.

Replace the monobloc

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present.

End of diagnosis

The fault persists.

Contact Technical Service for further information on the problem.
