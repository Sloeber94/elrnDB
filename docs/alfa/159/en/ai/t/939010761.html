Removing

Refitting

The following procedure describes left front brake caliper bracket removal/refitting, do the same for the right front brake caliper bracket.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the battery terminal (-).

Description

Connector

-

Battery

See A001 BATTERY

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL - R.R

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the brake pad wear sensor and release the wiring from the fastenings.

Description

Connector

1

Left brake pad wear sensor (switch)

See K020 LEFT BRAKE PAD WEAR SENSOR (SWITCH)

There is no brake pad wear sensor on the right front caliper.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Remove the brake caliper traction spring.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Remove the plugs (1a) and undo the brake caliper fixing bolts (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

- Move the brake caliper out of its housing without disconnect the flexible pipe and secure it appropriately.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the front brake caliper bracket (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods
