- On this car, it is not possible to remove/refit the pressure pump mount on the car.

To perform this operation, remove/refit the power unit

Op. 1004B10 POWER UNIT WITH MANUAL GEARBOX - R.R

and then work at the bench, customising the operation according to the engine service procedures.
