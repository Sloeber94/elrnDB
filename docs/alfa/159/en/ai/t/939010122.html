Whilst the steering is operated there is a noise when it reaches the end of travel position.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Visually inspect the level of the power assisted steering fluid in the reservoir. Conduct a test by steering completely to one side and then releasing the steering wheel

Move on to Step 1

1. Fluid level too low.

1. Restore the correct fluid level: if there are leaks, continue follow the instructions in

See table X030 POWER ASSISTED STEERING SYSTEM FLUID LEAKS

2. The noise increases towards the end of travel position and with the engine speed (roar)

2. Move on to Step 1

3. The noise remains constant (hissing whilst steering, puffing on release)

3. No intervention is nececssary: the noise is due to the by-pass valve that discharges the excess pressure

1

CHECK ON POWER ASSISTED STEERING FLUID QUALITY

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Visually inspect the power assisted steering fluid in the reservoir. Make sure that there is no emulsified fluid (fluid that is lighter in colour with bubbles)

Move on to Step 2

Emulsified fluid.

Eliminate any air infiltration from the power assisted steering system pipes

Op. 4130B10 POWER STEERING FLUID RESEVOIR - R.R.

2

POWER ASSISTED STEERING PIPES CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for constrictions in the flexible and rigid system pipes.

Move on to Step 3

Pipes faulty.

Replace the damaged parts, replacing all the gaskets. See Assembly

Op. 4130B POWER STEERING HYDRAULIC SYTEM

3

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION OF THE POWER STEERING PUMP

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the power assisted steering pump is working properly

End of diagnosis

Power assisted steering pump malfunction

Replace the power assisted steering pump

Op. 4130B24
