Noise, knocking whilst driving

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK ON MATCHING BETWEEN STRIKERS AND LOCK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the matching between the strikers and the lock (or lock mounting bracket on backrest frame).

Move on to Step 2

Interference between matching elements (striker not correctly positioned).

Adjust strikers and lubricate with Krytox grease.

2

CHECK ON STIKER SURFACE LAYER

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the superficial layer of the finishing treatment (bare metal on show) for the strikers and impressions/distortions on the lock fork.

End of diagnosis

1.Superficial layer of finish damaged (bare metal on show)

1.Replace the striker and lubricate with Krytox grease.

2. Impressions on lock attachment elements.

2. Replace the lock and lubricate with Krytox grease.
