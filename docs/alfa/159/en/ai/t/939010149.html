Applies to: all engines; all production periods

After rain or washing, water infiltration in the area of one of the front doors is noticed

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

DOOR RAIN GUTTER CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the door housing rain gutter.

In particular, check:

  • for dirt, leaves etc.;
  • that it is correctly fitted in the area of the corner (the seal should not cover the special air inlet opening)

Move on to Step 2

1. Dirt in the rain gutter.

1. Clean the rain gutter.

2. Rain gutter fitting faulty.

2. Refit the rain gutter correctly

Op. 7055A14 RAIN GUTTER ON DOOR HOUSING (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

2

DOOR HOUSING SEAL CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the door seal.

In particular, check:

  • for kinks in the seal;
  • the condition of the seal.

Move on to Step 3

Seal fitting faulty

Refit the seal correctly

Op. 7005A34 FRONT SIDE DOOR COMPARTMENT SEAL - R.R

3

DOOR ALIGNMENT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the fitting of the door.

In particular, check:

  • correct alignment of the door - housing;
  • seal adheres correctly to the door.

Move on to Step 4

Door fitting faulty.

Refit the door correctly

Op. 7005A14 FRONT SIDE DOOR - ADJUST CENTERING AND MOULDING - INCLUDES ADAPTATION OF HINGES AND SEALS ON DOOR COMPARTMENT

4

MOVING GLASS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the moving glass seal.

In particular, check:

  • glass closes correctly;
  • correct positioning of glass channel;
  • correct fitting of glass scraper.

Move on to Step 5

1. Channel fitting faulty.

1. Refit the channel correctly

Op. 7005D18 FRONT SIDE DOOR DESCENDING WINDOW GLASS CHANNEL (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

2. Glass fitting faulty.

2. Refit the window opening mechanism and the moving glass correctly

Op. 7005D15 FRONT SIDE DOOR DESCENDING WINDOW GLASS (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

3. Glass scraper fitting faulty.

3. Refit the glass scraper correctly

Op. 7005D26 FRONT SIDE DESCENDING WINDOW GLASS SCRAPER (OUTER) (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT- R.R

5

DOOR DRAINAGE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the drainage openings in the lower part of the door.

In particular, check: for dirt, leaves etc.

End of diagnosis

Dirt in the drains.

Clean the drains.

If necessary, restore the correct fitting.

Op. 7005A46 FRONT SIDE DOOR LOWER SEAL - R.R.
