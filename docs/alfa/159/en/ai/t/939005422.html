An engine coolant leak can be seen with consequent dripping and/or fouling of the engine.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

- Carry out an initial visual inspection to locate the component that is leaking or the area of the leak.

- Check that there is no engine oil in the coolant.

- Check that there is no water or emulsion in the engine oil.

- If the visual check is not sufficient, wash the engine, dry it and start slowly to bring it up to the correct temperature. Test the watertightness of the system to identify the leak

PIPE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the pipes and the seal. More specifically:

- the condition of the bands making sure they are correctly fitted;

- the condition of the hoses, connectors and plugs

Move on to Step 2

Tightening not correct or components damaged.

Restore the correct tightening or replace the components involved

2

RADIATOR CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the radiator

Move on to Step 3

Radiator damaged or distorted.

Replace the radiator

Op. 1088B20 ENGINE COOLING RADIATOR - R + R

3

EXPANSION TANK CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Visually check:

- the condition of the expansion tank;

- the cap seal. Check the condition of the cap breather valve.

Move on to Step 4

Tank damaged or distorted.

Replace the tank or the cap

4

WATER PUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Visually check:

- the condition of the water pump;

- the seal of the gaskets.

Move on to Step 5

Water pump faulty.

Replace the water pump or the gaskets

Op. 1088C10 WATER PUMP - R + R

5

CHECK THERMOSTAT UNIT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Visually check:

- the condition of the thermostat unit;

- the seal of the gaskets.

Move on to Step 6

Thermostat unit faulty.

Replace the thermostat unit or the gaskets

Op. 1088C30 THERMOSTAT UNIT - R+R

6

CYLINDER HEAD GASKET CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check:

- that the cylinder head is correctly tightened;

- the seal of the gasket.

Move on to Step 7

Cylinder head tightening incorrect

Restore correct tightening.

Gasket damaged.

Replace the gasket.

7

HEATER RADIATOR CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the heater radiator

Move on to Step 8

Heater radiator damaged or distorted.

Replace the heater radiator

Op. 5040C50 AIR CONDITIONING HEATER RADIATOR - R.R.

8

CHECK ENGINE BLOCK SURFACE

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Remove the head (only for 2.2 JTS version)

Op. 1016C10 SINGLE CYLINDER HEAD - R.R. AND REPLACE GASKET

(only for 3.2 V6 version)

Op. 1016D10 CYLINDER HEAD – R.R. AND REPLACE GASKETS

Check that there are no leaks:

- oil deposits on valves;

- defects on engine block;

- defects on cylinder head gasket.

Move on to Step 9

Oil deposits on the valves.

(only for 2.2 JTS version) Overhaul the head

Op. 1016E10 SINGLE CYLINDER HEAD, REMOVED - OVERHAUL

(only for 3.2 V6 version) Overhaul the head

Op. 1016E20 CYLINDER HEAD (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT, REMOVED - OVERHAUL

Cylinder block plane defective.

Level the engine block.
