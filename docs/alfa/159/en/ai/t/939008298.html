Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the engine running, there is noise at take off when the various gears are engaged: the noise changes as the speed changes.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

REDUCTION GEAR CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the reduction gear

More specifically:

- it should not be worn or damaged;

- there should not be excessive clearance;

- the ring gear fastenings should not be slack.

Move on to Step 2

Reduction gear damaged

Restore the clearance or, if necessary, replace the reduction gear

2

SATELLITE CARRIER PIN CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the wear of the satellite carrier pin or the housing in the differential casing

Op. 2110D20 DIFFERENTIAL INTERNAL CASING, REMOVED - DISMANTLING AND REASSEMBLY, CHECK PLANET AND SATELLITE GEARS

End of diagnosis

Differential casing wear

Replace the differential casing

Op. 2110D10 DIFFERENTIAL INTERNAL CASING - R + R WITH MANUAL GEARBOX REMOVED - INCLUDES GEARBOX DISMANTLING AND REASSEMBLY
