FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

The "boot open" signal is sent by the switch - located in the courtesy light G10 - to pin 35 of connector E of the Body Computer M1.

According to the control logic illustrated, the Body Computer M1 implements the boot release positive control function from pin 11 of connector F - via the line supplied by fuse F38 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2, pin 18 of connector D - controlling the operation of the boot lock geared motor N57.

The boot unlocking function, inside the Body Computer M1 pin 1 of connector F also has a dedicated earth connection: pin 20 of connector F.

The "boot not properly shut" signal is sent from the switch located in N57 to pin 24 of connector E.

the contact in question signals the "boot not properly shut" condition; the switch is fitted with another contact that signals the "boot open" condition, a contact used by the boot light functionSee E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - BOOT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT - DESCRIPTION

The fuel flap opening motor N58 is operated by the same control logic as the driver's front door lock, from pins 1 and 2 of connector D of M66. (HIGH version) or from pins 4 and 6 of connector D of M66 LOW version);

See E3040 CENTRAL LOCKING

Control unit M66 is supplied at pin 11 of connector D - by a line protected by fuse F32 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2 .

Pin 7 of connector D of M086 is earthed.
