The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P010F (air mass/flow meter inconsistency).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected if the engine speed is below 50 rpm and the difference between the supercharging pressure and the barometric pressure sensor is more than 0.15 bar for at least 10 sec. As a result, the control unit carries out the calculations with a replacement value of 1.013 bar, the maximum power is reduced and the supercharging pressure can no longer be controlled.
