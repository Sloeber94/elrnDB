The rigid pipe is connected to the ABS hydraulic control unit in the threaded opening marked "VR".

1. Undo the fixing nuts and move the engine coolant reservoir to one side.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

- Unscrew the brake-clutch fluid reservoir plug and fit the tool.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the brake-clutch minimum level sensor.
