Refitting

Removing

- Check that the pipe with the rapid connector (from the evaporator ) to the compressor and to the condenser is not damaged and that there are no cracks that could compromise the seal.

Replace the O rings for the connectors. Only use green coloured seals that are resistant to R134a coolant.Lubricate the connector threads using anti-freeze.

- Position the pipe with the rapid connector (from the evaporator) to the compressor and to the condenser.

- Connect the connector to the compressor and tighten the bolt to the recommended torque.
