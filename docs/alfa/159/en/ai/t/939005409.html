PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the following, in order:

- engine idle speed

- that the tyres are the correct type, in good condition and correctly inflated

- correct tightening of wheel bolts

Move on to Step 1

Engine idle irregular

Proceed as instructed by the

See table D010 ENGINE IDLE IRREGULAR

Tyre inflation pressure not correct

Restore the correct tyre inflation pressure

Tyres unsuitable or worn

Replace the tyres

Wheel bolts not correctly tightened

Tighten the wheel studs correctly

STEERING COLUMN CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the steering column support and that it is working properly. In particular, check:

- that there is no clearance between the support bushes and steering column

- that the steering column joints are correctly tightened

- the condition of the steering column supports and that they are working properly

Move on to Step 4

Bushes worn or damaged

Restore the efficiency of the steering column. If necessary, replace the bearings involved

Op. 4110A30 SUPPORT WITH STEERING CONTROL SHAFTS - R+R

Joint spider clearance too large

Tighten the bolts or replace the steering column

Op. 4110A30 SUPPORT WITH STEERING CONTROL SHAFTS - R+R

Bearings worn or damaged

Restore the efficiency of the steering column. If necessary, replace the bearings involved

CHECK ON CONDITION OF FLEXIBLE MOUNTINGS AND BUSHES

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the bushes and mountings. In the following order:

- ball joints for steering box rods

- bushes for upper and lower track control arms

- anti-roll bar flexible mountings

Move on to Step 5

Steering box rod ball joints worn or damaged

Replace the rods

Op. 4110B10 STEERING LINKS - R+R ON VEHICLE - CHECK TOE-IN/TOE-OUT SEPARATELY

Bushes for upper or lower arms worn or damaged

Replace the upper track control arms

Op. 4410B68 LOWER SWINGING ARM(ONE), LH OR RH OF FRONT SUSPENSION - R R -CHECK WHEEL ALIGNMENT SEPARATELY

or the lower track control arms

Op. 4410B40 LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM (WITH BALLJOINT) FOR LEFT OR RIGHT FRONT SUSPENSION (ONE) - R+R - CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY

Anti-roll bar mountings

Replace the flexible mountings for anti-roll bar

Op. 4410C12 FRONT SUSPENSION ANTI ROLL BAR JOINT (END) (ONE), LH OR RH - R.R
