Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the surround.

3. Release all the trim retainers.

4. Release the cover and upholstery (4a) from the structure (4b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Release the cover (1a) from the upholstery (1b) and keep the special fabric (1c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the head restraint cover is not damaged.

- Position the special fabric on the upholstery and fit the cover.

- Mould the cover onto the upholstery.

- Fit the cover on the upholstery on the structure.

- Attach all the trim retainers.

- Position the trim and secure it using the bolts.
