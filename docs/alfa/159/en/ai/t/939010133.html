CHECK THE GASKETS ARE CLEAN

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for foreign bodies on the sunroof seals.

Move on to Step 3

Foreign bodies present on the sunroof seals.

Clean the seals.

2

CHECK WHETHER SEAL SQUEAKING PERSISTS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check whether seal squeaking persists during sun-roof opening/closure

End of diagnosis

Noise persists.

Moisten the affected seal with Kritox GPL 205 grease.

3

CHECK FOR FOREIGN BODIES ON THE SUNROOF SEAL WORKING FLANGE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for paint spots/superficial defects/corrugation on the sunroof seal working flange.

End of diagnosis

Presence of defects in the paintwork/panel.

Remove the panel/paintwork defects.
