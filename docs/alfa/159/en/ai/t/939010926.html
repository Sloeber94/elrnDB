Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the electrical connections (1a, 1b).

Description

Component

LEFT AIR DISTRIBUTION ACTUATOR

See N113 LEFT AIR DISTRIBUTION ACTUATOR

RIGHT AIR DISTRIBUTION ACTUATOR

See N112 RIGHT AIR DISTRIBUTION ACTUATOR

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the upper vent unit (2b).

3. Remove the evaporator with the expansion valve.

4. If necessary, unscrew bolts (4a) and remove expansion valve (4b), then recover the gaskets.

Do not reuse the gaskets when refitting.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Replace any damaged gaskets.

- Check that the evaporator is intact and does not show signs of damage or deposits of dirt on the finned surface.

- If removed, refit the expansion valve with the new gaskets and secure it using the bolts.

- Fit the evaporator with the expansion valve.

- Position the upper vent unit and tighten the bolts.

- Connect the electrical connections.
