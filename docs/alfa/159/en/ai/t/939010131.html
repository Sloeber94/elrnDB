Applies to: all engines; all production periods

While driving with the roof fully open, noise can be heard from the sunroof area

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check whether the noise only occurs with the roof closed.

Move on to Step 1

1. The noise is heard with the roof open

1. Proceed as instructed in the diagnosis

See table S075 SUN ROOF NOISY / ROOF OPEN

2. The noise is heard with the roof open to the swivel position.

2. Proceed as instructed in the diagnosis

See table S077 SUN ROOF NOISY / WITH HINGED OPENING SUN ROOF

1

SUN ROOF FASTENING CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the correct fastening:

  • of the transparent roof panel to the slides;
  • of the full roof mechanism to the body

Move on to Step 2

Faulty fastenings.

Restore the fastening of the entire mechanism and roof

Op. 7030B12 SLIDING ROOF (ASSEMBLY) - R+R

2

CHECK SUN ROOF CONTROL DEVICES

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Remove the glass panel

Op. 7030B18 SLIDING ROOF GLASS PANEL - R+R

and check the operation of the sun-roof operating devices:

  • ensure the felt pads are present on the central crossmember;
  • ensure the inner trim drive springs are present and correctly secured;
  • ensure the teflon mount is present between slides and rails;
  • ensure the felt pads are present between spoiler and front channel.

End of diagnosis

1. Faulty fastenings.

1. Restore all the components and the fastening of the entire mechanism

Op. 7030B SLIDING ROOF

2. Faults in the sunroof operating devices.

2. Replace the affected parts

Op. 7030B SLIDING ROOF
