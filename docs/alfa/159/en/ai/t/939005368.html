CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Disconnect the high tension lead from the individual spark plugs and check that there is no microwelding at the terminal of the actual spark plugs

Replace the damaged spark plugs

Op. 5510D10 SPARK PLUGS - R+R

2

Remove the spark plugs from the engine head

Op. 5510D10 SPARK PLUGS - R+R

and check that there are no signs of fouling like those illustrated in the diagrams below

Move on to Step 6

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

3

Check that the spark plug earth electrodes are not bent or out of alignment as illustrated below

Move on to Step 6

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

4

Check that the spark plug ceramic insulator nozzle is not damaged as illustrated below

Move on to Step 6

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

5

Using a tester, check that the resistance between the connection terminal and the centre electrode (shown in the diagram below) for each spark plug is between 2 and 50 kOhm. The test is over

Replace the spark plugs outside the range

Op. 5510D10 SPARK PLUGS - R+R

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

6

Connect the diagnostic equipment and check that error P0300 is present together with at least one other error out of P0301, P0302, P0303, P0304

Replace the set of spark plugs

Op. 5510D10 SPARK PLUGS - R+R

7

Replace the spark plugs in the cylinders where the error was detected (e.g. for P0301 the spark plug for cylinder 1)

Op. 5510D10 SPARK PLUGS - R+R
