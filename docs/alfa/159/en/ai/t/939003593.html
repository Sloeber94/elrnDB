When the brake pedal is pressed, the travel is greater than normal

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

First of all check whether the problem is accompanied by poor braking action

Move on to Step 1

There is also poor braking action

Proceed as instructed in the diagnosis

See table F001 POOR BRAKING ACTION

1

OIL LEAKS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Visually inspect for fluid leaks in the braking system circuit.

Move on to Step 2

1. Fluid leak detected

1. Retighten connectors

Op. 3330A HYDRAULIC BRAKING SYSTEM TESTS AND CHECKS

2. Component defective

2. Replace the defective component

2

CHECK FOR FLUID LEAKS FROM BRAKE PUMP

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Disconnect the non-return valve connector from the brake servo and introduce a pipe cleaner in order to reach the lowest part of the brake servo chamber.

Check that there is no fluid in the chamber.

Move on to Step 3

Fluid in the brake servo chamber

Replace brake servo

Op. 3330D10

3

CHECK FOR AIR IN THE CIRCUIT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for any air in the braking circuit

Move on to Step 4

Air in the circuit

Bleed the air from the system

Op. 0010T65 HYDRAULIC BRAKE SYSTEM - BLEED AIR

4

REAR BRAKE CLEARANCE RECOVERY DEVICE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the rear brakes clearance recovery device is working efficiently.

End of diagnosis

Rear brake caliper faulty.

Replace the rear brake caliper involved

Op. 3310B36 REAR BRAKE CALIPER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R
