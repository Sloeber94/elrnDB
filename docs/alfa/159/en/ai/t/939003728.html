Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The seat belt tongue will not attach to the buckle

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

On the rear seat belts, the central seat belt tongue can only be attached to the corresponding central buckle. Attachment cannot take place if the two parts do not correspond.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0
