1

CHECK ON CORRECT ATTACHMENT OF PULLMAFLEX PLATE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the pullmaflex plate is correctly attached to the frame.

Move on to Step 2

Pullmaflex plate partly attached.

Attach the Pullmaflex plate correctly (check the condition of the female screws).

2

CHECK ON CORRECT ATTACHMENT OF REGULATOR CABLES

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the regulator cables are correctly attached to the pullmaflex plate.

Move on to Step 3

1. Cable detached from plate

1. Attach the cable in the special housing.

2. Cable broken

2. Replace the lumbar adjustment assembly

CHECK ON REGULATOR CASING SEAL

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the regulator casing seal and make sure there is no slackness.

Move on to Step 4

Bolts securing regulator casing to frame not correctly tightened.

Tighten the fixing bolt correctly or replace it.

ACTION

Check the condition of the lumbar regulator assembly.
