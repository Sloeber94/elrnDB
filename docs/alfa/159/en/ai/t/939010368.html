0010T12 ENGINE OIL AND FILTER - REPLACE

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Engine oil is harmful to skin: keep contact with skin to a minimum and if there is contact wash with soap and water.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Remove the filler cap with the engine hot.

- Release the engine oil dipstick.

- Remove the engine oil cover using a suitable tool.

- Remove the seal.

- Extract the engine oil filter cartridge.

Collect the engine oil that comes out in a suitable container.

- Undo the drain plug in the crankcase sump and leave the oil to drain into a suitable container.

Be very careful when removing the drain plug, the oil could be very hot.

Do not let the oil disperse into the environment as indiscriminate dispersion of this product causes pollution.

- Clean the drain plug and tighten it complete with a new gasket to the recommended torque.
