Op. 1056F61 LEFT CYLINDER HEAD FUEL MANIFOLD PIPE - R.R.

- Cut the adhesive tape securing the right cylinder head injector wiring to the fuel manifold.

- Remove the right cylinder head fuel manifold pipe from its housing.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Lubricate the O-rings on the injectors with engine oil.

- Fit any retaining clips on the injectors that may have been removed during the removal of the fuel distribution manifold.

- Fit the fuel distribution manifold for the right head on the injectors.

Take great care not to damage the O-rings for the injectors during the fitting of the fuel distribution manifold.

- Secure the injectors to the right cylinder head fuel distribution manifold using the retaining clips.
