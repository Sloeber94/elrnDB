Removing

Refitting

1. Extract the cigar lighter.

2. Gently extract the cigar lighter seat (2a) using the special tool (2b).

Extracting cigar lighter housing

3. Disconnect the electrical connection.

ADDITIONAL CURRENT SOCKET

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. If necessary, remove the ring nut (1a) by undoing the retainer (1b) from the seat (1c).

2. Extract the bulb holder and bulb.

3. Remove the bulb from the bulb holder (press-fitted).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the cigar lighter seat, the bulb and the bulb holder are not damaged.

- Refit the bulb and the bulb holder.

- Position the locknut and attach the retainer in its seat.

- Connect the electrical connection.

- Fit the cigar lighter seat.

- Check that the current socket is working.

- Fit the cigar lighter.
