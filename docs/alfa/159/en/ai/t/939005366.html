CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Connect the diagnostic equipment and check that none of the following errors is present: P0300, P0301, P0302, P0303, P0304, P0305, P0306

Continue as described by the diagnostic equipment

2

Start up the engine and let it warm up. Keep the engine at a speed of 2000 rpm and check that there is no misfire.

The test can be considered over

Move on to Step 3

3

Check that the errors have been detected at all the cylinders in one bank (P0301, P0302 and P0303 or P0304, P0305 and P0306 respectively).

Move on to Step 10

Move on to Step 4

4

Press the START/STOP button (engine off).

Disconnect the corresponding cylinder coil.

Press the START/STOP button (panel on).

Measure the voltage between Pin 2 of the coil

See A030 IGNITION COIL

and the Earth, checking that it corresponds to the battery voltage

Move on to Step 8

5

Measure the voltage between Pins 2 and 3 of the coil

See A030 IGNITION COIL

checking that it corresponds to the battery voltage

Restore or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Move on to Step 11

6

Measure the voltage between Pins 2 and 4 of the coil

See A030 IGNITION COIL

checking that it corresponds to the battery voltage

Restore or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Move on to Step 11

7

If faults are present, repair or if necessary replace the ignition coil electrical wiring.

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Move on to Step 11

Replace the faulty ignition coil

Op. 5510C14 IGNITION COIL/S - R.R.

Move on to Step 11

8

Check that none of the components connected to the same line as the coil has faults in the electrical connections such as short circuits or poor insulation

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Restore or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Move on to Step 11

9

Replace the coil protective fuse

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Move on to Step 11

10

If faults are present, repair or if necessary replace the ignition coil electrical wiring.

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

11

Reconnect all the electrical components disconnected previously. Using the diagnostic equipment, delete the errors in the engine management control unit. Start the engine and check that there are no longer any errors

Continue as described by the diagnostic equipment
