Removing

Refitting

1. Fit the differential internal casing in a vice and undo the bolts (1a) fixing the ring gear to the differential internal casing using a suitable tool.

2. Remove the bearings (2b) using a suitable extractor (2a).

3. Fit the differential internal casing on a suitable gauge and separate the ring gear from the differential internal casing.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the differential ring gear in the differential internal casing using a rubber hammer and taking care to align the openings for the fixing bolts.

- Fit the ring gear in the differential internal casing using new bolts to be tightened to the recommended torque.
