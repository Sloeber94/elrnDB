1. Undo the bolt fixing the end of the front seat belt on the seat.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Release the door housing seals in the area of the upper pillar cover.

2. Release the bolt covers.

3. Undo the bolts.

4. Keep the upper pillar cover (4a), releasing it from the seat belt (4b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the upper pillar cover is not damaged.

- Place the cover and the seat belt in position.

- Tighten the bolts.

- Fit the bolt covers.

- Fit the door housing seals.

- Position the end of the front seat belt on the front seat and tighten the bolt to the recommended torque.
