Detachment, release of backrest adjustment knob

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK ON CONFORMITY OF RETAINING ELEMENTS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the conformity of the retaining elements on the inside of the knob.

Move on to Step 2

Retaining elements damaged.

Replace the knob

Op. 7045A24 FRONT SEAT BACKREST ANGLE ADJUSTMENT KNOB (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

2

CHECK ON CONFORMITY OF HANDLES

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the conformity of the handles at the end of the joint connecting rod.

Move on to Step 3

Handles damaged

Replace the insulating rod (releasing the retaining washer).

3

CHECK ON JOINT CONNECTING ROD SEAL

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the seal of the joint connecting rod (square rod)

Move on to Step 4

Connecting rod released from seat

Fit the rod retaining washer and check the seal of the rod to traction.

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present.

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace the seat frame

Op. 7045A86 FRONT SEAT FRAME (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT, R.R.
