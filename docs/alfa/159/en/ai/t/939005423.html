An engine oil leak can be seen with consequent dripping and/or fouling of the engine

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Carry out an initial visual inspection to locate the component that is leaking or the area of the leak.

If the visual inspection was not enough, wash the engine, dry it and spread talc on the parts involved: carry out a road test to identify the leak.

Leak from the sump.

Move on to Step 3.

Move on to Step 4.

DRAIN PLUG CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition and seal of the oil drain plug.

Move on to Step 2

Drain plug not intact.

Replace the drain plug

Op. 1020A30 ENGINE OIL DRAIN PLUG - REPLACE

Exhaust cap tightening not correct.

Restore the correct exhaust cap tightening.

2

OIL SUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check:

- the condition of the oil sump;

- the seal of the sealant between the sump and cylinder block/crankcase;

- that the sump fixing bolts are correctly tightened.

Move on to Step 3

Oil sump damaged or distorted.

Replace the sump

Op. 1020A20 CRANKCASE OIL SUMP - R.R CLEAN SURFACES FOR SEALANT APPLICATION

Incorrect tightening of retaining bolts.

Restore the correct tightening of the retaining bolts.

3

OIL FILTER CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check:

- the condition of the oil filter;

- the seal and condition of the seal;

- the condition of the water-oil heat exchanger

Move on to Step 4

Oil filter damaged or distorted.

Replace the oil filter and seal

Oil leaks from the heat exchanger.

Replace the heat exchanger

Op. 1084C10 ENGINE OIL HEAT EXCHANGER - R + R

4

ENGINE GASKET CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Access the crankshaft and camshaft oil seals. Check the seal of these gaskets.

End of diagnosis

Crankshaft seals damaged.

Replace the seals concerned.

Camshaft seals damaged

Replace the seals concerned.
