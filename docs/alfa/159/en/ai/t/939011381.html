Removing

Refitting

Op. 7050A20 ONE-PIECE REAR SEAT CUSHION - R.R.

1. Undo the nut for the right or left rod involved.

2. Remove the right mounting rod (2a) and separate it from the centre mounting rod (2b), or remove the left mounting rod (2c) and separate it from the end of the middle seat belt (2d).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the mounting rod is not damaged.

1. Connect the right mounting rod (1a) to the centre mounting rod (1b), or the left mounting rod (1c) to the end of the middle seat belt (1d), the place it in position.

2. Screw in the nut and tighten it to the recommended torque.
