I061 DIPPED HEADLAMPS DO NOT LIGHT UP AS THEY SHOULD

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Both the dipped headlamps do not light up at the correct distance but point upwards or downwards

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK ON INSTALLATION OF ALIGNMENT SENSORS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the front and rear alignment sensors and make sure they are correctly installed.

Make sure that the fasteing of the bracket and the connecting rod is correct for both the front and rear sensors

Move on to Step 2

1. Mechanical installation of front sensor (brackets, connecting rods) incorrect

1. Refit front sensor correctly

Op. 5540C42 SENSOR FOR CORRECTING/ALIGNING GAS DISCHARGE LIGHTING UNITS ON FRONT SUSPENSION - R R

2. Mechanical installation of rear sensor (brackets, connecting rods) incorrect

2. Refit rear sensor correctly

Op. 5540C46 SENSOR FOR CORRECTING/ALIGNING GAS DISCHARGE LIGHTING UNITS ON REAR SUSPENSION - R.R.

2

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that there are no errors by connecting to the master headlamp (left side) control unit.

Move on to Step 3

1. Presence of error detected

B1020 - Front sensor signal

1. Move on to Step 3

2. Presence of error detected

B1021 - Rear sensor signal

2. Move on to Step 3

3. Presence of error detected

B1022 - Sensor power supply

3. Move on to Step 3

4. Presence of error detected

B1040 - Headlamp alignment corrector

4. Move on to Step 5

3

CHECK ON ALIGNMENT SENSOR SUPPLY

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the attitude sensors are receiving a correct supply by carrying out the test

See Test 5540CA HEADLAMP ALIGNMENT SENSOR CHECK

Move on to Step 4

4

CHECK ON CONNECTIONS INSIDE THE HEADLAMPS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the connections inside the headlamps for the vertical adjustment motors are correctly connected

See E3080 HEADLAMP AGLINMENT CORRECTOR

Move on to Step 5

1. Connector for right headlamp motor not correctly fitted

1. Fit connector for right headlamp

2. Connector for left headlamp motor not correctly fitted

2. Fit connector for left headlamp

5

CHECK ON OPERATION OF HEADLAMP ALIGNMENT ACTUATORS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Replace the left headlamps

Op. 5540B81 LEFT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

and right

Op. 5540B82 RIGHT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

and check that the motors are working properly

End of diagnosis

1. Right headlamp alignment corrector not working

1. Move on to Step 6

2. Left headlamp alignment corrector not working

2. Move on to Step 6

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Refit the headlamp removed previously and replace the ballast control unit

Op. 5540B42

End of diagnosis
