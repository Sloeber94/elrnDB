The customer complains of poor driving comfort especially noticeable at the front.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

ROAD TEST TO EVALUATE THE DRIVING COMFORT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Carry out a road test to evaluate the driving comfort of the front suspension

See Test 44A ROAD TEST FOR COMFORT EVALUATION

End of diagnosis

Subjective judgement compared with the reference vehicle 6

Acceptable behaviour of the vehicle

Subjective judgement compared with the reference vehicle 5.5

Move on to Step 2

Subjective judgement compared with the reference vehicle 3-4

Move on to Step 4

2

END OF TRAVEL BUFFER CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Raise the vehicle on a two column lift and check that the end of travel buffer is correctly inserted in its housing carrying out the

See Test 4410AB CHECKING RUBBER BLOCK AND BEARING ON FRONT SHOCK ABSORBER

Move on to Step 3

End of travel buffer not correctly inserted

Refit the buffer correctly

3

UPPER AND LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM BUSHES CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the upper and lower track control arm bushes

The guard under the engine must be removed in order to check the condition of the lower track control arms

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

Move on to Step 4

Upper track control arm bushes deteriorated

Replace the upper track control arm

Op. 4410B68 LOWER SWINGING ARM(ONE), LH OR RH OF FRONT SUSPENSION - R R -CHECK WHEEL ALIGNMENT SEPARATELY

Lower track control arm bushes deteriorated

Replace the lower track control arm

Op. 4410B52 FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM REAR BUSH LEFT OR RIGHT - REPLACE AT BENCH

4

SHOCK ABSORBER EFFICIENCY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the efficiency of the shock absorbers by carrying out manual compression manoeuvres (resting on the bonnet by the centre of the wheel), which should be carried out on both sides of the vehicle.

Move on to Step 5

Sudden return without damping

Replace the shock absorber that is not working properly

Op. 4410A12 FRONT SUSPENSION DAMPER (ONE), LH OR RH - FOR MCPHERSON STRUT SYSTEM - INCLUDES DISMANTLING/REASSEMBLY OF ASSEMBLY AT BENCH - R.R

5

VEHICLE GEOMETRY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

After replacing the shock absorbers, check the geometry

End of diagnosis

Geometry values do not conform

Restore the geometry to the correct values

Op. 4450A10 FRONT WHEEL GEOMETRY - CHECK USING OPTICAL EQUIPMENT - INCLUDES ADJUSTMENT IF NECESSARY
