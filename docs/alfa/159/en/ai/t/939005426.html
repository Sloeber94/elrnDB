PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check whether the problem is accompanied by a hardening of the steering or noise. Visually inspect the level of the power assisted steering fluid in the reservoir.

Move on to Step 1

Hardening of the steering detected.

Restore the correct level and carry out a road test. If the fault persists, move on to Step 1.

Power assisted steering fluid level too low.

Continue following the

See table H010 STEERING HARD / (STEERING IN BOTH DIRECTIONS)

1

RESERVOIR LEAKS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Visually check that there are no fluid leaks from the power assisted steering fluid reservoir.

Move on to Step 2

Leaks from the power assisted steering fluid reservoir.

Replace the power steering fluid reservoir

Op. 4130B10 POWER STEERING FLUID RESEVOIR - R.R.

2

PIPE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Visually check, using talc, that there are no leaks from the connectors for all the rigid pipes and hoses for the power assisted steering system.

Move on to Step 3

Leaks from pipes.

Tighten the connectors or replace the damaged parts, replacing all the seals.

3

STEERING BOX CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Visually check that there are no fluid leaks from:

- the rigid pipes on the steering box and the attachments;

- the lower steering box plug;

- the steering column mounting;

- the steering box itself.

Move on to Step 4

Leaks from the rigid pipes

Tighten the connectors or replace the steering box

Op. 4130A15 POWER STEERING BOX R R WITH FRONT SUSPENSION FRAME REMOVED

Leaks from the steering column mounting or the actual steering box.

Replace the steering box

Op. 4130A15 POWER STEERING BOX R R WITH FRONT SUSPENSION FRAME REMOVED

Replace the steering box

Op. 4130A15 POWER STEERING BOX R R WITH FRONT SUSPENSION FRAME REMOVED
