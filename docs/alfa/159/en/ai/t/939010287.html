5520CA GLOW PLUGS FUNCTIONAL TEST

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check that the glow plugs operate correctly

Replace the faulty glow plugs

Op. 5520C10 HEATER PLUGS - R+R

2

Press the START/STOP button and turn off the engine.

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from the glow plug control unit

See M015 PLUG PREHEATING CONTROL UNIT

Measure the voltage between Pin 3 of control unit wiring side connector A and Earth, ensuring it is greater than 11 V.

See M015 PLUG PREHEATING CONTROL UNIT

Move on to Step 6

Move on to Step 3

3

In the engine bay junction unti, remove fuse F11 from the block (third from the right in the lower row).

See B001 JUNCTION UNIT

Measure the voltage between the fuse input contact and earth, checking it is less than 11 V

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the junction unit in the engine compartment and the glow plug control unit.

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

4

Restore the connections or, if necessary, replace the junction unit in the engine compartment

Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.

The test may be considered completed

5

Measure the voltage between Pins 1 and 3 of control unit wiring side connector A and Earth, ensuring it is greater than 11 V.

See M015 PLUG PREHEATING CONTROL UNIT

Restore the earth connection of the glow plug control unit or if necessary replace the electrical wiring

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

6

Press the START/STOP button.

Measure the voltage between Pin 1 of control unit wiring side connector A and Earth, ensuring it is greater than 11 V.

See M015 PLUG PREHEATING CONTROL UNIT

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the junction unit in the engine compartment and the glow plug control unit.

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

7

Select the following control unit at the diagnostic equipment: EDC16C39 Diesel electronic injection control unit.

Carry out the active diagnosis for the preheating relay, checking that the result is positive.

Move on to Step 10

Move on to Step 9

8

If faults are present, repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the glow plug preheating and the engine control unit.

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

The test can be considered over

Replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R

9

Connect a shunt cable with fuse between pin 1 of control unit connector A and earth.

See M015 PLUG PREHEATING CONTROL UNIT

Carry out active diagnosis of the preheating relay to check whether it is successful or not

Move on to Step 8

10

Press the START/STOP button.

Disconnect the shunt connection.

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from engine control unit connector A

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

Press the START/STOP button.

Measure the voltage between Pin 2 of control unit wiring side connector A and Earth, ensuring it is less than 0.3 V.

See M015 PLUG PREHEATING CONTROL UNIT

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine compartment control unit and the glow plug control unit.

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

11

Press the START/STOP button.

Measure the resistance between Pin 2 of control unit wiring side connector A and Earth, ensuring it is greater than 500 V.

See M015 PLUG PREHEATING CONTROL UNIT

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine compartment control unit and the glow plug control unit.

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

12

Measure the resistance between Pin 2 of control unit connector A and Pin 93 of control unit connector A to ensure it is less than 5 Ohms

See M015 PLUG PREHEATING CONTROL UNIT

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine compartment control unit and the glow plug control unit.

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

13

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from the first two glow plugs A and B.

See A040 HEATER PLUGS

Turn the ignition key ON.

Measure the voltage between Pins 1 and 2 of the control unit and Earth, ensuring it is less than 0.3 V.

See M015 PLUG PREHEATING CONTROL UNIT

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the glow plug control unt and the glow plugs

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

14

Press the START/STOP button.

Measure the resistance between Pins 1 and 2 of the control unit and glow plugs A and B, ensuring it is less than 5 Ohms.

See M015 PLUG PREHEATING CONTROL UNIT

See A040 HEATER PLUGS

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the glow plug control unt and the glow plugs

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

16

Replace the glow plug control unti and ensure the fault disappears.

Op. 5520C20 HEATER PLUGS CONTROL UNIT - R.R

The test can be considered over

Move on to Step 16

16

Repair the original glow plug control unit.

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from the other two glow plugs C and D.

See A040 HEATER PLUGS

Press the START/STOP button.

Measure the voltage between Pins 3 and 4 of the control unit and Earth, ensuring it is less than 0.3 V.

See M015 PLUG PREHEATING CONTROL UNIT

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the glow plug control unt and the glow plugs

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

17

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Measure the resistance between Pins 3 and 4 of the control unit and glow plugs C and D, ensuring it is less than 5 Ohms.

See M015 PLUG PREHEATING CONTROL UNIT

See A040 HEATER PLUGS

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the glow plug control unt and the glow plugs

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

18

Replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R
