Refitting

Removing

- Push the brake caliper piston into its housing using a suitable tool.

The level of the brake fluid in the reservoir will increase; if necessary, draw off any excess fluid using a suitable tool.

- Grease the brake caliper and bracket in the areas of the pad support surfaces.

- Place the front brake pads in their housings.

- Place the brake caliper in its housing.

1. Tighten the bolts (1a) fixing the brake caliper to the recommended torque and fit the plugs (1b).

Op. 4450B12 FRONT WHEELS (TWO) - R+R

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the brake pad wear cable.

Description

Connector

1

Left brake pad wear sensor (switch)

See K020 LEFT BRAKE PAD WEAR SENSOR (SWITCH)

There is no brake pad wear sensor on the right front brake caliper.

2. Take out the pins (2a) and remove the clip (2b).

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Remove the front brake pads.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Compress the pistons for the front brake calipers.

The level of the brake fluid in the reservoir will increase; if necessary, draw off any excess fluid using a suitable tool.

- Place the front brake pads in their housings.

- Fit the clip in position and secure it using the pins.

- Connect the electrical connection for the brake pad wear cable.

Op. 4450B12 FRONT WHEELS (TWO) - R+R

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
