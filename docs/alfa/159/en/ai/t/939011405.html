7210E52 R.R. REAR WING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Check that the components connected are not distorted by checking the bodyshell alignment measurements using suitable equipment (gauges, templates or jigs).

- Straighten the body as necessary.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Use a hack sawing machine following the cutting lines shown in the diagram.

- Use a rotary brush to remove the paint to gain access to the spot welds.

2. Use the chamfering machine to remove the spot welds shown in the diagram.

- Use the drill and chamfer the spot welds not accessible with the chamfering machine.

- Use the hammer and chisel and remove the spot welds chamfered previously.

3. Remove the part from the vehicle separating the edges joined by heat expanding sealant.

- Remove the offcuts from the vehicle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Straighten the edges of the vehicle using a mallet and profiled block.

- Remove the spot weld residues using a disc grinder.

- Use the rotary brush and remove the heat expanding sealant residues on the vehicle.

- Use a rotary brush to clean the areas treated previously.

1. Apply electro-galvanizing paint.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Work at the bench and cut the replacement wing, at the points shown in the diagram, making sure that a section of the panel remains for overlapping.

2. Remove the anti-corrosion treatment from the entire perimeter of the inside and outside of the replacement part using a rotary brush.

3. Drill the part at the points shown using the drill.

- Apply electro-weldable paint to the edges involved.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Position the replacement part correctly on the vehicle and secure it using self-locking clamps.

2. Fit the door housing seal, striker and close the door temporarily.

- Close the boot lid temporarily.

- Check the alignment and the surrounding gaps.

3. Use the circular saw to cut the edges of the panel so that the join line is perfect.

- Remove the replacement part, the excess panels and the components used for the adjustment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Apply heat expanding sealant to the splining illustrated in the diagram.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Position the replacement part correctly on the vehicle and secure it using self-locking clamps.

- Check the alignment and the surrounding gaps.

2. Use a spot welder in the areas shown in the diagram.

3. Use a MIG welder and weld the openings made previously in the replacement part.

4. Use a MIG welder to seam weld in the join areas.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Correct any distortions to the panel using a hammer and dolly block.

1. Use a disc grinder to level the welds.

- Use a rotary brush to clean the areas welded previously.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Apply anti-oxidant protection to the areas involved in the welding.

- Seal the join lines between the replacement part and the vehicle.

- Apply the wax based oil protection to the inside of the rear wing

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Removing

Refitting

- Check that connected components are not distorted by checking the bodyshell alignment measurements using suitable equipment (gauges, templates or jigs).

- Straighten the body as necessary.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

1. Use a jigsaw following the cutting lines in the diagram.

- Use a rotary brush to remove the paint and gain access to the spot welds.

2. Use the chamfering machine to remove the spot welds shown in the diagram.

- Use the drill to chamfer the spot welds that aren't accessible to the chamfering machine.

- Use a hammer and chisel to remove the previously chamfered spot welds.

3. Remove the part from the vehicle.

4. Remove the edges joined with heat expanding sealant.

5. Remove the edges joined with sealant.

- Remove the offcuts from the vehicle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

- Straighten the edges of the vehicle using a hammer and dolly block.

- Remove the spot weld residues using a disc grinder.

1. Use the rotary brush to remove sealant residues from the vehicle.

2. Use the rotary brush to remove heat expandable sealant residues from the vehicle.

- Use a rotary brush to clean the previously treated areas.

3. Apply electro-galvanizing paint.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

Refitting

Removing

1. Work at the bench and cut the replacement wing at the points shown in the diagram, making sure that a section of the panel remains for overlapping.

2. Remove the anti-corrosion treatment from the entire perimeter of the inside and outside of the replacement part using a rotary brush.

3. Drill out the part at the indicated points.

- Apply electro-weldable paint to the relevant edges.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

1. Position the replacement part correctly on the vehicle and secure it using self-locking clamps.

2. Fit the rear door housing seal and striker, and close the door temporarily.

- Close the boot lid temporarily.

- Check the alignment and the surrounding gaps.

3. Use the circular saw to cut the edges of the panel so that the join line is perfect.

- Remove the replacement part, the excess panels and the components used for the adjustment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

1. Apply heat expanding sealant to the splining illustrated in the diagram.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

1. Apply sealant to the end of the fuel inlet base, as shown in the diagram.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

1. Position the replacement part correctly on the vehicle and secure it using self-locking clamps.

- Check the alignment and the surrounding gaps.

2. Use a spot welder to weld the areas shown in the diagram.

3. Use a MIG welder to fill weld the openings made previously.

4. Use a MIG welder to seam weld along the join areas.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

- Correct any distortions to the panel using a mallet and dolly block.

1. Use a disc grinder to level the welds.

- Use a rotary brush to clean the previously welded areas.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Station Wagon

1. Apply anti-oxidant protection to the areas involved in the welding.

- Seal the join lines between the replacement part and the vehicle.

- Proceed with the painting stage.

- Apply the wax based oil protection to the inside of the rear wing.
