Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The seat cushion and front backrest heater pad is not working / not working properly.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

SEAT CONNECTION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the connection between the seat connection and the floor connection

Move on to Step 2

Heater pad supply connector detached from the connection on the vehicle floor.

Fit the seat heater pad connector correctly with the vehicle floor connector.

2

HEATER PAD WIRING CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for any breaks/tears in the heater pad wiring

Move on to Step 3

1. Presence of cuts/tears in the heater pad supply leads.

1. Replacement of cushion heater pad

Op. 7045M22 HEATER PAD FOR FRONT SEAT CUSHION, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

2. Backrest heater pad resistance broken.

2. Replacement of backrest heater pad

Op. 7045M26 L/H OR R/H FRONT SEAT SQUAB HEAT PAD - R+R FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL WIRING CHECK

3

CHECK ON CONDITION OF HEATER PAD ON SWITCH

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition/operation of the heater pad on switch and temperature adjustment (3 positions).

Move on to Step 4

Switch rockers broken, temperature position switch broken, oxidation of button/switch contacts.

Replacement of heater pad on button/position switch

Op. 7045M10 FRONT SEAT HEATING SYSTEM SWITCH, LEFT OR RIGHT - R+R

4

CHECK ON ATTACHMENT OF HEATER PAD CONNECTION WITH TEMPERATURE ADJUSTMENT CONTROL UNIT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the heater pad connection is correctly attached to the temperature adjustment control unit.

Move on to Step 5

Heater pad wiring connector detached from adjustment control unit.

Fit the heater pad wiring connector correctly in the control unit.

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present.

End of diagnosis

The fault persists.

Replace the heater pad control unit

Op. 7045M32 ELECTRONIC THERMOSTAT FOR HEATING CIRCUIT OF ONE FRONT SEAT, LH OR RH R R
