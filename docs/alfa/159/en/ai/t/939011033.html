Removing

Refitting

- Open the bonnet.

1. Remove the cover (bayonet mount).

2. Disconnect the electrical connection for the bulb.

3. Release the bulb retaining clips.

4. Extract the bulb from the bulb holder.

If the bulb is working properly, avoid touching it with your hands. If this does happen, clean the bulb with alcohol.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

- Check the condition of the bulb and make sure that it is clean.

Avoid touching the bulb with your hands. If this does happen, clean the bulb with alcohol.

1. Fit the bulb in the bulb holder. The correct position for the bulb is illustrated.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
