N.B.: The command to switch on the handbrake warning light is sent by the switch on the lever to the Body Computer Node which makes it available on the B-CAN to the Instrument Panel Node.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

PRELIMINARY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the warning light comes on during the check when the panel is switched on

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment and check at the Instrument Panel Node that the handbrake warning light is working through active diagnosis

The warning light does not flash

Replace instrument panel

Op. 5560B10 CONTROL PANEL - R+R

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the continuity of the wiring between the handbrake lever switch and the Body Computer

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Move on to Step 3

Wiring broken

Restore the correct wiring

3

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Switch defective
