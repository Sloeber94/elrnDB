The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0088 (Pressure at pipe)

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected if the pressure of the oil in the distribution line is greater than 1.750 bar. As a result, the engine is switched off.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

0

Preliminary checks

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

The fuel system is highly sensitive. The work must be carried out with the greatest cleanliness and care. Any air in the system could damage the high pressure fuel pump. The vehicle must therefore never be driven with the fuel tank empty.

Check that the tank contains the correct type of fuel. The fuel reserve should be more than 5 litres. Check that there are no leaks from the fuel system. Check the hoses and pipes connected to identify any obstructions, damage, etc. Check that the connectors and seals are correctly fitted.

Move on to Step 1

1.The tank contains irregular substances (petrol, other fluids)

1.Clean the tank and refill it correctly

2.There are less than 5 litres in the tank

2.Top up the fuel level to more than 5 litres

3.Fuel system leaking or not correctly sealed

3.Renew the seal for the system. If necessary, replace the faulty components

4.Pipes obstructed or damaged

4.Renew the pipes. If necessary, replace the faulty components
