Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts and remove the flange with the seal (1b).

2. Undo the bolts and remove the differential internal casing support (2a), the scraper ring (2b) and the seal (2c).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

- Clean the contact surfaces between the differential internal casing support and the clutch casing thoroughly.

1. Remove the differential internal casing from its housing complete with bearing outer races.

2. Remove the lubrication oil ducts from the housings for the two layshafts.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the lubrication oil ducts in the housings for the two layshafts.

- Apply the LOCTITE 510 sealant to the entire matching surface between the differential internal casing housing and the clutch casing.

- Fit the differential casing internal housing and support tightening the bolts to the recommended torque. The bolts should be tightened in three stages as described below: tighten the bolts to 0.5 daNm; tighten the bolts to 50% of the final recommended value; tighten the bolts to 100% of the final recommended value.
