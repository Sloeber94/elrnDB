- Fasten the electrical wiring for the right rear Lambda sensor to the catalytic converter with the clip on the heat shield.

- Connect the electrical connection for the right rear Lambda sensor to the catalytic converter.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Position a hydraulic jack underneath the front silencer.

- Disconnect the front and rear silencers from the flexible mountings and then lower them using the hydraulic jack.

- Undo the fastenings and remove the protection for the rear Lambda sensor connections.
