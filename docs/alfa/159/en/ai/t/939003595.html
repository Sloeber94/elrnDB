Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the brake pedal is pressed there is a hardening in the pedal travel

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic test equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the tester input.

Check that there are no faults in the ABS control unit.

Move on to Step 2

1. Error detected in the control unit

1. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2. No communication with the control unit.

2. Restore:

  • Connection between the ABS control unit and the diagnostic connector
  • ABS control unit supply

See E7020 ABS

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) and carry out the active diagnosis for:

  • Pump motor;
  • Solenoid valves.

Move on to Step 3

Fault detected in the pump or in a solenoid valve.

Replace the electronic control unit electrohydraulic unit

Op. 3340A12 ABS HYDRAULIC AND ELECTRONIC CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

3

VACUUM PIPE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the vacuum pipe is correctly fitted/in good condition.

Move on to Step 4

1. Pipe not fitted correctly or bent

1. Refit correctly

2. Pipe damaged.

2. Replace vacuum pipe

Op. 3330D24

4

BRAKE SERVO SINGLE-ACTING VALVE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the brake servo single-acting valve is working properly.

Move on to Step 5

Single-acting valve not working properly.

Clean the one-way valve if it is fouled or dirty, or else replace it.

Op. 3330D20 SINGLE-ACTING VALVE FOR BRAKE SERVO - R+R

5

BRAKE SERVO VACUUM CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the brake servo vacuum unit.

Check the condition of the vacuum pipe and the connectors and bands

Move on to Step 6

1. Brake servo vacuum unit faulty

1. Replace the brake servo vacuum unit.

Op. 3330D30 VACUUM PUMP - R+R

2. Pipe damaged.

2. Renew the fastenings or replace the vacuum pipe

6

BRAKE SERVO CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the brake servo is working properly.

See Test 3330DA CHECK VACUUM PUMP OPERATION AND BRAKE SERVO VACUUM CIRCUIT

End of diagnosis

Brake servo not working properly.

Replace brake servo

Op. 3330D10
