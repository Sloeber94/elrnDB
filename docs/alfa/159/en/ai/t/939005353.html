Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Insert the ignition key (panel on).

Check that there is a whilstling noise in the next 2 seconds coming from the fuel pump indicating that it is activated and then the pump should be deactivated.

The test can be considered over

Move on to Step 2

2

With the diagnostic equipment in active diagnosis carry out the active diagnosis of the fuel pump relay checking that the result is positive

Move on to Step 4

Move on to Step 3

3

If faults are present, repair or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring between the engine management control unit and the engine compartment junction unit.

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

The test can be considered over

Replace the fuel pump relay

4

Press the START/STOP button (panel off).

Connect a pressure gauge to the injectors.

Press the START/STOP button (panel on).

Check that the fuel pressure rises (whilst the pump is working) between 5.7 and 6.3 bar, then slowly decreases and returns to the previous value.

Move on to Step 7

Move on to Step 5

5

Press the START/STOP button (panel off).

Remove the fuel pump relay T10 in the engine compartment junction unit.

Press the START/STOP button (panel on).

Check that the fuel pressure continues not to decrease

Replace the fuel pump relay

6

If faults are present, repair or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring between the fuel pump and the engine compartment junction unit.

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

The test can be considered over

Replace the engine compartment junction unit

Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.

7

Press the START/STOP button (panel off).

Remove fuse F21 in the engine compartment junction unit and check that it has not blown.

Move on to Step 12

Move on to Step 8

8

Fit a new F21 fuse. Disconnect the connector at the fuel pump

See N040 FUEL PUMP AND LEVEL GAUGE

and check that there are no short circuits

Restore or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

9

Check the electrical wiring between the fuel pump and the engine compartment junction unit

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

making sure there are no faults present

Restore or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

10

Press the START/STOP button (panel on). Check that the fuel pressure increases and that fuse F21 intervenes

The circuit between the engine compartment junction unit and the fuel pump has been overloaded: check the wiring

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

11

If faults are present, repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring for the fuel pump.

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Check, amongst other things, that the connectors do not show signs of corrosion or damage.

The test can be considered over

Replace the fuel pump assembly

Op. 1040A70 SUBMERGED PUMP ASSEMBLY COMPLETE WITH LEVEL GAUGE CONTROL - R + R

12

Press the START/STOP button (panel off).

Remove the fuel pump relay T10 in the engine compartment junction unit.

Press the START/STOP button (panel on).

Check the power supply at the base of relay T10 is not faulty

Restore or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

13

Connect a cable and bridge with a 15 A fuse between the battery positive

See A001 BATTERY

and Pin B of the pump

See N040 FUEL PUMP AND LEVEL GAUGE

Check that the fuel pressure increases and the pump is activated. Move on to Step 3

Move on to Step 11
