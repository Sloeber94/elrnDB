The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P2620 (motorized throttle (feedback).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected if the control unit recognizes a malfunction in the circuit through an internal logic evaluation: short circuit to earth/positive or break in the connecting circuit at Pin 59 of the junction unit.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
