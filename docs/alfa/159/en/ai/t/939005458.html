Op. 7040G36 BULB HOLDER FOR LOAD CARRYING COMPARTMENT lighting on tailgate - R.R.

Op. 7015A05 TAILGATE LOWER INTERIOR PANEL-R R

1. Undo the badge retaining nuts.

2. Working from the outside of the tailgate, remove the badge (2a) releasing the flexible retainers (2b).

3. Keep the gasket.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the badge is not damaged.

- Position the gasket.

- Position the badge and attach the flexible retainers.

- Tighten the badge retaining nuts.
