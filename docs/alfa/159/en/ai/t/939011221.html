Removing

Refitting

Op. 7025B38 BOOT LID LOCK - R+R

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the lock (2a) from the motor (2b).

3. If necessary, undo the bolts (3a), then disconnect the electrical connection (3b) and separate the wiring (3c) from the motor (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the motor and wiring are not damaged.

- If removed, position the wiring, then connect the electrical connection and tighten the bolts.

- Position the motor on the lock.

- Tighten the bolts.

Op. 7025B38 BOOT LID LOCK - R+R
