Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The seat belt will not lock even when the belt is suddenly tugged out.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK LOCKING DEVICE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Tug out the belt suddenly to check whether the inertia reel locks. Also check that the reel is correctly fitted.

Move on to Step 2

The strap of one front belt does not lock

Replace the front belt

Op. 7065A10 FRONT SEAT BELT (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

The strap of one rear belt does not lock

Replace the rear seat belt

Op. 7065B10 REAR SEAT BELT (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Incorrect installation

Proceed as instructed in the

See table O031 SEAT BELTS LOCKED

2

ROAD TEST

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Carry out a road test and check whether the belt locks following a sudden braking manoeuvre.

The front belt does not lock.

Replace the front belt

Op. 7065A10 FRONT SEAT BELT (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

The rear belt does not lock

Replace the rear seat belt

Op. 7065B10 REAR SEAT BELT (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.
