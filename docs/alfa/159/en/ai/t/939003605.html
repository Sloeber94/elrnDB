H008 EXCESSIVE ROLLING/PITCHING - AT REAR

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The customer complains of an excessive rolling and/or pitching sensation of the vehicle, especially at the back.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

ROAD TEST TO EVALUATE THE ROLLING/PITCHING

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Carry out a road test to evalute the folling/pitching of the vehicle

See Test 44B ROAD TEST FOR ROLLING/PITCHING EVALUATION

End of diagnosis

Subjective judgement compared with the reference vehicle 6

Acceptable behaviour of the vehicle

Subjective judgement compared with the reference vehicle 5.5

Move on to Step 2

Subjective judgement compared with the reference vehicle 3-4

Move on to Step 4

2

LONGITUDINAL ARM CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check condition:

- of the bush fastening the longitudinal arm to the body;

- of the bushes fastening the longitudinal arm to the steering knuckle

Move on to Step 3

Bush fastening longitudinal arm to the body not intact

Replacement of longitudianl arm complete with transverse plate and bodyshell mounting bracket, in accordance with the

Op. 4410B40 LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM (WITH BALLJOINT) FOR LEFT OR RIGHT FRONT SUSPENSION (ONE) - R+R - CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY

checking the toe-in values are correct

Bushes fastening the longitudinal arm to the steering knuckle not intact

Replacement of rear stub axle, checking that the toe in values are correct

3

CAMBER ROD CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check condition:

- of the bush securing the camber rod to the crossmember;

- of the bush securing the camber rod to the steering knuckle

Move on to Step 4

Bush securing the camber rod to the crossmember not intact

Replacement of camber rod in accordance with the

Op. 4410B68 LOWER SWINGING ARM(ONE), LH OR RH OF FRONT SUSPENSION - R R -CHECK WHEEL ALIGNMENT SEPARATELY

checking the camber values are correct

Bush securing the camber rod to the steering knuckle not intact

Replacement of rear stub axle, checking that the toe in values are correct

4

SHOCK ABSORBER EFFICIENCY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the efficiency of the shock absorbers by carrying out manual compression manoeuvres (resting on the wheel arch by the centre of the wheel), which should be carried out on both sides of the vehicle.

Move on to Step 5

Sudden return without damping

Replace the shock absorber that is not working properly

Op. 4420A12 REAR SUSPENSION DAMPER (ONE), LH OR RH - FOR MCPHERSON STRUT SYSTEM - INCLUDES DISMENTLING/REBUILDING OF ASSEMBLY AT BENCH R R

5

VEHICLE GEOMETRY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

After replacing the shock absorbers, check the geometry

End of diagnosis

Geometry values do not conform

Restore the geometry to the correct values

Op. 4450A40 REAR WHEEL GEOMETRY - CHECK USING OPTICAL EQUIPMENT, INCLUDES ADJUSTMENT IF NECESSARY
