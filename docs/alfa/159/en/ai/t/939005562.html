Removing

Refitting

- Raise the seat until it is in the end of travel position.

Op. 7045A10 FRONT SEAT (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Op. 7045A42 GUARD (ONE) FOR FRONT SEAT, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection.

Description

Component

DRIVER'S SEAT SLIDING ADJUSTMENT MOTOR

See N127 DRIVER'S SEAT SLIDING ADJUSTMENT MOTOR

PASSENGER SEAT SLIDING ADJUSTMENT MOTOR

See N130 PASSENGER SIDE SEAT SLIDE ADJUSTMENT MOTOR

2. Raise motor (2a) by releasing retaining flanges (2b) from seats (2c).

3. Remove motor (2a) complete with flexible shafts (3a).

4. Separate motor (2a) from flexible shafts (3a).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Electric seats

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the motor is not damaged.

1. Connect motor (1a) to flexible shafts (1b).

2. Position motor (1a) in its seat complete with flexible shafts (1b) and connect shaft ends (2a) to their seats.

3. Do up motor retaining flanges (3a) in their seats (3b).

4. Connect the electrical connection.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Electric seats

Op. 7045A42 GUARD (ONE) FOR FRONT SEAT, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Op. 7045A10 FRONT SEAT (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

- Check that the seat slides correctly.
