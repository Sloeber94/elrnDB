Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the Connect system comes on with key on

Check the Connect system comes on when the On/Off key is pressed

End of diagnosis

1.Will not come on with the key on

1. Move on to Step 1

2.Will not come on when the button is pressed

2. Move on to Step 1

1

ELECTRICAL CONNECTION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Withdraw the Connect from the dashboard.

  • Check connections and wiring continuity
  • Check the condition of the fuse on the Connect body.
  • Check that the correct power supplies are present at the Connect connectors

See Test 5580PO CONNECT POWER SUPPLY CHECK

Move on to Step 2

1.Wiring interrupted

1. Renew the blown fuse after ascertaining the cause

2.No power

2. Restore correct power supply

See E4090 INSTRUMENT PANEL AND INDICATORS

3. Incorrect earth connection

3. Restore the correct earth connection

See E4090 INSTRUMENT PANEL AND INDICATORS

2

ACTION

Check that it is working properly

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace Connect
