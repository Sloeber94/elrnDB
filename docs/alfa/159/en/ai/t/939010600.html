Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

- On this car, it is not possible to remove/refit the exhaust manifold on the car.

To perform this operation, remove/refit the power unit

Op. 1004B10 POWER UNIT WITH MANUAL GEARBOX - R.R

and then work at the bench, customising the operation according to the engine service procedures.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

The exhaust manifold is built into the turbocharger, therefore refer toOp. 1064A27 TURBOCHARGER UNIT AND EXHAUST MANIFOLD - R.R.
