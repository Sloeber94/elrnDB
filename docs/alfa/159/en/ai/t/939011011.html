Removing

Refitting

- Open the bonnet.

1. Disconnect the negative terminal (1a) from the battery after having loosened it by rotating the cam lever (1b).

Description

Component

See A001 BATTERY

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Release the retainers (1a) and remove the cover (1b) for the supply box.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the retaining bracket.

3. Loosen the nut for the positive battery terminal.

4. Move the supply box to the side.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the nut (1a) and move the battery retaining belt (1b).

2. Remove the battery.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

- Check that the battery is not damaged.

- Place the battery in its housing.

- Position the retaining belt on the battery and tighten the nut.
