The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0120 (Accelerator pedal sensor 1).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected if, with fault code P0641 not recognized, the junction unit input voltage (Pin 9) is more than 4.67 V (positive short circuit) or less than 0.15 V (short circuit to earth or break in the circuit). As a result, the accelerator pedal does not work.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
