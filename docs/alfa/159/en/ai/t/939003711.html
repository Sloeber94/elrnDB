N011 RADIO CANNOT BE HEARD, IS HARD TO HEAR

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the relevant control is operated, the radio comes on but it cannot be heard properly or crackles, radio station reception is disturbed.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Use the Examiner to connect to the Connect control unit and check for errors

Move on to Step 2

2. AM/FM aerial connection interrupted because connector incorrectly inserted

1.Repair the connection between aerial and Connect

2. Aerial impedence defective or interrupted

2. Replace the aerial

Op. 5580M70 AERIAL FOR ON-BOARD NAVIGATOR - R.R.

2

RECEPTION AREA CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is not present in areas of poor reception. The reception should improve significantly when the vehicle moves away from such areas.

If the reception area is very poorly served, attempts to switch between frequencies may be perceived as pauses

Move on to Step 3

Area with poor reception

Move to an area with the radio reception is good and try FM and non-FM stations

3

CHECK SPEAKER OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Test the speakers by carried out the

See Test 5580PJ CONNECT AUTODIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE

in SETUP screen 5.

Check the connections between the speakers and the radio, see the wiring diagram

See E3510 RADIO

Move on to Step 4

1. Connections not correct

1. Repair the connections

2.Front speakers defective

2. Replace the speakers

Op. 5570T90 FRONT SPEAKER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

3.Rear speakers

3.Replace the speakers

Op. 5570T98 REAR SPEAKER (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
