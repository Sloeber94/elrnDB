The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P02658 (Injector for cylinder 3)

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected during the diagnosis of the junction unit final stage if there is a short circuit to earth/positive or break in the connecting circuit at Pins 1 and 46 of the junction unit. The fault is memorized directly as soon as it is recognized. In this case the engine is switched off.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
