L026 ELECTRIC AIR PREHEATING IS NOT ACTIVATED WITH LOW EXTERNAL TEMPERATURE

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Automatic Air Conditioning

The outside temperature information is correctly displayed on the reconfigurable matrix.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Automatic Air Conditioning

The electrical pre-heating device (only fitted on the JTD versions) consists of two (PTC) resistances with different resistance values which develop a maximum power of 900 W when a current passes through; when one of the two resistances or both of them are activated, the control unit is capable of modulating the heating.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Automatic Air Conditioning

The system can only be activated in the following circumstances:

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Automatic Air Conditioning

  • Ignition on and climate control switched on;
  • Outside temperature below 20° C;
  • Engine coolant temperature below 64° C;
  • Battery voltage more than 12.2 V or more than 12.6 V (if only the less powerful section of the resistances is activated)
  • Engine rpm higher than 700 rpm
  • Driver's temperature below the passenger compartment sensor temperature.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Automatic Air Conditioning
