Refitting

Removing

- Check that the striker is not damaged.

1. Position the striker.

2. Screw in the bolts without tightening them.

3. Move the striker into the correct position, using the slotted openings in the bolts. The arrows indicate the possible movements for the adjustment.

4. Tighten the retaining bolts to the recommended torque.
