Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Lower the crossmember in order to unscrew the bolt securing the rear suspension front wishbone to the crossmember.

Op. 4420D42 REAR SUSPENSION BEAM - R R

1. Unscrew the bolt (1a), bolt (1b) and remove the rear suspension front wishbone (1c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Refit the rear suspension front wishbone (1a) and secure by tightening new bolts (1b) and (1c) to the specified torque.
