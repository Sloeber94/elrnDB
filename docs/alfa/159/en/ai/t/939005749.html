Applies to: engines: 1.8; all production periods

The main specifications of the 1.8 engine are as follows:

- four cylinder in-line arrangement;

- dual overhead camshaft with 16 valve timing system;

- Simtec 75.3 injection system;

- dual continuous variable valve timing;

- mechanical tappets;

- oil filter mounting with heat exchanger;

- timing system with toothed belt drive;

- ignition module with built-in spark plug connectors;

- low fuel consumption and emission levels (meet Euro 4 requirements);

- EOBD and two Lambda sensors.
