Applies to: all engines; all production periods

FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

The (earth) activation signal for the fog lights reaches pin 1 of connector I of M1 from the control button in the panel to the left of the steering wheel H90.

The side lights activation signal is sent to pin 28 of connector G and 2 of connector I of the Body Computer M1 from the steering column switch unit H5.

See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

The Body Computer M1 controls the power supply for the fog lights sending an earth signal to the relay T14: of the engine compartment junction unit B1 - from pin 8 of connector A of M1 - which energizes relay T14: this supplies the fog lights F15 (left) and F16 (right). The circuit is protected by fuse F30 of junction unit B2.

The Body Computer M1 is connected via the CAN line to the instrument panel E50 to manage the "fog lights on" warning light.
