#### ATTENTION

#### WARNINGS
This vehicle is equipped with a starting system known as Smart-Key where there is no longer a traditional ignition key having been replaced with an electronic key "T.E.G." (Transmitter for Entry and go) combined with an electronic key reader and TEG-Reader radio frequency receiver.
> Note: When the battery is disconnected, the Steering Lock Node remains in the same condition (released or locked) as previously. Whenever it is necessary to turn the steering wheel with the battery disconnected during workshop operations, leave the TEG inserted when disconnecting the battery: the steering will thereby remain unlocked and free to turn.

## 3380A04 HANDBRAKE - ADJUST TRAVEL  [939005323]
- Path: Procedures > 33 BRAKES > 3380 HANDBRAKE > 3380A HANDBRAKE CONTROL AND LINKAGE > 3380A04 HANDBRAKE - ADJUST TRAVEL
- Kind: repair procedure (removal / refitting, operation number = code)
- Applies to: all engines; all production periods
- Web: https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB/#/939005323

> Note: The adjustment of the parking brake should only be carried out after having replaced: the rear brake pads - the handbrake control cables - the rear brake calilpers because the recovery of the clearance due to wear is automatic.
- Position the vehicle on a lift.
1. Lift up the cover for the front armrest with oddments pocket.
- Remove the lower trim for the oddments compartment.
2. Loosen the adjustment nut for the handbrake control cable by at least two or three turns in order to ensure that the cable is completely loosened.
> Note: Before loosening the nut, mark its original position.
[image: https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB/media/946000427.jpg]

- Start the engine and with the engine idling, press the brake pedal forcefully at least ten or fifteen times.
- Retighten the handbrake control cable adjustment nut to the original position marked previously.
- Check that the rear wheels are free to turn with the handbrake lever at rest.
- Check that the handbrake braking action begins from the first click.
- At the end of the adjustment, check that the handbrake lever engages between five and six notches of the toothed sector.
- Switch off the engine.
- Place the lower trim for the oddments compartment in its housing and lower the front armrest.
- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
