ALFA 159 workshop manual: guide

Read this first. It explains how the manual is organised, what the codes mean and how to move between pages.

1. Establish the vehicle first

Content differs by engine, production period and equipment. Ask for these before answering, and ignore any topic or block whose Applies to line excludes the car.

  • Engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V
  • Production periods: Up to 12/05, From 01/06
  • Equipment: Station Wagon, 4x4, Right hand drive, C.A.E., Smart gearbox, GTA, High setup, Low setup, Xenon Gas headlamps, Radio setup, BOSE System, Manual Air Conditioning, Automatic Air Conditioning, VDC, Powered sunroof, Connect Nav+, Split rear seat, One-piece rear seat, Bluetooth, Electric seats. "Station Wagon" means Sportwagon; "C.A.E." means the automatic gearbox, so pages marked C.A.E. do not apply to manual cars.

Some topics exist as several variants with the same code and title (for example one for the Sportwagon and one for right-hand drive). Pick the variant whose Applies to matches the car.

2. How the pages link

  • Every topic has its own page, t/<id>.html, with its path (the breadcrumb at the top), its kind, and what it applies to.
  • Subtopics lists the children. Chapters have no content of their own, only subtopics.
  • Topics linked from this page lists everything the text points to: pinouts, components, procedures.
  • Topics that link to this page is the reverse: use it to find every diagram, pinout and procedure that involves a component or topic.
  • Diagrams and location drawings are images; each has a link to the image file.

3. Sections

  • Technical Data: technical data / specifications
  • Descriptions: system description
  • Fault diagnosis: fault diagnosis table (symptom -> numbered test steps)
  • Test: test / check procedure
  • Procedures: repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
  • Electrical equipment: electrical functions, harnesses and pinouts

Typical fault-tracing route: Fault diagnosis (symptom table, numbered steps) -> Test (checks) -> Electrical equipment (functional description, wiring diagram, pinouts) -> Procedures (replace the part, by operation number).

4. Electrical equipment codes

  • Functions are coded E + 4 digits (E2022 reversing lights). Each has a DESCRIPTION, a FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION (which fuse feeds it, which pins and components are involved), a WIRING DIAGRAM and a COMPONENT LOCATION.
  • Components are coded with a letter and 3 digits (B002, F036, I020, M001). Families:
  • A STARTING - RECHARGING
  • B VARIOUS FUSE HOLDERS AND VALVES
  • C EARTHS
  • D INTERMEDIATE COUPLINGS
  • E INSTRUMENT PANEL AND INDICATORS
  • F EXTERIOR LIGHTS
  • G INTERIOR LIGHTS
  • H MANUAL ELECTRIC CONTROLS
  • I SWITCHES
  • J RELAYS
  • K SENSORS AND SENDER UNITS
  • L ELECTROMAGNETS AND SOLENOID VALVES
  • M INTERMITTENT AND TIMED ELECTRONIC DEVICES
  • N ELECTRIC MOTORS AND ACTUATORS
  • O RESISTANCES AND DIMMERS
  • P VARIOUS CONSUMERS
  • Q SAFETY DEVICES
  • Pinouts give every pin: wire section (mm²), wire colour (decoded below) and the pin at the other end. "via splice" means the wire joins others at a splice (a diamond in the diagrams).
  • Fuses are F + number inside a junction unit (e.g. fuse F35 in junction unit B2 under the dashboard). Ratings and locations: see the fuse and relay index.
  • Circuit labels: INT = ignition-switched supply, CPL = permanent battery supply, CAN / A-BUS = data lines.
