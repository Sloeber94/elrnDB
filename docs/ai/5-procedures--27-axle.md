#### ATTENTION

#### WARNINGS
This vehicle is equipped with a starting system known as Smart-Key where there is no longer a traditional ignition key having been replaced with an electronic key "T.E.G." (Transmitter for Entry and go) combined with an electronic key reader and TEG-Reader radio frequency receiver.
> Note: When the battery is disconnected, the Steering Lock Node remains in the same condition (released or locked) as previously. Whenever it is necessary to turn the steering wheel with the battery disconnected during workshop operations, leave the TEG inserted when disconnecting the battery: the steering will thereby remain unlocked and free to turn.

## 2720D18 DIFFERENTIAL SIDE CONSTANT VELOCITY JOINT (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. WITH DRIVESHAFT REMOVED - INCLUDES BOOT REPLACEMENT  [939005318]

- Kind: repair procedure (removal / refitting, operation number = code)
- Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods
- Web: https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB/#/939005318

##### Removing
- Position the rear driveshaft in a vice fitted with protective jaws.
1. Remove the protective boot retaining bands.
2. Retract the protective boot to uncover the joint.
- Remove the grease from the joint.
[image: https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB/media/946000412.jpg]

1. Remove the circlip (1a) and release the differential internal driveshaft (1b) from the bearing.
[image: https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB/media/946000413.jpg]

1. Remove the bearing circlip.
2. Using a suitable drift (2a) and mallet, remove the bearing (2b) from the driveshaft.
- Remove the boot from the driveshaft.
[image: https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB/media/946000414.jpg]

##### Refitting
- Wash the constant velocity joint components thoroughly using diesel oil or petrol and visually inspect that there are no signs of seizing or grooves on the balls or seats.
- Position the new protective boot on the driveshaft.
- Fit the bearing for the joint in its housing and secure it using a new circlilp.
- Fill the differential internal driveshaft and the protective boot with the recommended grease.
| Component | Type | Description | Classification | Qty. | Validity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Differential side constant velocity joint | Grease (g) | TUTELA MRM ZERO | NLGI 0 | 70 | 3.2 JTS |
- Place the differential internal driveshaft in position and secure it using the new circlip.
- Place the new protective boot in its housing and secure it to the driveshaft using the new band.
- Fit a new band securing the protective boot to the differential internal driveshaft using the recommended tool.
