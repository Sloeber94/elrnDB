#### ATTENTION

#### WARNINGS
This vehicle is equipped with a starting system known as Smart-Key where there is no longer a traditional ignition key having been replaced with an electronic key "T.E.G." (Transmitter for Entry and go) combined with an electronic key reader and TEG-Reader radio frequency receiver.
> Note: When the battery is disconnected, the Steering Lock Node remains in the same condition (released or locked) as previously. Whenever it is necessary to turn the steering wheel with the battery disconnected during workshop operations, leave the TEG inserted when disconnecting the battery: the steering will thereby remain unlocked and free to turn.

## 4110A10 STEERING WHEEL - R+R FOR REPLACEMENT OR CHECKS  [939010817]
- Path: Procedures > 41 STEERING UNIT > 4110 STEERING CONTROL AND LINKAGE > 4110A STEERING CONTROL > 4110A10 STEERING WHEEL - R+R FOR REPLACEMENT OR CHECKS
- Kind: repair procedure (removal / refitting, operation number = code)
- Applies to: all engines; all production periods
- Web: https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB/#/939010817

##### Removing
Op. 5580C18 AIR BAG MODULE - R+R [→939011097]
> Note: Follow all the recommended safety instructions.
- Position the front wheels so that they are perfectly straight and mark the position of the steering wheel in relation to the upper half casing.
1. Disconnect the electrical connection.
2. Remove the protection.
3. Undo the nut (3a) and remove the steering wheel (3b).
> Note: The nut securing the steering wheel must be replaced each time it is refitted.
[image: https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB/media/939001651.jpg]

##### Refitting
- Place the steering wheel in position checking that the marks made previously on the steering wheel and the upper half casing are aligned.
> Note: Whilst the steering wheel is being fitted the steering column switch unit cables must be fully extracted from the hub on the steering wheel.
- Tighten a new steering wheel securing nut to the recommended torque.
| Component | Fastening | dia | Value (daNm) | Validity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Steering wheel | Nut | M16 | (steering shaft side) 4.5 - 5.5 |  |
- Place the protection back in its housing.
- Connect the electrical connection.
> Note: Check that the electrical cables are correctly positioned in the special ducts in the steering wheel; this operation is vital or else the positioning of the Air Bag module and the operation of the horn will be adversely affected.
Op. 5580C18 AIR BAG MODULE - R+R [→939011097]

##### Removing
> Note: The following procedure describes the removing-refitting of the left steering rod, carry out the same operations for the right steering rod.
- Position the vehicle on a lift.
Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL - R.R [→939010871]
1. Measure the free length of the steering rod thread.
2. Loosen the locknut.
> Note: Oppose the steering rod end using a spanner.
3. Undo the nut fixing the steering rod to the right front steering knuckle.
4. Disconnect the steering rod (4a) from the right front steering knuckle using the tool (4b).
| Tool | Description | Function | Validity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1871000700 | Extractor | Extracting steering rod from steering knuckle |  |
[image: https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB/media/939000597.jpg]

- Remove the steering rod by unscrewing it from the power assisted steering box by hand.

##### Refitting
- Tighten the steering rod on the power assisted steering box observing the free length of the thread measured at the removal.
- Connect the steering rod to the right front steering knuckle and secure it tightening a new nut to the recommended torque.
| Component | Fastening | dia | Value (daNm) | Validity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Steering rods | Nut | M12 | (steering knuckle side) 9.0 - 11.0 |  |
- Tighten the locknut the recommended torque.
> Note: Oppose the steering rod end using a spanner.
| Component | Fastening | dia | Value (daNm) | Validity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Steering rod | Locknut | M20 | (power assisted steering box side) 11.7 - 14.3 |  |
Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL - R.R [→939010871]
- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
