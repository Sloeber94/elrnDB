O005 FRONT SEAT HEIGHT ADJUSTMENT NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The backrest electrical adjustment is not working / not working properly.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK ON SWITCH STATUS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Select the following control unit at the diagnostic equipment:

Driver's/passenger seat control unit.

Check the value of the following parameter:

Seat lifiting switch and check that it indicates released when the switch is released and indicates pressed when the switch is pressed.

Move on to Step 5

There is no change in the status of the switch.

Move on to Step 2

2

CHECK ON WIRING CONNECTIONS AT SEAT LIFTING SWITCH

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the connection for the wiring at the electric seat control switch.

Also check for any oxidation/impurities at the connector/switch.

Move on to Step 3

1.Switch wiring connector detached or badly positioned on seat mounting.

1.Fit connector correctly on switch.

2. Presence of oxidation/impurities at connector/control unit.

2.Eliminate oxidation/impurities at connector/switch.

3

CHECK ON WIRING CONNECTIONS AT CONTROL UNIT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the connection for the wiring at the electric seat control unit.

Also check for any oxidation/impurities at the connector/control unit.

Move on to Step 4

1.Switch wiring connector detached or badly positioned on seat control unit.

1.Fit connector correctly on control unit

2. Presence of oxidation/impurities at connector/control unit

2.Eliminate oxidation/impurities at connector/control unit.

4

SEAT LIFTING SWITCH OPERATION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the seat lifting switch is working properly

Move on to Step 9

Seat lifiting switch not working correctly.

Replace the switch

Op. 7045J50

5

CHECKS USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the diagnostic equipment connected, check:

that the backrest actuator is working properly through active diagnosis.

Move on to Step 9

Seat lifiting actuator not working correctly.

Move on to Step 6

6

CHECK ON CONNECTION AT MOTOR

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the connection is correctly attached to the motor

Also check for any oxidation/impurities at the connector/motor.

Move on to Step 7

1.Connectors at motor detached or badly positioned.

1.Fit connector correctly on motor

2.Presence of oxidation/impurities at connector/motor.

2.Eliminate oxidation/impurities at connector/motor.

7

CHECK ON ELECTRICAL SEAT WIRING

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the electric seat wiring

Move on to Step 8

Presence of cuts/tears to seat wiring.

Replace cut/broken wiring see wiring diagram

See E3076 ELECTRICALLY-ADJUSTABLE SEATS

8

CHECK ON SEAT LIFITING ADJUSTMENT MOTOR

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the seat lifting movement motor is working properly.

Move on to Step 9

Seat lifting motor not working correctly.

Replace the backrest adjustment motor

Op. 7045J30 FRONT SEAT LIFTING MOTOR, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL WIRING CHECK

9

DRIVER'S/PASSENGER SEAT CONTROL UNIT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Replace the driver's/passenger seat control unit

Op. 7045J36 SEAT MOVEMENT ECU R R FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Contact technical service for further information on the problem.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Seat lifiting lever loose, detached/slipping

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK CONDITION OF LEVER ATTACHMENT SYSTEM

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the lever attachment system.

Move on to Step 2

1.Lever and/or ratchet retaining elements broken.

1. Replace the lever

Op. 7045A56 DRIVER'S SEAT HEIGHT ADJUSTMENT DEVICE CONTROL LEVER - R.R

2. Lever fixing bolt slack/thread stripped

2. Replace the bolt.

2

CHECK ON CONDITION OF RATCHET ATTACHMENT SYSTEM

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the seat lifting ratchet attachment system.

Move on to Step 3

Ratchet loose (fastening elements to seat frame).

Retighten the ratchet to the frame correctly.

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present.

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace the seat lifting ratchet

Op. 7045A36 DRIVER'S SEAT MANUAL HEIGHT ADJUSTMENT DEVICE - R.R
