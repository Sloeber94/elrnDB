With the ignition ON, the maximum turbocharger pressure warning light in the instrument panel is not working

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

The turbocharger pressure signal is made available to the instrument panel by the engine management node and, via the serial line, to the gauge.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected to the diagnostic socket, check the operation of the warning light through active diagnosis

Move on to Step 2

1.The warning light does not work because the wiring for the serial line and the NQS is interrupted

1.Renew serial line

2.Instrument panel defective

2.Replace instrument panel

Op. 5560C51 COMBINED ANALOGUE CONTROL PANEL - R+R OR REPLACE

3.Instrument power supply interrupted

3.Restore correct power supply.

2

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for the presence of a PWM signal from the engine management node to the instrument panel

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Move on to Step 3

1.Wiring between turbo sensor and NCM interrupted

1.Renew the wiring

2.Turbo sensor defective

2.Replace the supercharging sensor

Op. 1060G16 PRESSURE RELIEF SENSOR - R.R.

3.Signal between NCM and NQS interrupted

3.Restore correct signal.

4.Instrument defective

4.Replace gauge

Op. 5560C51 COMBINED ANALOGUE CONTROL PANEL - R+R OR REPLACE

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace Body Computer

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R.R
