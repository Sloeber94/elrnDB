Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Loosen the bolt (1a) and undo the bolt (1b) and remove the front bumper mounting bracket (1c).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Undo the bolt securing the air duct to the radiator.

2. Loosen the bolt (2a), undo the bolt (2b) and remove the radiator mounting bracket (2c) complete with flexible mounting and lower radiator support pin.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Fit the new radiator support pin in its housing until the retaining clip clicks into place.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

- Fit the flexible mounting on the lower radiator support pin.

- Tighten the bolt securing the air duct to the radiator.

- Place the radiator mounting bracket complete with the flexible mounting in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

- Place the bumper mounting bracket in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

- Connect the bumper to the bracket fitted previously.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
