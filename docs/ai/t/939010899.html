Removing

Refitting

Op. 7040L71 BOX ON TUNNEL REAR COATING - R R

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the vent (2a) releasing the internal retainers (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the vent is not damaged.

- Refit the vent and attach the retainers.

- Tighten the bolts.
