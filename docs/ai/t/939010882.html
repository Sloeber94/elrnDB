5010A12 HEATER WATER SUPPLY PIPE - R.R.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Follow the recommended safety instructions (see 5010 - Precautions).

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

Op. 5530B10 BATTERY - R+R

- Remove the engine compartment components that prevent access to the pipe.

- Arrange a suitable container under the vehicle to connect the coolant.

1. Adjust the heater inlet rapid connector and extract the pipe, collecting the fluid.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Remove the band.

2. Disconnect the pipe from the connector on the engine.

- Release the pipe and keep it.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the pipe is undamaged and is not affected by cracks that affect the seal.

- Fit the pipe on the engine side connector and secure with the band.

- Position the pipe and secure it correctly.

- Connect the rapid connector to the heater inlet.

- Refill the engine cooling circuit.
