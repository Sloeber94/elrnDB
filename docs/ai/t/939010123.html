After operating the steering numerous times, a noise is heard when the steering wheel is left still

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Visually inspect the level of the power assisted steering fluid in the reservoir. Conduct a test by steering completely to one side and then releasing the steering wheel

Move on to Step 1

1. Fluid level too low.

1. Restore the correct fluid level: if there are leaks, continue follow the instructions in

See table X030 POWER ASSISTED STEERING SYSTEM FLUID LEAKS

2. The noise heard is a knock/striking noise

2. Move on to Step 1

3. A blowing noise is heard in correspondence with the three steering wheel positions (120° apart)

3. No intervention is nececssary: the noise is due to the by-pass valve that discharges the excess pressure

1

CHECK STEERING COLUMN BEARINGS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for excessive play in the steering column: check the condition and efficiency of the steering column mount bearings.

End of diagnosis

Bearings worn or damaged

Replace the affected bearings

Op. 4110A30 SUPPORT WITH STEERING CONTROL SHAFTS - R+R
