Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The "Instrument Panel Node" receives information directly from the "Mode", "+" and "-" keys.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment and check, in Instrument Panel Node mode, that there are no errors and that the parameters are present.

Move on to Step 6

1. Impossible to connect to the NQS

1. Move on to Step 2

2. Presence of errors.

2. Move on to Step 5

2

ELECTRICAL POWER SUPPLY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the electricity supply to the Instrument Panel Node is correct.

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Move on to Step 3

Fault in positive electrical supply and/or earth

Renew and/or replace the defective component (in the case of a fuse, find out what caused it to blow).

3

CHECK ELECTRICAL CONNECTIONS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the electrical wiring/connections of the instrument Panel Node and those between node and steering wheel stalk. Check, in particular, that each pin is correctly connected and inserted in its seat.

Move on to Step 4

Electrical wiring and/or connections not operating.

Restore damaged wiring and/or electrical connections.

4

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Use an Examiner to check the possibility of connection to the Instrument Panel Node

Move on to Step 5

Impossible to connect to the control unit.

Replace the instrument panel

Op. 5560B10 CONTROL PANEL - R+R

5

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the correct operation of the following components:

  • Wiring between node and steering wheel stalk
  • Control keys, see wiring diagram

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Move on to Step 6

1. Wiring faulty

1. Repair wiring.

2. Stalk controls malfunctioning.

2. Replace the steering column switch unit

Op. 5550A12

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
