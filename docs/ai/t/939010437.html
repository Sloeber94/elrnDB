Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts (1a), (1b) and remove the flywheel side oil seal cover (1c).

Cut the sealant between the flywheel side oil seal cover and the crankcase sump.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Clean sealant residues off the flywheel side oil seal cover contact surface with the crankcase sump.

- Apply silicone sealant to the contact surface between the crankcase sump and flywheel side oil seal cover.

1. Place the flywheel side cover (1a) in its housing and secure it tightening the new bolts (1b) and bolts (1c) to the recommended torque.

When positioning the flywheel side oil seal cover, avoid significant sideways movements that could remove the sealant.
