Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Press the START/STOP button and turn off the engine.

Reconnect all the disconnected components.

Turn off all the electrical users.

Insert a new fuse F11 in the engine compartment junction unit and check it does not blow.

Move on to Step 3

Move on to Step 2

2

If necessary, repair errors in the wiring between the engine compartment junction unit and spark plug control unit

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

The test can be considered over

Replace the glow plug preheating unit

Op. 5520C20 HEATER PLUGS CONTROL UNIT - R.R

3

Press the START/STOP button.

Check that fuse F11 cuts in

A temporary current overload has occurred in the circuit behind fuse F11. connect the electrical connectors for the junction unit.

4

Press the START/STOP button.

Disconnect the harness from a spark plug.

Fit a new fuse F11 and check it does not blow.

Reconnect the spark plugs and disconnect another, repeating the test until all the spark plugs have been checked.

Replace the spark plug that does not trigger the fuse

Op. 5520C10 HEATER PLUGS - R+R

5

If necessary, repair the faults in the wiring between the spark plug control unit and the glow plugs

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Replace the glow plug preheating unit

Op. 5520C20 HEATER PLUGS CONTROL UNIT - R.R
