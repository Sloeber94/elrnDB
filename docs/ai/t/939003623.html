J042 RIGHT DOOR MIRROR ELECTRICAL ADJUSTMENT NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the control is operated the right external mirror does not move.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The mirror is adjusted by the Passenger Door Node according to information from the adjustment joysticks located on the driver's door handle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The joystick information (selection, controls) is sent from the Driver's Door Node to the Passenger Door Node.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic connect and check the following at the Driver's Door Node:

In errors ambient

  • that there are no faults

In parameters ambient

  • the plausibility of the parameters for joystick (left/right/up/down).

Move on to Step 2

1. Presence of faults

1. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment.

2. Mirror selector joystick parameters (left/right/up/down) not plausible.

2. Move on to Step 3

2

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected to the diagnostic connector, check the following at the Passenger Door Node

In errors ambient

  • that there are no faults.

In active diagnosis ambient

  • the operation of the mirror movement motors.

Move on to Step 3

1. Presence of faults

1. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment.

2. Adjustment motors not working.

2. Move on to Step 4

3

MIRROR ADJUSTMENT JOYSTICK OPERATION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Remove the control panel on the driver's door, disconnect the connector and check the operation of the joystick.

Op. 7005R40

Op. 7005RA

Move on to Step 7

Mirror control joystick faulty

Replace the external mirrors control joystick

Op. 7005R40

4

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Remove the right rear view mirror, disconnect the connector for the movement motors, remove the Passenger Door Node and check:

  • the continuity between the connector and the Passenger Door Node;
  • the presence of the joint supply positive.

Op. 7005R35 DOOR MIRROR REFLECTOR (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

See E3032 DOOR MIRROR ADJUSTMENT

Move on to Step 5

1. Electrical connection interrupted.

Move on to Step 5
