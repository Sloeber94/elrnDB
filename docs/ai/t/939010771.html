Refitting

The following procedure describes the removing-refitting of the left front wheel rpm sensor, carry out the same operations for the right front wheel rpm sensor.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the battery terminal (-).
