Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK CORRECT POWER SUPPLY TO WOOFER

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the subwoofer power supply is correct by carrying out

See Test 5570TC CHECK ON OPERATION OF SUBWOOFER

Move on to Step 2

Power supply interrupted

Restore correct power supply, see wiring diagram

See E3510 RADIO

2

CHECK THE AUDIO SIGNALS CONTROLLING THE SUBWOOFER

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for the presence of audio signals controlling the subwoofer by carrying out

See Test 5570TC

Move on to Step 3

Audio signals interrupted

Restore the correct audio signal wiring

3

CHECK SUBWOOFER ACTIVATION ENABLEMENT SIGNAL

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that a subwoofer activation enablement signal is present by carrying out

See Test 5570TC

Move on to Step 4

1. Subwoofer activation signal interrupted.

1. Restore the correct signal.

2. Subwoofer defective

2. Replace Bose subwoofer

Op. 5580T40 BOSE SOUND SYSTEM SUBWOOFER - R.R.

3. Audio amplifier defective.

3. Replace Bose audio amplifier

Op. 5580T42 BOSE SOUND SYSTEM AMPLIFIER - R.R.

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
