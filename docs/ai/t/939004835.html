Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Move the front courtesy light assembly with sun roof switch (1a), releasing the retainers (1b).

2. Disconnect the electrical connection.

Description

Component

SUN ROOF SWITCH

See H060 SUN ROOF SWITCH

3. Disconnect the sun roof switch casing (3a) from the front courtesy light (3b) releasing the retainers (3c) and the attachments (3d).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the sun roof switch is not damaged.

- Connect the sun roof switch to the front courtesy light, engaging the attachments and the retainers.

- Connect the electrical connection.

- Position the front courtesy light assembly and sun roof switch and attach the retainers.

- Check that the dismantled parts are working properly.
