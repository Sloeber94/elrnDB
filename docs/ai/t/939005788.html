Refitting

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

- Draw off the power assisted steering fluid from the reservoir using a suitable syringe.

1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the return pipe from the steering box to the heat exchanger, heat exchanger side.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and the nut (1b) securing the return pipe from the power steering box to the heat exchanger to the front suspension frame.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Undo the connector (1a) for the return pipe from the power steering box to the heat exchanger (1b), power steering box side and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the return pipe from the power steering box to the heat exhanger in position and secure it to the suspension frame using the nut and the bolt.

- Connect the return pipe from the power steering box to the heat exchanger to the power steering box and tighten the connector.

- Connect the rapid connector for the return pipe from the steering box to the heat exchanger, heat exchanger side.
