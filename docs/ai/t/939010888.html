Op. 5010B70 SIDE CONNECTOR BRACKETS ONE, LH OR RH, BETWEEN AIR CONDITIONER CASING AND DUCT FOR R

1. Undo the bolts fixing the bracket.

2. Remove the bracket.

3. Undo the bolt securing the air duct left arm.

4. Remove the air duct left arm (4a), releasing the two ends (4b) and (4c).

5. Remove the air duct right arm, releasing the front end.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the air duct is not damaged.

- Position the air duct right arm and attach the front end.

- Position the air duct left arm and attach the front end, then tighten the bolt.

- Position the bracket and tighten the bolts.
