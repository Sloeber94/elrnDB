Removing

Refitting

1. Loosen the band and disconnect the branch of the hose from the air flow meter to the throttle casing, resonator side.

2. Loosen the bands (2a) and remove the hose from the air flow meter to the throttle body (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Replace the sleeve from the air flow meter to the throttle valve in its housing and tighten with the bands.

- Connect the branchd of the hose from the air flow meter to the throttle casing, resonator side and secure it using the band.
