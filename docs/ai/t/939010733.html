Front differential bearing removal

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Undo bolts (1a) securing the differential ring gear with the upper casing half (1b) and separate frm the lower casing half.

2. Use a press to separate the differential ring gear from upper differential casing half pinion (2a) using plate (2b) and reaction bar (2c).

During this operation, support the differential from below.

Fitting rear differential bearing

Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Insert the differential ring gear to the upper differential casing half with the aid of a rubber mallet and taking care to align the retaining bolt holes.

Join the differential casing half with the differential ring gear and tighten the bolts to the specified torque.
