PRELIMINARY CHECKS

Operation

Check that there is no engine oil in the coolant, also check that the exhaust tail pipe is not dirty with oil and check that there are no bluish exhaust fumes

CHECK ENGINE BLOCK SURFACE

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Remove the head (only for 2.2 JTS version0

Op. 1016C10 SINGLE CYLINDER HEAD - R.R. AND REPLACE GASKET

(only for 3.2 V6 version)

Op. 1016D10 CYLINDER HEAD – R.R. AND REPLACE GASKETS

and check that there are no:

- oil deposits on valves;

- defects on engine block;

- defects on the cylinder head gasket.

(only for 2.2 JTS version) Overhaul the head

Cylinder block plane defective.

Level the engine block.

CYLINDER BORE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the cylinder liners/bores. More specifically:

- that there are no deep machining grooves;

- that there is no vitrification of the liners/bores;

- measure that there is no ovality or wear.

End of diagnosis

Presence of machining grooves.

Grind the cylinder.

Liners/bores vitrified

Grind the cylinder.

Piston rings faulty.

Replace the piston rings
