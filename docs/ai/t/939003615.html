J003 SCREEN WASHER NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the lever on the right of the steering wheel is pulled towards it, the windscreen washer function is not activated properly.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

First of all, check the level of the detergent fluid in the reservoir, also make sure that it is not leaking, also check that when the lever on the right of the steering wheel is operated, the windscreen wipers work properly.

Move on to Step 1

1. Mixture level too low.

1. Restore the correct level.

Op. 5050A14 RESERVOIR PLUG (IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT) OR WINDSCREEN WASHER FLUID LEVEL SENSOR - CHECK AND TOP UP IF NECESSARY

2. Reservoir damaged.

2. Replace the reservoir.

Op. 5050A10 WINDSCREEN WASHER FLUID RESERVOIR (IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT) - R+R

3. The windscreen wipers are not working properly.

3. Proceed as instructed in the diagnosis

See table J002 WINDSCREEN WIPERS NOT WORKING

1

PIPE AND JET CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that there are no obstructions or incorrect connections for the windscreen washer pipes.

Op. 5050B14 PIPES AND CONNECTORS FOR FRONT WINDSCREEN WASHER. - R.R.

Check the condition of the jet.

Move on to Step 2

1. Pipes blocked or damaged

1. Repair or replace the pipes.

2. Jet obstructed or damaged.

2. Repair or replace the jet

Op. 5050B10 FRONT WINDSCREEN WASHER JETS - R+R CLEAN AND/OR POSITION

2

WINDSCREEN WASHER PUMP SUPPLY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the electrical connection between the steering column switch unit and the screen washer pump is correct

See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE

Move on to Step 3

Electrical connection interrupted.

Restore the correct connections.

3

SCREEN WASHER PUMP CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the screen washer pump is working.

End of diagnosis

Pump faulty.

Replace the screen washer pump.

Op. 5050B20 WINDSCREEN WASHER ELECTRICAL PUMP - R+R
