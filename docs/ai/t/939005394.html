WIRING CONTINUITY CHECK

Check the continuity of the cable for the speed signal between the body computer and the 14-way connector on the master headlamp (left side)

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

Move on to Step 2

1. Connector not correctly fitted

1. Restore the connection

2. Wiring between body computer and master headlamp faulty

2. Restore or, if necessary, replace the wiring
