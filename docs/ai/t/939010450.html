Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the piston rings.

2. Release the pin (2a) and separate the connecting rod (2b) from the piston (2c).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Join the connecting rod and piston.

- Place the gudgeon pin in its housings and secure it using the circlips.

Refitting

Removing
