The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0403 (EGR valve), with detail "excessive temperature"

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected if the temperature of the electronic control module detected is not within the planned limits for at least 0.24 secs.
