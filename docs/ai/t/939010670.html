Refitting

Removing

- Place the air conditioning condenser back in its housing on the engine cooling radiator and secure it using the nuts.

- Fit the fan in place on the engine cooling radiator and secure it using the bolts.

- Place the engine cooling radiator complete with fan and air conditioning condenser in its housing.

- Fasten the power assisted steering system heat exchanger pipe using the bands to the engine cooling radiator.

Op. 7210A15 R.R. FRONT WING/HEADLAMP HOUSING CROSSMEMBER

1. Connect the air conditioning system pipes (1a) to the air conditioning condenser and secure them tightening the nuts (1b) to the recommended torque.

Replace any O-Rings for the connectors using green coloured seals only which are resistant to the R134a refrigerant fluid.Lubricate the connector threads using anti-freeze.

Refitting

Removing

- Assemble the heat exchanger and the engine cooling radiator and secure it using the nuts.

- Assemble the air conditioning condenser and the heat exchanger and secure it using the nuts.

- Fit the air duct in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

- Place the engine cooling radiator in its housing, complete with heat exchanger and air conditioning condenser, using the hydraulic jack.

- Place the engine cooling radiator mounting brackets in their housings and secure them using the bolts.

- Remove the hydraulic jack from underneath the engine coolant radiator.

- Fasten the power assisted steering system heat exchanger pipe to the engine cooling radiator, using the bands.

1. Connect air conditioning system pipes (1a) to the air conditioning condenser and secure them, tightening nuts (1b) to the recommended torque.

Replace any O-Rings for the connectors, using green coloured seals only, which are resistant to the R134a refrigerant fluid.Lubricate the connector threads using anti-freeze.
