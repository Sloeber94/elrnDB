Removing

Refitting

- Open the boot lid.

1. Working in the luggage compartment, disconnect the electrical connection.

Description

Component

RADIO HIFI AMPLIFIER

2. Undo the bolts.

3 Keep the amplifier.

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the amplifier is not damaged.

- Position the amplifier.

- Tighten the bolts.

- Connect the electrical connection.

- Close the boot lid.
