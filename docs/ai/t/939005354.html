Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS; all production periods

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Remove the pump from the fuel tank and draw the fuel from the bottom of the tank. Fill a 100 ml glass cylinder with 1 ml graduations with this fuel to the 90 ml level. Add 10 ml of water to reach the 100 ml level and close the cover. Shake the cylinder vigorously for 10-15 seconds, then remove the cover paying attention: some of the alcohol vapours naturally present in the fuel will escape. Close the cover and shake the cylinder again for 10-15 seconds. Leave the cylinder to rest on a horizontal surface for about 5 minutes to allow the liquids to separate

2

Check that there is not a liquid layer in the lower part of the cylinder (colloid suspension of water and alcohol) that exceeds a level of 10 ml

Remove the fuel from the tank and clean the actual tank

3

Draw off 0.5 litres of fuel from the bottom of the tank. Fill a glass container with this fuel. Leave the container to rest on a horizontal surface for about 5 minutes to allow the sedimentation of the particles in suspension. Check that there are no white or light brown crystals (sand) or irregular black particles (rubber)

Remove the fuel from the tank and clean the actual tank
