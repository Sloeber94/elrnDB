With the vehicle moving, there is noise coming from the sun roof, with the roof closed

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the noise disappears when the roof is opened.

Check that the wind noise is actually coming from the sun roof.

Move on to Step 1

The noise persists with the roof open.

Proceed as instructed in the

See table S075 SUN ROOF NOISY / ROOF OPEN

See table T001 WIND NOISE FROM THE FRONT DOORS

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T002 WIND NOISE FROM THE REAR DOORS

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T005 BONNET WIND NOISE

The wind noise is coming from the area of the windscreen.

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T006 WINDSCREEN WIND NOISE

The wind noise is coming from the lower part of the passenger compartment.

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T010 PASSENGER COMPARTMENT WIND NOISE, LOWER AREA (UNDERBODY)

Proceed as instructed in the

See table T025 WIND NOISE FROM THE REAR AREA

1

SUN ROOF ALIGNMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the sun roof is fitted correctly. In particular, make sure that the roof - housing alignment is correct

Move on to Step 2

Problems with the sun roof alignment

Adjust the sun roof

Op. 7030B54 SUN ROOF (ASSEMBLY) - ALIGNMENT AND POSITIONING

2

SUN ROOF ADJUSTMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the sun roof end of travel adjustment is correct

Move on to Step 3

Electric end of travel stops not aligned with mechanical stops

Adjust the sun roof end of travel stops

Op. 7030B54 SUN ROOF (ASSEMBLY) - ALIGNMENT AND POSITIONING

3

SUN ROOF SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the sun roof seal

End of diagnosis

Seal fitting faulty

Refit the seal correctly
