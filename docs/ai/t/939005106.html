Applies to: engines: 3.2 JTS; all production periods

The intake manifold is made from aluminium, in a single component, tightened to the cylinder heads by six bolts; it distributes the intake air that flows through the 72 mm electronic throttle body to the cylinders.

The connection for the brake servo pipe, those for the oil vapour recovery from the left head and for the petrol vapour recovery and the intake air pressure sensor are located on the intake chamber.
