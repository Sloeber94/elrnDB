Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods; only with equipment: C.A.E.

DESCRIPTION

The vehicle is equipped with a white reversing light located in the right rear light cluster on the moving part of the boot lid.

It is activated each time reverse gear is engaged: this information arrives from the automatic transmission control unit. The circuit for the reversing lights receives a power supply with the TEG inserted (INT) and is protected by a dedicated fuse in the junction unit under the dashboard.

The signal that reverse gear has been engaged is managed by the Body Computer which forwards this information to the steering column switch unit for the automatic operation of the rearscreen wiper and, via the CAN, to the other nodes, for example to the parking sensors control unit.
