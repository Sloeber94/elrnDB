Removing

Refitting

Op. 1016A10 SOUND-PROOFING COVER - R.R

1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the fuel supply pipe from the filter.

2. Disconnect the fuel supply pipe rapid connector from the pressure pump.

3. Open the retaining clips and remove the fuel supply pipe between the filter and the pressure pump.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the fuel supply pipe between the filter and the pressure pump in position and secure it using the retaining clips.

- Connect the rapid connectors for the fuel supply pipe to the pressure pump and to the filter.
