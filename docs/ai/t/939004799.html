Backrest not attached/difficult to attach

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

SIDE STRIKER POSITION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the side striker is correctly positioned on the bodyshell.

Move on to Step 2

Striker interfering with the lock (or cover) on the backrest.

Reposition the striker and adjust.

2

CHECK ON MATCHING BETWEEN BACKREST AND VEHICLE TRIM

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the matching of the backrest with the vehicle trims (rear parcel shelf, side covers, etc.).

Move on to Step 3

Interference of trim with backrest.

Reposition the trims and/or carry out the correct left backrest tightening/fitting cycle

Op. 7050A13 SPLIT REAR SEAT LEFT BACKREST - R.R.

or right backrest

Op. 7050A15 SPLIT REAR SEAT RIGHT BACKREST - R.R.

3

BACKREST LOCK OPERATION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the backrest lock is working properly

End of diagnosis

Backrest lock not working properly.

Replace the backrest lock.
