Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Working in the centre of the dashboard, place the air vents in the closed air flow position.

2. Undo the bolts.

3. Move the vent assembly (3a) slightly releasing the flexible retainers (3b).

4. Disconnect the electrical connection.

Description

Component

CENTRAL FROMT SPEAKER

See P032 CENTRE FRONT SPEAKER

5. Undo the bolts.

6. Remove the speaker.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the front speaker is not damaged.

- Remove the speaker.

- Tighten the bolts.

- Connect the electrical connection.

- Position the vent assembly and attach the retainers.

- Tighten the bolts.

- Check that the speaker is working.
