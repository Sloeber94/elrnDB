Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Press the START/STOP button and turn off the engine.

Clean the diesel filter and replace if necessary

Op. 1044B10 FUEL FILTER ASSEMBLY - R + R OR REPLACE

Press the START/STOP button. Select the following system on the diagnostic instrument: Electronic diesel injection system EDC16C39. Check that error P2264 – Water in diesel filter is “Present”

Water present in diesel filter

Check there is no water in the fuel tank.

Clean the tank if necessary

2

Press the START/STOP button.

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from the temperature sensor and fuel heater coil and from engine control unit connector A.

Press the START/STOP button.

Check that the voltage across pin 5 of the control unit

See K100

and the earth is greater than 0.3 V.

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the water presence sensor and the engine control unit

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

3

Press the START/STOP button. Check that the resistance across Pin 5 of the sensor

See K100

and the earth is more than 500 kOhm.

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the water presence sensor and the engine control unit

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

4

Check that the resistance across pin 5 of the sensor

See K100

and pin 74 of the control unit

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

is less than 5 Ohms

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the water presence sensor and the engine control unit

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

5

Connect a shunt cable with fuse between pin 72 of control unit connector A

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT ECU

and the earth.

Check that the voltage across pin 3 of the control unit

See K100

and the earth is greater than 11 V.

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the water presence sensor and the engine compartment junction unit

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

6

Check that the resistance across pin 4 of the sensor

See K100

and the earth is greater than 5 Ohms.

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the water presence sensor and earth

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

7

Replace the water presence sensor

Op. 1060G30 WATER PRESENCE SENSOR IN FUEL FILTER - R.R.

Repair the original sensor and replace the engine control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R
