The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P2148 (Group 1 injector control).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected if the voltage mesured by the control unit at the energizing outlet module for hte injectors does not correspond to the nominal value. In the case of detail "general short circuit" or "short circuit to earth", the engine is switched off and the warning light will shortly be ON.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
