Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and disconnect the intermediate exhaust pipe (1b), exhaust manifold and catalytic converter side.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts fixing the lower reaction rod for the power unit, engine crankcase sump side.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts fixing the power unit rear support flexible mounting, rigid support side.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the rigid power unit rear support (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the power unit rigid rear support (1a) in its housing and secure it tightening the bolts (1b) to the recommended torque.
