Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Both the headlamps come on, the initial test is carried out and the remain immobile, facing downwards

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK ON ALIGNMENT SENSOR WIRING

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the wiring for the alignment sensors.

Check that the alignment sensors are correctly fitted and, in particular, that the fastening of the bracket with the connecting rod is correct

Move on to Step 2

1. Wiring short circuited with +Vbatt or to earth

1. Restore insulation for wiring

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

2. Wiring broken

2. Restore or, if necessary, replace the wiring

3. Mechanical installation of front sensor (brackets, connecting rods) incorrect

3. Refit front sensor correctly

Op. 5540C42 SENSOR FOR CORRECTING/ALIGNING GAS DISCHARGE LIGHTING UNITS ON FRONT SUSPENSION - R R

4. Mechanical installation of rear sensor (brackets, connecting rods) incorrect

4. Refit rear sensor correctly

Op. 5540C46 SENSOR FOR CORRECTING/ALIGNING GAS DISCHARGE LIGHTING UNITS ON REAR SUSPENSION - R.R.

2

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that there are no errors by connecting to the master headlamp (left) control unit

End of diagnosis

1. Presence of error detected

B1020 - Front sensor signal

1. Move on to Step 3

2. Presence of error detected

B1021 - Rear sensor signal

2. Move on to Step 3

3. Presence of error detected

B1022 - Sensor power supply

3. Move on to Step 3

4. Presence of error detected

B1030 - Master/Slave communication

4. Move on to Step 4

5. Presence of error detected

B1040 - Headlamp alignment corrector

5. Move on to Step 5

3

CHECK ON ALIGNMENT SENSORS SUPPLY

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the alignment sensors are receiving a correct supply by carrying out the test

See Test 5540CA HEADLAMP ALIGNMENT SENSOR CHECK

Move on to Step 4

4

BIDIRECTIONAL SERIAL LINE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the continuity and the insulation of the two-way serial line between the master and slave headlamps, carrying out the test

See Test 5540BL BIDIRECTIONAL SERIAL LINE CHECK

Move on to Step 5

5

CHECK ON CONNECTIONS INSIDE THE HEADLAMPS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the connections inside the headlamps for the vertical adjustment motors are correctly connected by carrying out the test

See Test 5540BM CHECK THE HEADLAMP ALIGNMENT MOTOR SUPPLY WIRING

Move on to Step 6

6

CHECK ON OPERATION OF HEADLAMP ALIGNMENT ACTUATORS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Replace the headlamps and check in active diagnosis that the diagnostic equipment activates the correct operation of the actuators

End of diagnosis

1. Right headlamp alignment corrector not working

1. Move on to Step 7

2. Left headlamp alignment corrector not working

2. Move on to Step 7

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Refit the headlamps replaced previously and replace the ballast control units.

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Contact the Service Department for further information on the problem
