Before removing and refitting the sensor the following preliminary operations must be carried out.- Extract the electronic key.- Disconnect the battery.

- Open the bonnet.

1. Undo the sensor retaining bolt

2. Move the sensor (2a) and disconnect the electrical connection (2b).
