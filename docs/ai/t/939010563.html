Refitting

If the air flow meter is replaced, reset using an examiner after refitting.

- Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the battery terminal (-).

Op. 1016A10 SOUND-PROOFING COVER - R.R

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the air flow meter.

Description

Connector

1

Air flow meter

See K041 AIR FLOW METER

2. Loosen collars (2a) and disconnect air sleeve (2b) from the air flow meter and resonator.

3. Loosen the band (3a) and remove the air flow meter (3b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the air flow meter back in its housing and secure it using the band.

- Connect the air sleeve to the air flow meter and resonator and tighten the bands.

- Connect the electrical connection to the air flow meter.

Op. 1016A10 SOUND-PROOFING COVER - R.R

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

If the air flow meter is being replaced, when the refitting is complete the following operations must be carried out using the Examiner.

- Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the battery terminal (-).
