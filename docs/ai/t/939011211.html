Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Working under the dashboard in the area of the driver's side panel, unscrew the bolts securing the bonnet lid opening lever.

2. Move the lever slightly

3. Disconnect the control cable (3a) from lever retainers (3b).

4. Keep the lever.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Connect the control cable to the lever retainers.

- Position the lever and tighten the bolts.

- Check the operation of the bonnet lid opening mechanism.
