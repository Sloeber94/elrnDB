5540BL BIDIRECTIONAL SERIAL LINE CHECK

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Disconnect the wiring of the master headlamp 14-way connector.

Disconnect the wiring of the slave headlamp 14-way connector.

Connect the diagnostic tester in ohmmeter mode and check the continuity between Pin 12 of the headlamp vehicle side wiring 14-way connector

See F010 LEFT HEADLAMP

and Pin 12 of the headlamp vehicle wiring 14-way connector

See F011 RIGHT HEADLAMP

Restore wiring continuity

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

2

With the headlamp 14-way connectors disconnected, check the insulation to earth by connecting across Pin 12 Pin 12 of each of the connectors and Earth

Restore insulation

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

3

Check the continuity across Pin 12 of the headlamp 14 way connector

See F010 LEFT HEADLAMP

and Pin 14 of the 21-way connector for connection to the Ballast control unit

Replace the Master headlamp

Op. 5540B81 LEFT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

4

Check the continuity across Pin 12 of the headlamp 14 way connector

See F011 RIGHT HEADLAMP

and Pin 14 of the 21-way connector for connection to the Ballast control unit

Replace the slave headlamp

Op. 5540B82 RIGHT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.
