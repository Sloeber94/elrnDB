1. Unscrew the lower nut securing the ABS hydraulic and electronic control unit mounting bracket to the bodyshell.

2. Raise the retaining clip (2a) and disconnect the clutch hydraulic circuit vibration damper (2b), clutch master cylinder side.

Collect any brake-clutch fluid that comes out.

3. Release the rigid pipe from the vibration damper to the operating cylinder from the retaining clip.
