The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0401 (EGR valve).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

This error is detected if the actual value of the exhaust gas recirculation flow is less than the nominal value of the exhaust gas recirculation flow.As a result, the exhaust gas recirculation valve is switched off and the supercharging pressure can longer be controlled and the quantity of fuel injected is reduced.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
