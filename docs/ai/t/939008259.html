PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Having carried out the road test, during the

See table S015 NOISE WHILST DRIVING

there is transverse vibration in the front section. Continue depending on when the vibration is present.

End of diagnosis

The vibration is noticeable during take off and release.

Move on to Step 5

The vibration is accompanied by noise which increases with speed.

Move on to Step 2

The vibration is accompanied by noise over unmade roads.

Move on to Step 1

1

WHEEL SIDE CONSTANT VELOCITY JOINT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Raise the vehicle on a two column lift and check:

- that the driveshaft-wheel side joint matching is correct;

- that the seal is present.

Move on to Step 2

Incorrect matching.

Replace the constant velocity joint and the driveshaft

Op. 2710A10 DRIVESHAFT WITH JOINTS (ONE), L/H OR R/H - R + R

Sealing ring missing.

Replace the constant velocity joint and the driveshaft

Op. 2710A10 DRIVESHAFT WITH JOINTS (ONE), L/H OR R/H - R + R

2

INTERMEDIATE SHAFT BEARING CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the intermediate shaft bearing.

Move on to Step 3

Bearing damaged

Replace the bearing

Op. 2710A35 INTERMEDIATE DRIVESHAFT BEARING SUPPORT - R.R

3

WHEEL SIDE JOINT CLEARANCE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the clearance for the wheel side constant velocity joint is not too great.

Move on to Step 4

Excessive clearance compared with the opposite wheel side joint.

Replace the wheel side constant velocity joint

Op. 2710A20 WHEEL SIDE CONSTANT VELOCITY JOINT (ONE), L/H OR R/H - R + R WITH DRIVESHAFT REMOVED - INCLUDES BOOT REPLACEMENT

and carry out the correct greasing.

4

WHEEL SIDE JOINT SEIZING CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Rotate the wheel by hand and check that there is no seizing of the wheel side constant velocity joint.

Move on to Step 5

There is seizing of the wheel side joint.

Replace the wheel side constant velocity joint

Op. 2710A20 WHEEL SIDE CONSTANT VELOCITY JOINT (ONE), L/H OR R/H - R + R WITH DRIVESHAFT REMOVED - INCLUDES BOOT REPLACEMENT

and carry out the correct greasing.

5

WHEEL SIDE JOINT TIGHTENING CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Remove the left wheel

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL - R.R

and/or the right one

Op. 4450B04 RIGHT FRONT WHEEL - R.R

Check that the constant velocity joint fastening nut is tightened and that the staking is in place.

Move on to Step 6

Nut loosening

Replace the nut, restore the torque tightening and stake

Op. 4450C28 FRONT WHEEL HUB,LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R WITH WHEEL REMOVED, CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY

6

CLUTCH PLATE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that there is no irregular wear on the surface of the clutch plate.

Move on to Step 7

Surface of the clutch plate worn unevenly.

Replace the clutch plate

Op. 1810A20 CLUTCH - R + R

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present.

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

The fault is due to another system (engine, gearbox, differential, suspension).

Search for the cause in one of these systems, following the appropriate diagnostic procedures.
