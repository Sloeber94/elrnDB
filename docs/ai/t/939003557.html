When the alarm is switched on using the remote control, the system is activated correctly but the volumetric sensors do not intervene.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

The volumetric sensors are located in the courtesy light module and are connected to the Body Computer Node via the dialogue line known as the A-BUS. The volumetric sensors are operated and controlled by the Body Computer node by means of a series of coded messages and can be excluded by the customer using the special button. If there is a failure the Body Computer Node detects and memorizes the causes of the malfunction

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
