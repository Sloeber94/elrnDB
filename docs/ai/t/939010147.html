Applies to: all engines; all production periods

After rain or washing, water infiltration into the front part of the passenger compartment is noticed.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK ABSENCE OF WINDSCREEN INFILTRATIONS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for sealant on the windscreen

See Test 7035OA Check fixed window glass penetration (windscreen and rearscreen)

Move on to Step 2

Sealant missing.

Restore the sealant

Op. 7035A04 WINDSCREEN - ELIMINATE LEAKS

2

WINDSCREEN CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the windscreen.

In particular, visually check:

  • glass condition;
  • flatness of contact surface;
  • flatness of screen-printing.

Move on to Step 3

Faults in windscreen.

Replace the windscreen

Op. 7035A18 WINDSCREEN GLASS - R.R INCLUDING CLEANING HOUSING

3

CHECK THE ENGINE COMPARTMENT BULKHEAD DRAINS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the engine compartment bulkhead drainages.

In particular, check: for dirt, leaves etc...

Move on to Step 4

Dirt in drains.

Clean the drains. Restore the correct installation if necessary.

4

CHECK THE ENGINE COMPARTMENT BULKHEAD

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the engine compartment bulkhead.

In particular, check:

  • the plugs are present;
  • the plugs are correctly fitted;
  • the rubber cable passage fittings are correctly fitted;
  • sealant properly applied;
  • correct installation.

End of diagnosis

Faults in engine compartment bulkhead.

Restore the engine compartment bulkhead installation and fastenings.
