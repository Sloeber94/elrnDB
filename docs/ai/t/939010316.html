Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTS, 2.2 JTS, 3.2 JTS, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 JTD 16V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check the front ultrasound sensor power supply

  • Key in on position
  • Engage reverse gear
  • Press the parking sensor disablement button located on the front courtesy light and ensure the led on the button is on (sensors enabled)
  • Connect the voltmeter with the positive probe at pin 1 and the negative earth probe for the sensor connector and check that there is a positive voltage of 12 V
  • Renew the wiring between the parking sensor control unit and the sensor involved
  • Replace the sensor
  • Replace the parking sensor control unit

Op. 5580H10 PARKING OBSTACLE DETECTION DEVICE ELECTRONIC CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

2

Rear ultrasound sensor power supply check

  • Key in on position
  • Engage reverse gear
  • Connect the voltmeter with the positive probe at pin 1 and the negative earth probe for the sensor connector and check that there is a positive voltage of 12 V
  • Renew the wiring between the parking sensor control unit and the sensor involved
  • Replace the sensor

Op. 5580H36 PARKING SENSOR SYSTEM REAR SENSORS - R.R. WITH REAR BUMPER REMOVED

  • Replace the parking sensor control unit

Op. 5580H10 PARKING OBSTACLE DETECTION DEVICE ELECTRONIC CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

3

Check the earth

  • Connect the voltmeter with the red probe at pin 3 and the positive probe earthed and check that the voltage is 0 Volt
  • Renew the broken wiring between the NSP and the sensor involved
  • Replace the sensor
  • Replace the parking sensor control unit

Op. 5580H10 PARKING OBSTACLE DETECTION DEVICE ELECTRONIC CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

4

Ultrasound signal check

Place the key in the ON position

  • Connect the voltmeter with the positive probe to pin 2 and the negative probe earthed
  • Press the START button on the Examiner. The figures in the image that appears must be evaluated with a tolerance of about 20%. They should in any case be similar to the graph in the figure.
  • Replace any faulty rear sensor(s)

Op. 5580H36 PARKING SENSOR SYSTEM REAR SENSORS - R.R. WITH REAR BUMPER REMOVED

  • Replace any faulty front sensor(s)
  • Replace the parking sensor control unit

Op. 5580H10 PARKING OBSTACLE DETECTION DEVICE ELECTRONIC CONTROL UNIT - R.R.
