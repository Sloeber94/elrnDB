S065 SEATS CREAKING

Noise / creaking

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Electric seats

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Adjust the backrest by pressing with your hands to identify the sources of the noise coming from the backrest; dismantle the backrest

Op. 7045D30 FRONT SEAT BACKREST COVER, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Move on to Step 1

Components interfering.

Move the interfering components apart and, if the welding is insufficient, renew it.

1

CHECK ON ADJUSTMENT PLATE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the adjustment plate is not in contact with the backrest frame components.

Move on to Step 2

Components interfering.

Replace the adjustment plate.

2

CHECK ON MATCHING OF UPPER AND LOWER CROSSMEMBERS WITH BACKREST SIDES

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the areas where the upper and lower crossmembers are matched with the backrest sides.

Move on to Step 3

Breaks in the matching areas.

Replace the seat frame

Op. 7045A86 FRONT SEAT FRAME (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT, R.R.

3

ADJUSTMENT MOTOR CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for noise at the front backrest adjustment motor.

Move on to Step 4

1. Noise produced during backrest adjustment.

1. Replacement of backrest motor

Op. 7045J34 FRONT SEAT BACKREST ANGLE MOTOR, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL WIRING CHECK

2. Static noise (contacts with frame).

2. Move the parts in contact with the motor apart

4

CHECK ON WHIPLASH MECHANISM ON BACKREST

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for creakign caused by the whiplash mechanism on the backrest.

Move on to Step 5

1. Joint springs creaking

1. Lubricate the joint springs with grease.

2. Contacts with backrest internal components (frame, etc.).

2. Space out the parts in contact.

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present.

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace the seat frame

Op. 7045A86 FRONT SEAT FRAME (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT, R.R.
