Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The diasbling warning light is on and the passenger Air Bag is not excluded by the control switch

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the EXAMINER diagnostic equipment and check the following at the AIR BAG NODE:

  • No system/component errors
  • Plausibility of disabling command

Move on to Step 2

System/components faulty

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the continuity and insulation of the wiring between the control switch in the dashboard on the passenger side and the Air Bag control unit

See E7030 AIR BAG - DESCRIPTION

Move on to Step 3

Switch wiring short circuited or open circuit

Replacing wiring.

3

CONTROL SWITCH CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the switch in the dashboard on the passenger side is working properly by carrying out the Test

See Test 5580CG Check the passenger bag disablement selector

Move on to Step 4

Switch not working

Replace the passenger air bag disabling switch

Op. 5580C26 PASSENGER AIR BAG DISABLING KEY LOCK BARREL/SWITCH - R.R.

4

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the Examiner in Instrument Panel Node mode, check that the passenger air bag warning light is correctly activated in Active Diagnosis.

Move on to Step 5

1. The Air Bag warning light does not operate correctly

1. Replace the Instrument Panel

Op. 5560B10 CONTROL PANEL - R+R

2. The warning light operates correctly

2. Replace the Air Bag control unit

Op. 5580C14 AIR BAG ECU - R+R

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace Body Computer

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R.R
