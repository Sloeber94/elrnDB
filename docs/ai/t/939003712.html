1

ELECTRICAL CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the speaker connections are correctly connected.

If the customer complains of no or poor audio quality, check:

  • That the microphone for the hands-free function is working properly
  • Check that there are no microphone vibrations
  • Check the operation of the GSM aerial

Move on to Step 2

1.Speaker connections broken

1.Restore continuity

2.Microphone not correctly installed

2.Install the microphone correctly

3.GSM aerial defective.

3.Replace the GSM aerial

Op. 5580M70

CONTROLS ON THE STEERING WHEEL CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the controls on the steering wheel for adjusting the volume which can increase/decrease the audio volume are working properly see wiring diagram

See E3510 RADIO

If necessary, disconnect the controls on the steering wheel from the Connect and check that the fault disappears

Move on to Step 3

Audio volume controls on the sterring wheel defective

Replace the controls on the steering wheel

Op. 4110A18 TRIM ON STEERING WHEEL - R.R.
