Applies to: all engines; all production periods

It is located in the engine compartment, at the side of the right shock absorber upper mounting. It contains the following information.

A. Name of manufacturer.

B. Homologation number.

C. Vehicle type identification code.

D. Chassis manufacture serial number

E. Maximum authorized weight of vehicle when fully laden.

F. Maximum authorized weight of vehicle when fully laden plus trailer.

G. Maximum authorized weight on first axle (front).

H. Maximum authorized weight on second axle (rear).

I. Engine type.

L. Bodywork version code.

M. Spares number.

N. Correct value of fume absorption coefficient (Diesel engines only).
