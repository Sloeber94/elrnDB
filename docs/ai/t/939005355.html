CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check the fuel level signalled by the gauge in the panel and make a note of it.

Connect to the pump connector

See N040 FUEL PUMP AND LEVEL GAUGE

between Pins A and C and check that the resistance reading is consistent with the reading in the panel, following the instructions in the table below.

If the test is carried out to simply check the level gauge, without any further defects reported by the customer, if the reading is consistent, the test is over

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

2

Lift up the pump from the tank, checking that the electrical connection and the pipes that go to the second level sensor are correctly connected

Restore the correct electrical and hydraulic connections

3

Operate the pump and ensure that the fuel level in the tank is less than 1/4 full

Second level sensor and pump system faulty: replace the fuel tank

Op. 1040A14 FUEL TANK - R + R FOR REPLACEMENT

4

Extract the pump from the tank, taking care to disconnect the electrical connection and the pipes that go to the second level sensor.

Place the pump on a horizontal surface and short circuit the connection that goes to the disconnected sensor.

Check the consistency between the resistance measured at the pump and the height at which the base of the level float finds itself, referring to the table below

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

5

Second level sensor and pump system faulty: replace the fuel tank

Op. 1040A14 FUEL TANK - R + R FOR REPLACEMENT

The test can be considered over

6

The level gauge may be fitted incorrectly.

Refit the level gauge

Op. 1040A70 SUBMERGED PUMP ASSEMBLY COMPLETE WITH LEVEL GAUGE CONTROL - R + R

and move on to Step 4.

If the fault persists, replace the gauge

7

Check that the reading is now consistent.

The test can be considered over

Replace the fuel pump assembly

Op. 1040A70 SUBMERGED PUMP ASSEMBLY COMPLETE WITH LEVEL GAUGE CONTROL - R + R

8

Carry out the active diagnosis of the fuel level gauge in the instrument panel, checking that the pointer can complete the entire stroke throughout the scale

Replace the instrument panel

Op. 5560B10 CONTROL PANEL - R+R

9

Check that the wiring between Pin 1 of the gauge

See N040 FUEL PUMP AND LEVEL GAUGE

and Pin 4 of connector D of the Body Computer

See M001 BODY COMPUTER

does not have continuity or insulation faults

Restore or, if necessary, replace the wiring

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

10

Check that the wiring between Pin A of the gauge

See N040 FUEL PUMP AND LEVEL GAUGE

and Pin 15 of connector D of the Body Computer

See M001 BODY COMPUTER

does not have continuity or insulation faults

Restore or, if necessary, replace the wiring

See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

11

Check that the connector for the guage

See N040 FUEL PUMP AND LEVEL GAUGE

does not show signs of oxidation, deformation or breaks in the pins
